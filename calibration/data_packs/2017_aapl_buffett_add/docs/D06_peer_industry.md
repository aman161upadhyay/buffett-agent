The software vendor's reported revenue includes accounting deferrals, while the advertising vendor's fiscal 2016 annual results were not yet public. These are net margins, not interchangeable operating margins. Apple's just-reported quarter had 78.290m phone units versus 74.779m a year earlier, up 4.7% (January 31 supplement), but its extra reporting week and product mix prevent treating unit growth as a recurring installed-base monetization rate.

The relevant comparisons include consumer franchises with loyal customers and mobile-device platforms with different monetization models. Measure recurring services without double-counting the device economics that enable them. Compare buyback price with conservative business value, since reducing shares above intrinsic value destroys value. A platform's breadth is meaningful only insofar as it supports retention, pricing and durable cash generation. [Source above.]

Technical context: a consumer ecosystem may monetize both a device and recurring services, but they are linked rather than independent annuities. Customer retention, replacement frequency, pricing and the required cost of innovation determine owner economics. Separate hardware sales, installed-base activity and services revenue, avoiding double-counting value from the same customer relationship. Cash returned through buybacks must be compared with share issuance and purchase price; authorization is not execution. Foreign-held cash, taxes and operating liquidity needs affect deployable excess capital. Use one split basis for price, shares and EPS, since mismatched adjustments can make an otherwise correct earnings multiple meaningless. [Source above; analytical framework.]

Comparison discipline: compare businesses on the source of their cash and the capital required to earn it, rather than treating the nearest ticker as an exact substitute. Normalize financing and accounting differences before interpreting relative multiples. A peer can have a better balance sheet but a worse franchise, or a better franchise at an unattractive price. Relative cheapness alone cannot prove absolute value. Consider the opportunity cost of adding to an existing understandable holding, retaining liquidity, owning an entire business or buying the investor's own shares. Where a numerical peer table was not recovered, keep the comparison qualitative rather than inventing an industry average.

Evidence boundary: missing figures are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered for this digest. Analytical questions are not additional reported facts or claims about the investor's private thinking. Read the financial, risk and fund-context documents together; no individual accounting ratio supplies a complete investment conclusion.
