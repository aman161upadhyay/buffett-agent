Title: Compare full investment requirements, not only growth rates
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000012/googexhibit991q42025.htm ; https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/department-justice-wins-significant-remedies-against-google ; https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/LSB/PDF/LSB11362/LSB11362.1.pdf
Date: 2026-03-31

Microsoft and Amazon compete with Cloud, Meta competes for advertising, and AI model providers challenge aspects of discovery. Their mixes, accounting and capital commitments differ, so Cloud revenue growth alone is not a valuation comparison. Alphabet's disclosed Cloud profit is before some shared AI research costs; its consolidated return must include those costs and the loss-making activities funded by the profitable core. [1]

No synchronized peer consensus or capital-budget/enterprise-value matrix is recovered. A quantitative claim that one competitor is cheaper or more efficient would therefore exceed the evidence. The useful comparison is between sources of recurring demand, the cost of acquiring distribution, infrastructure utilization and the durability of customer relationships. The 2025 operating record demonstrates that current competitors have not prevented strong growth, but the current spending outlook demonstrates that defending and expanding the franchise is not costless. A long-horizon investor should evaluate full cash requirements before treating reported earnings growth as distributable owner earnings. [1-3]

Evidence ledger: February 4 results show 2025 revenue of $402.836 billion versus $350.018 billion in 2024; operating income $129.039 billion versus $112.390 billion; net income $132.170 billion; diluted EPS $10.81. Fourth-quarter revenue was $113.828 billion, operating income $35.934 billion and EPS $2.82. Services revenue was $95.862 billion and Cloud $17.664 billion, up 48%; segment operating income was $40.132 billion and $5.313 billion respectively. Other Bets lost $3.617 billion, including a Waymo compensation charge, and central activities cost $5.894 billion. Cash/marketable securities totaled $126.843 billion. Management expects 2026 capital expenditures of $175-$185 billion. These plans are forward-looking; no second-quarter capital commitment by an outside investor is included. [1-3]

Comparison discipline: industry membership does not make two businesses economically identical. Geography, customer mix, contract duration, capital intensity, leverage and accounting adjustments can dominate headline growth comparisons. No synchronized peer-price and consensus-earnings dataset was recovered for this cutoff; relative cheapness is therefore not asserted numerically. The useful comparison is between sources of durable return and exposure to common pressures. A competitor's weakness is not automatically this company's moat, and an industry recovery is not required to be uniform across participants.
