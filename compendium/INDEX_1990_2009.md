# Buffett trade compendium — 1990–2009 decision cohorts

## Reading this index

This is an **evidence-weighted historical inventory of 80 dossiers**, not an audited transaction ledger or a claim to recover every private investment. It follows the sibling Ackman compendium's eight-section structure, dated primary-source spine, separation of reasoning from ownership evidence, and explicit gaps. Source URLs/dates for each row are in its linked dossier.

- **Era labels:** B = 1980–1999; C = 2000–2015. Files carry the relevant `era_B` / `era_C` pattern tag. An agreement made in 1999 and completed in 2000 is not silently relabeled a different decision.
- **Filename versus first entry:** years identify the requested decision/disclosure cohort when exact first purchase is unknown. Wells began in **1989**; POSCO's first purchase year remains uncertain; BNSF's 2006 label does not establish a first-trade day. Entry-date fields stay `unknown` unless an actual supported month/day was recovered.
- **Agreement versus closing:** GEICO agreed in 1995, closed January 2, 1996; Dairy Queen agreed 1997, closed early 1998; MidAmerican agreed 1999, closed 2000; Shaw/Johns Manville agreed 2000, closed 2001; Fruit/Larson-Juhl agreed 2001, completed 2002. BYD agreed September 2008, **completed July 30, 2009**. Dow's 2008 commitment funded **April 1, 2009**. Linked dossiers preserve these distinctions.
- **Disclosure dates:** a recovered letter's signing date is a **document-date checkpoint**, not necessarily its public-release date or the first market disclosure. Do not use a signing-date field as a verified availability timestamp in a daily replay. The 2008 letter was signed February 27, 2009; public release followed. A filing/press-release date is preferred where recovered; otherwise first disclosure remains a research gap.
- **Historical status / outcomes:** `open` describes the last explicitly sourced ownership checkpoint, **not verified ownership on October 25, 2026**. A known completed sale/trim can be `closed` despite unknown exact final execution. `ongoing` with a closed episode indicates the full outcome remains unresolved, **not that the position is still held**. Quantified realized win/loss labels are used where supported; policy/abstention `flat` is not a measured investment return. These historical metadata conventions must be normalized against newer filings before current-portfolio use.
- **Sizes:** “peak_position” often contains an explicitly dated **observed size**, not a proved lifetime peak. Cost, market value, note principal, option notional, premiums and accounting liabilities are distinct. Never sum overlapping predecessor/successor holdings or a credit-book aggregate with its component financings.
- **Calibration:** the three-to-six driver slots include explicit evidence boundaries where the original thesis was not recovered. **A rationale-gap driver is not a positive Buffett investment reason.** Thin dossiers can test direction/disclosure; exclude them from reasoning-overlap answer keys until direct explanations are found.
- **Deciders:** BYD is Munger-originated with Sokol's crucial understanding/diligence and Buffett approval. Bank of America's small 2007–10 common position and Nalco were **Lou Simpson's**, not Buffett's; Republic also has later Simpson attribution. The schema lacks `Simpson`/`Sokol`, so these use `unknown` with explicit prose, rather than invent Buffett authorship.

## Verified corrections and non-double-counting rules

1. **American Express cost timestamps:** December 1994 cost $723.919M; $1.36B was the March 1995 letter-writing checkpoint; December 1995 cost **$1.3927B**. Do not collapse them into one year-end figure. [AXP dossier](trades/1994_axp.md)
2. **Acquisition spree dating:** Jordan's 1999; MiTek/XTRA 2001; Shaw/Johns Manville 2000 agreements with 2001 completions. “2000 spree” is a grouping, not a license to move closing dates.
3. **Attribution:** Nalco and small BAC were Simpson; Republic has later Simpson attribution; BYD Munger/Sokol. Small Berkshire 13F holdings do not automatically equal Buffett decisions.
4. **Results:** PetroChina's $4B is proceeds; Constellation's $1.092B is two reported components; derivatives notional is not premium or capital at risk; unrealized Irish-bank and COP losses are not complete realized-return ledgers.
5. **Disney:** 1999–2000 major sales versus possible 2001 residual exit remains explicitly provisional. The 1966 partnership entry is a separate sibling dossier.
6. **BNSF:** minority accumulation, 2009 bid and the sibling [2010 closing checkpoint](trades/2010_bnsf_closing.md) are stages of one economic investment. Do not add their reported values as three independent profits. Likewise Cap Cities/Disney, Gillette/P&G, D&B/Moody's and Salomon/Travelers need lineage-aware accounting.
7. **Policy/abstention:** the 1999 Fortune essay and 1999-letter/2000 buyback discussion are decisions, not fabricated securities transactions. “Buy American” is an actual personal-account statement without named securities or prices.

## Open gaps / research priorities

- **Thin entry theses:** Gannett, PNC, USB, Sanofi, BDX, Anheuser, initial McDonald's/Disney sale, early Moody's and specific 2005 Wal-Mart valuation. Do not infer a direct quotation from a portfolio table or generic principles.
- **Incomplete original data:** Dairy Queen, Johns Manville and several private acquisitions need original announcements/proxies and transaction-level valuation work; recovered direct later-letter explanations are not complete contemporaneous data packs.
- **Exact dates:** many first purchases, final residual sales and early public-release timestamps remain unknown. Day-level calibration must recover those before replay.
- **Final P&L/current status:** no audited subsidiary-by-subsidiary realized cash-flow ledger or complete through-2026 ownership reconciliation was recovered in this cohort task. Historical `open` does not imply still held today.
- **Sources/attribution:** original 1991 Congressional testimony, original Freddie/Fannie sale explanations, some note purchaser allocations, BD/USB/Sanofi individual deciders, direct contemporary BYD diligence materials, and primary Disney 1999–2001 filings remain priorities.
- **Public discussion:** dated press is included where retrieved. No unauthenticated X/Reddit/VIC/COBF posts are promoted into contemporary evidence. Absence of a recovered forum thread does not establish consensus.
