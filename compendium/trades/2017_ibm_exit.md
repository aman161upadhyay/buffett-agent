## Buffett's thesis in his own words

Buffett’s May 2017 explanation: he valued IBM less highly than six years earlier and had revalued it downward as competition intensified. The reporting explicitly frames a changed business assessment, not a forecast of next quarter’s share price. [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
