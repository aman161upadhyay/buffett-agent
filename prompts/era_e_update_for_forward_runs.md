## What changed after the August 2025 firewall

**Healthcare was not a permanent core commitment.** Berkshire's Q2 2025 13F, released August 14, first showed **5,039,564 UnitedHealth shares**, quarter-end mark $1.572bn. That same share count persisted through December, then disappeared from the March 2026 filing. No authenticated security-specific entry or exit explanation, execution day or individual stock picker is established. Do not describe the episode as Buffett personally declaring that all healthcare/regulatory worries were temporary, or infer a new buy because the stock once appeared. [1]

**Alphabet is not safely attributable to a deputy.** First disclosed November 14, 2025: 17.846m Class A shares, September mark $4.338bn. By June 2026 Berkshire owned 78.791m A and 27.188m C, combined **$37.764bn**. In July Buffett explicitly said “I initiated it,” while calling Abel “the decider.” The interview emphasizes high returns on capital over extended periods, acknowledges prior omission, and flags AI's enormous capital commitments. This expands the demonstrated opportunity set but does **not** abolish circle-of-competence, maintenance-capex or price discipline. Do not add an announced $10bn private placement again on top of the June holdings or invent its separate funding reconciliation. [1,4]

**Whole businesses remain central.** OxyChem was announced October 2, 2025 at $9.7bn and closed January 2, 2026 for about **$9.4bn** after adjustments, with legacy environmental liabilities retained by Occidental. This is purchase of an operating business, not more OXY common or a promise to buy its parent. Abel's February letter describes the Lubrizol/Liebert team's integration role. Taylor Morrison was announced May 31 and closed July 24: **$6.8bn equity/$8.5bn enterprise**, Palmer retained to lead the site-built platform. These are operating-capital allocations under Abel, not proof that cyclical businesses pass regardless of price. [2,5]

**The sales/cash story is more nuanced than “Buffett is selling everything.”** Apple fell from 280m shares June 2025 to 238.213m September and 227.918m December, then stayed unchanged through June 2026. BofA fell from 605.267m June 2025 to 517.296m December and 483.394m June 2026. Earlier 2024 tax comments do not establish each later sale's rationale. June net insurance/other cash plus bills was **$359.230bn**, versus March's $380.2bn news headline; consolidated net liquidity including rail/utilities cash was $364.743bn. Denominators matter. H1 equity purchases $39.405bn exceeded sales $27.780bn; substantial liquidity coexisted with net buying. Buybacks **resumed**: equity statements $235m Q1/$4.527bn Q2, distinct from cash-flow settlement amounts. No timeless “buyback halt” assumption. [1–3]

## Who decides now

Combs' resignation to join JPMorgan was announced **December 8, 2025**; he is not a current Berkshire picker. Nancy Pierce succeeded him at GEICO. Abel became CEO January 1, 2026 and explicitly accepts ultimate capital-allocation responsibility. Weschler manages about **6%** of investments, including part of Combs' inherited portfolio; neither that assignment nor 13F manager codes identify every original selector. Adam Johnson oversees 32 consumer/service/retail businesses; Jain remains insurance vice chairman. [3,6]

Buffett's November 2025 letter says ideas are “few – but not zero.” Abel's May meeting preserves patience: “It doesn’t mean you need to deploy all your capital and spend all your money.” Buffett was still able to originate Alphabet. **September 18 completed another governance transition: Howard Buffett chairman, Warren chairman emeritus/director**, Abel running the company. The AGENT.md shorthand “Buffett chairman” is stale for this forward cutoff. Preserve the distinction among originator, approver, executor and legal beneficial owner. [3–4,7]

## Forward-use implications, not predetermined decisions

Compare liquid alternatives, existing franchises, repurchases and acquisitions. Re-underwrite inherited positions instead of assigning an everlasting deputy label; actual Q1 exits included UNH, Visa, Mastercard, Amazon, Domino's and Pool. Japan is a continuing long-term capital-allocation relationship, not a fixed 10% cap: old commitments were relaxed, with possible further increases reported September; exact new five-house ceilings remain a gap [F06]. Recent Lennar additions and Bending Spoons subscription are already public [F01/F04], not undisclosed discoveries.

Forecast **future disclosure**, not a clairvoyant October trading book: Q2 is latest ordinary 13F, while specific later issuer/Form 4 reports can supersede it for individual names.
