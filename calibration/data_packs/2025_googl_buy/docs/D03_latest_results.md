Title: Double-digit search growth and stronger Cloud profitability
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204425000056/googexhibit991q22025.htm ; https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/department-justice-wins-significant-remedies-against-google ; https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/LSB/PDF/LSB11362/LSB11362.1.pdf
Date: 2025-09-30

The July release shows Search and other advertising growing from $48.509 billion to $54.190 billion. Cloud revenue increased 32%, with operating income rising from $1.172 billion to $2.826 billion. That combination is evidence of scale benefits, although central AI research costs are not allocated entirely to Cloud. YouTube advertising and subscriptions/platforms/devices also grew, while Network advertising declined slightly. [1]

Pichai described AI Overviews and AI Mode as performing well and said Cloud demand justified increasing annual capital spending to about $85 billion. The analyst should not treat product usage claims as separately audited unit economics. The current release gives neither an AI query contribution margin nor an independently measured long-run share of search advertising. The next quarter's results are not yet available. The appropriate update is that the existing business continues earning heavily while funding a new capital cycle, not that the competitive threat has disappeared. [1]

Evidence ledger: the July 23 release reports second-quarter revenue of $96.428 billion, up 14%; operating income of $31.271 billion; net income of $28.196 billion; and diluted EPS of $2.31. Services revenue was $82.543 billion, Cloud $13.624 billion and Other Bets $373 million. Services operating income was $33.063 billion, Cloud $2.826 billion, Other Bets a $1.246 billion loss and central activities a $3.372 billion cost. Cash plus marketable securities totaled $95.148 billion, versus $23.607 billion long-term debt. Trailing operating cash flow was $133.708 billion, capital expenditures $66.980 billion and free cash flow $66.728 billion. Management increased planned annual capital spending to approximately $85 billion. September court remedies prohibit specified exclusivity arrangements and require access to certain data/services. [1-3]

Interpretation: the issuer's latest released quarter is the information boundary, not the quarter containing this assessment date. Forward guidance is management's expectation, not a realized result. Growth percentages must be matched to the stated comparison period and accounting basis. Where adjusted measures differ from GAAP, both are retained rather than mixing the favorable numerator from one with the denominator from the other. A favorable quarter does not settle the durability question; the operational causes and cash conversion still require scrutiny.
