Title: Latest published operating evidence
Source: https://newsroom.cisco.com/c/r/newsroom/en/us/a/y2000/m02/cisco-systems-reports-second-quarter-earnings-and-two-for-one-stock-split.html
Date: 2000-03-01

The latest release shows revenue expanding more than 50% and management describes increasing adoption by businesses and governments. Enterprise, service-provider and smaller-business customers broaden the demand base. The Catalyst family and large-router products connect software familiarity with hardware replacement. Acquisitions fill adjacent product gaps and can make the supplier more valuable to a customer standardizing its network. A bull can reasonably regard the company as a durable infrastructure franchise rather than a venture-stage technology bet. [S1; bull interpretation]

Fiscal second-quarter sales were $4.35 billion versus $2.85 billion, up 53%. GAAP net income was $825 million, or $0.23 per share, versus $282 million, or $0.08. Pro-forma net income was $906 million, or $0.25 per share, versus $609 million, or $0.17. The pro-forma measure excludes purchased in-process R&D, acquisition costs, goodwill and purchased-intangible amortization and certain investment gains. Six-month revenue was $8.26 billion, up 52%; actual six-month net income was $1.25 billion, or $0.34 per share, while pro-forma income was $1.73 billion, or $0.48. The company announced a two-for-one split, with February 22 record date and expected March 22 distribution. Per-share figures in this release precede that distribution. Seven completed acquisitions are discussed; three were accounted for as poolings, with relevant historical results restated. Revenue growth is demonstrated, but comparability needs acquisition and accounting review. [S1]

### Underwriting distinction

A results release contains at least three kinds of evidence: completed-period accounting, operating indicators and management expectations. They should not be merged. Customer activity can be healthier than reported earnings without proving that the earnings decline is temporary; conversely, a good quarter can coexist with emerging competitive or funding risks. The bullish reading of the latest report is set out above, but the financial bridge must preserve the less favorable observations as well. [Analytical interpretation of S1]

For a decision on this date, ask which part of the changed result is volume, price, mix, investment, financing or accounting timing. A recovery case should identify the actual variable that must improve. The record does not include a fresh post-cutoff quarter or an independent forecast proving that improvement. Reconcile the operating evidence below before treating a management target as an earnings base. [Analytical interpretation of S1]
