The 10-K's cash-flow discussion separately reports FY2015 continuing operating cash of $1,702m and continuing capex of $455m, giving $1,247m of mechanical FCF. The $2m difference from the summary capex is a scope difference, not a rounding adjustment. FY2015 EPS includes valuation and impairment charges; do not substitute adjusted EPS without reconciliation.

The quarter ended June 28, reported in the August 7 filing, shows $399 million net income attributable to the company versus $483 million a year earlier. Shares outstanding on July 30 were 137,497,621. These are current financial observations, not evidence that earnings have reached a sustainable floor. An acquisition valuation needs consolidated debt, cash, minority interests and recurring capital needs in addition to the public share count. [Source above.]

Technical context: qualified industrial production can have a moat based on process expertise, certification and reliability rather than a consumer brand. Long program lives support repeat business but customer concentration can transfer bargaining power to the aircraft or engine maker. Backlog is not distributable cash and can move with production schedules. Test inventory, material costs, maintenance investment and acquisition accounting when translating reported profit into owner earnings. A whole-business price includes common equity and assumed net obligations; using enterprise value with after-interest earnings mismatches numerator and denominator. Normalize across relevant end markets instead of treating one strong period as permanent. [Source above; analytical framework.]

Accounting discipline: the figures stated above are reported observations unless expressly labeled arithmetic. A period-end balance is not a flow and a market mark is not purchase cost. Do not mix fiscal years, currencies, share bases or enterprise and equity values. To construct owner earnings, begin with the relevant reported profit, reconcile noncash items and subtract the investment required to maintain economic position. Changes in working capital and recurring compensation remain economic costs even when a convenient adjusted metric excludes them. If maintenance investment, leverage or the proper share denominator is missing, state that the yield cannot be completed rather than present false precision.

Evidence boundary: missing figures are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered for this digest. Analytical questions are not additional reported facts or claims about the investor's private thinking. Read the financial, risk and fund-context documents together; no individual accounting ratio supplies a complete investment conclusion.
