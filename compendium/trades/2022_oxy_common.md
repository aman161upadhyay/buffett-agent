## Buffett's thesis in his own words

The 2023 letter explains a long-term view of US oil/gas reserves, carbon-capture efforts and confidence in Vicki Hollub. Buffett explicitly said Berkshire had no interest in buying or managing the company. It is a later explanation, not a publicly available 2022 entry-day quotation. [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did

Large open-market common re-entry in 2022, followed by additions. The 2025 beneficial-ownership filing is **Schedule 13G**, not a 13D activist-control filing; it includes exercisable warrants, unlike the common-only 13F. At Q2 2026 common shares were 264,941,431. [1,2; filing table]
