## Buffett's thesis in his own words

November 14 reporting says Buffett was struck by IBM’s ability to retain corporate clients. The 2011 letter explains why a shareholder planning to add should welcome lower prices while IBM repurchases stock. This is a per-share ownership argument, not a claim every buyback is accretive. [1,2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

An explicit attempt to view enterprise technology through customer entrenchment and capital allocation. The subsequent revision tests whether perceived switching costs protected economics as expected. [1–3]

## Public discussion

Reuters described a spectacular reversal of Buffett’s historic technology aversion; the actual explanation was customer retention, not a new policy to buy all technology stocks. [2]
