The last public annual Berkshire cash and bills were approximately $167.6 billion at year-end 2023. Apple represented roughly half of the listed equity portfolio by market value, a different denominator from cash or total Berkshire assets. Large unrealized gains make tax important. This existing-position review does not include the current-quarter share-count change or Buffett's same-day allocation commentary. [Source above.]

The 2023 letter discusses Apple as a very large commitment and the longstanding Coca-Cola and American Express interests. It also describes Occidental and Japanese trading-house holdings. Those businesses and controlled subsidiaries remain productive assets even when cash is large. A matched full float and equity-portfolio concentration table was not recovered beyond the specific concentration facts stated above. [Fund baseline cited above.]

Technical context: a consumer ecosystem may monetize both a device and recurring services, but they are linked rather than independent annuities. Customer retention, replacement frequency, pricing and the required cost of innovation determine owner economics. Separate hardware sales, installed-base activity and services revenue, avoiding double-counting value from the same customer relationship. Cash returned through buybacks must be compared with share issuance and purchase price; authorization is not execution. Foreign-held cash, taxes and operating liquidity needs affect deployable excess capital. Use one split basis for price, shares and EPS, since mismatched adjustments can make an otherwise correct earnings multiple meaningless. [Source above; analytical framework.]

Allocation discipline: Berkshire is an operating and insurance company, not a redeemable investment partnership. The metadata liquidity figure is a dated scale reference, not contemporaneous discretionary NAV. Cash supports insurance, subsidiaries and contractual obligations before any new allocation. Equity-portfolio weight, percentage of issuer ownership and percentage of cash are different sizing measures. An existing position's basis affects tax but does not determine current intrinsic value. Distinguish a deputy's mandate from a Buffett-scale commitment, and avoid assigning a named decision maker without evidence. This context does not disclose the action being evaluated; the analyst must weigh opportunity cost and the durability of the underlying business independently.

Analytical questions are not additional reported facts or claims about the investor's private thinking. Read the financial, risk and fund-context documents together; no individual accounting ratio supplies a complete investment conclusion.
