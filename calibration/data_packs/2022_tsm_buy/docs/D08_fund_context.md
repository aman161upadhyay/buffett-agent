Title: Berkshire context without the allocation answer
Source: https://investor.tsmc.com/chinese/encrypt/files/encrypt_file/reports/2022-10/a924f47cae2629ecb5f8801629cf7fb8773e5444/3Q22EarningsRelease.pdf (published 2022-10-13); https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2021ltr.pdf (last public fund baseline identified in text)
Date: 2022-11-14

Berkshire's approximately $146.7 billion year-end 2021 cash provides scale. This scenario is a fresh decision without revealing a current position. Large cash resources allow patience but do not solve the difficulty of pricing a geographical catastrophe. A proposed position should be compared with other global franchises, including those operating in jurisdictions with less concentrated production risk. [Source above.]

The 2021 letter reports $147 billion float and highlights Apple, American Express, Bank of America and Coca-Cola among the major equity interests. Their durable economics compete for additional capital. Float is held against policyholder claims, not redeemable shareholder funds. A new allocation must preserve liquidity without treating every government bill as permanently unavailable for investment. [Fund baseline cited above.]

Technical context: foundry manufacturing has scale and process barriers but requires continual expensive reinvestment. Gross margin is not owner earnings after fabs, equipment, research and working capital. Distinguish leading-edge from mature processes, production location from customer location, and booked revenue from durable through-cycle utilization. A technically excellent company can have concentration risks outside management control. Overseas facilities require time, qualification and capital; they are not immediately interchangeable with an existing production cluster. An ADR may represent several ordinary shares, so EPS and price must use the same unit. A low multiple on a peak quarter is not automatically conservative. [Source above; analytical framework.]

Allocation discipline: Berkshire is an operating and insurance company, not a redeemable investment partnership. The metadata liquidity figure is a dated scale reference, not contemporaneous discretionary NAV. Cash supports insurance, subsidiaries and contractual obligations before any new allocation. Equity-portfolio weight, percentage of issuer ownership and percentage of cash are different sizing measures. An existing position's basis affects tax but does not determine current intrinsic value. Distinguish a deputy's mandate from a Buffett-scale commitment, and avoid assigning a named decision maker without evidence. This context does not disclose the action being evaluated; the analyst must weigh opportunity cost and the durability of the underlying business independently.

Evidence boundary: missing figures are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered for this digest. Analytical questions are not additional reported facts or claims about the investor's private thinking. Read the financial, risk and fund-context documents together; no individual accounting ratio supplies a complete investment conclusion.
