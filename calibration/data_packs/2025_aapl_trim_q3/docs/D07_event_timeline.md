Title: A published record, not a quarter-end ownership update
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019324000123/aapl-20240928.htm ; https://www.apple.com/newsroom/pdfs/fy2025-q2/FY25_Q2_Consolidated_Financial_Statements.pdf ; https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/05/apple-reports-second-quarter-results/ ; https://www.cnbc.com/2025/05/01/apple-has-managed-tariffs-so-far-says-tough-to-predict-beyond-june.html
Date: 2025-06-30

November 1 of the previous year: Apple filed its annual report, including the fiscal three-year record and risk disclosures. May 1: it published March results, increased the dividend and authorized additional repurchases. The same day, Cook discussed the June-quarter tariff estimate. June 30: the quoted close was $205.17. The June accounting period has ended, but its earnings are not yet an available input. [1-4; historical price below]

This matters to an existing shareholder: the analyst can compare the original consumer-franchise thesis with current margins and pricing, without smuggling in an unreleased quarter. Changes in a large shareholder's holdings after the last public report also cannot be assumed merely from changes in share price. Product loyalty, capital returns and trade-policy exposure remain distinct questions. No prediction is made about a specific future device release or the date on which a large owner might alter its exposure. Private tax lots and execution instructions are not part of the record.

Evidence ledger: fiscal revenues in 2022, 2023 and 2024 were $394.328 billion, $383.285 billion and $391.035 billion. Fiscal 2024 diluted EPS was $6.08. In the March quarter, revenue was $95.359 billion, operating income $29.589 billion, net income $24.780 billion and diluted EPS $1.65. Products supplied $68.714 billion sales and Services $26.645 billion. First-half operating cash flow was $53.887 billion and capital spending $6.011 billion. March cash and marketable securities totaled $132.922 billion; commercial paper and term debt totaled $98.186 billion. The board authorized another $100 billion repurchase program and a $0.26 quarterly dividend. Cook estimated about $900 million additional tariff costs for the June quarter, conditional on unchanged policy. [1-4]

Timing discipline: the chronology separates publication dates from the economic periods discussed. A result for an earlier quarter only becomes an input when released. The market close is an observation available on the stated assessment date, not evidence of a particular investor's execution. This timeline includes no presumed private conversations or unannounced agreement. There is no catalyst completion forecast here: the analyst must decide whether the available business evidence is sufficient without relying on a future disclosure to reveal someone else's decision.
