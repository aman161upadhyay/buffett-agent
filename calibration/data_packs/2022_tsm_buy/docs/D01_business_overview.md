Title: Business economics and ownership claim
Source: https://investor.tsmc.com/chinese/encrypt/files/encrypt_file/reports/2022-10/a924f47cae2629ecb5f8801629cf7fb8773e5444/3Q22EarningsRelease.pdf (published 2022-10-13)
Date: 2022-11-14

TSMC is a dedicated semiconductor foundry producing chips designed by customers rather than competing with them through a broad branded chip portfolio. Advanced process technology, manufacturing yield, scale and customer trust support the franchise. Fabrication facilities are exceptionally capital intensive and technologically demanding. The leading-edge production base is concentrated in Taiwan, so business quality and geographic risk must be assessed separately. [Source above.]

Technical context: foundry manufacturing has scale and process barriers but requires continual expensive reinvestment. Gross margin is not owner earnings after fabs, equipment, research and working capital. Distinguish leading-edge from mature processes, production location from customer location, and booked revenue from durable through-cycle utilization. A technically excellent company can have concentration risks outside management control. Overseas facilities require time, qualification and capital; they are not immediately interchangeable with an existing production cluster. An ADR may represent several ordinary shares, so EPS and price must use the same unit. A low multiple on a peak quarter is not automatically conservative. [Source above; analytical framework.]

Underwriting questions: identify the actual paying customer, repeat-purchase or replacement mechanism, and what prevents a capable competitor from taking the profit. Distinguish an enduring economic advantage from an asset that is merely expensive to build. The ownership claim being evaluated must match the economics described: operating control, common shares and a preferred claim confer different rights. Do not infer honest or capable management solely from a famous name or a long corporate history. Where direct customer-retention or pricing evidence is absent, leave that criterion uncertain rather than supply a generic franchise label. Record what observable deterioration would invalidate the business thesis before considering position size.
