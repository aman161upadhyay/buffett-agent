## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**2001, not 2000:** MiTek's UK parent wanted to sell. CEO Gene Toombs sent Buffett a connector plate and a letter before the annual meeting. [1]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
“Our kind of manager and MiTek our kind of business.” For managers' equity: “What they paid is what they live with.” **February 2002 retrospective.** [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Made a cash offer; arranged for 55 managers to buy 10%, each investing at least $100,000. Management bore actual capital risk. [1]

## What happened after
No long-run transaction return supplied. Subsequent construction-cycle losses should not be erased by the initially enthusiastic assessment. [1]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Niche:** Leading product used in roof trusses. [1]
- **Operator:** Toombs impressed Buffett directly. [1]
- **Alignment:** Cash-funded stakes exposed managers to losses. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: management alignment has a specific economic meaning, not merely owning options. [1]
