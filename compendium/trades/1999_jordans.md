## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**1999, not 2000:** Berkshire acquired Jordan's during 1999. The later acquisition-spree grouping must not change the actual year. Exact entry-date valuation remains unknown. [1]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
“Jordan's has the highest sales per square foot of any major furniture operation in the country.” **March 2000 retrospective:** Buffett linked the business results to the Tatelmans' “shoppertainment” customer experience and regional dominance. [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Acquired Jordan's for cash in 1999. The letter explicitly contrasts this and the agreed MidAmerican transaction with stock-funded acquisitions. [1]

Barry and Eliot Tatelman had grown the family business into the largest furniture retailer in Massachusetts and New Hampshire. They gave employees at least 50 cents for every hour worked, $9 million total from their own proceeds, not Berkshire money. The anecdote supports Buffett's culture assessment; it is not an extra Berkshire acquisition payment. [1]
