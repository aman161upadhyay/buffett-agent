## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**Summer 1997 decision:** Buffett and Munger assessed materially declining bullion inventories. Exact dated inventory series and an entry-price model were not recovered; the annual letter supplies the following-year explanation. [1]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
“A higher price would be needed to establish equilibrium between supply and demand.” “Inflation expectations ... play no part in our calculation of silver's value.” **February 1998 retrospective.** [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Purchased 111.2 million ounces during 1997. This annual-letter figure should not be silently replaced by later reports of larger aggregate holdings. Exact 1997–98 settlement and storage chronology unknown. [1]

## What happened after
Marked to market, the position generated a $97.4 million pretax gain in 1997. This is not realized lifetime P&L; ultimate liquidation remains a sourcing gap. Status is the historical checkpoint. [1]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Inventories:** Physical supply depletion. [1]
- **Equilibrium:** Price needed to ration demand or encourage supply. [1]
- **Boundary:** Explicitly not an inflation hedge rationale. [1]
