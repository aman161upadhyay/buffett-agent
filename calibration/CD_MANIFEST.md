## Replay boundary and grading cautions

Dates and modes requested by the assignment are retained. Many are disclosure dates or points inside an already-started accumulation, **not** first-trade predictions. `first_action_date` is null where an exact execution day was not authenticated; the window is in metadata. New mode for COP, Apple 2016, IBM, Japan and TSM buy is a fresh-underwriting counterfactual, not a false claim of no historical ownership. New mode for BNSF/PCC means control acquisition, and for ATVI means a new arbitrage strategy layered on a deputy's existing stock.

Same-day outcome disclosures (Apple May 4, cash November 2, TSM November 14/February 14, Japan announcement) are deliberately omitted from docs even when technically public on the calendar date. They are the decisions to predict. Same-day financing term sheets may be used with acceptance, provider and endorsement removed; next-day negotiated terms are key-only. The 2017 Apple pack includes the after-hours results with explicit end-of-day boundary, while the close is pre-release.

**Grades:** B = useful sourced reconstruction with material price/financial gaps; C = major financial or attribution gaps or reasoning not independently scoreable. No pack is claimed A-quality. Digests include explicit accounting/underwriting frameworks; these are not quotes or additional company facts. D05 stress cases are analyst reconstructions, not fabricated named bearish commentary. Exact consensus, historical peer tables and many cutoff quotes were not recovered. Do not interpret generic analytical questions as authenticated private Buffett reasons.

**Source corrections:** BNSF's $34B was total equity value including the 22.6% already owned; the incremental 77.4% was about $26.3–26.5B, with $44B enterprise value including debt. PCC's $37.2B is enterprise value, not incremental equity cash. Heinz announced $12.12B commitment ($8B preferred + $4.12B common); closing common was about $4.25B. COP was already held in 2006. Apple starter deputy not identified publicly as Combs versus Weschler. ATVI deputy pre-bid shares are not Buffett's arbitrage. BAC below-10% crossing occurred in October, not July; motive not disclosed. WFC and TSM final exits are multi-quarter, not complete sales on replay dates.

Error cases retain BUY labels. Mea culpas and later sale reasons live only in keys. TSM entry, BAC trim and deputy Apple lack authenticated contemporaneous own-words drivers: do not invent three drivers to score them. WFC culture criticism is documented, but is not proof of the motive for each later sale. Preferred contractual economics are not necessarily three independently stated investor reasons.
