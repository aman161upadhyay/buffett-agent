## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**2006–07 accumulation:** The April 2007 disclosure revealed 10.9%. Contemporary Reuters explicitly said the purchase reason was not immediately clear. Later control-purchase explanations must not be silently backdated to the first trade. [1]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
A direct original minority-entry quotation remains unknown. The 2009 whole-company announcement supplies a later, separate thesis; see `2009_bnsf_bid.md`. [1–2]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Disclosed 39,027,430 shares as of April 5, 2007, then accumulated to 76,777,029 / 22.5% by December 2009. The 2009 letter carried the holding at $85.78 per share under its accounting presentation; that is not a verified weighted execution price. [1–2]

## What happened after
The stake melded into the full acquisition. No sale-return multiple should be created by treating merger accounting as cash proceeds. [2]
