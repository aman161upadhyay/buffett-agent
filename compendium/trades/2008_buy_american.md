## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**October 16, 2008:** Financial panic was spreading into the real economy. Buffett explicitly expected worsening unemployment/business activity; buying was not a claim that recession or further market declines were over. [1–2]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
“So ... I've been buying American stocks.” “This is my personal account I'm talking about.” The essay says favorable long-term prospects can coexist with frightening short-term news. [1–2]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
**No Berkshire transaction is identified by this essay.** Buffett described personal purchases and a conditional move toward all-US-equity non-Berkshire personal wealth. His Berkshire shares were expressly excluded from the account description. [1–2]
