## Definitions and limits

- One row per CUSIP/class. Changed CUSIPs remain separate so mergers, tracker restructurings and split-offs are not silently erased; 174 is a security count, not 174 independent companies/trades.
- First/last quarter mean presence **inside this window**. A first Q1 2010 row is left-censored, not proof Berkshire first bought then. Gaps and re-entries are possible (e.g. Exxon, Verizon, Delta, Occidental). Last presence is not execution date. If last is Q2 2026, it is right-censored, not an exit.
- Peak is largest quarter-end **reported market value within the window**, USD millions; not cash invested, realized gain or lifetime peak. An amended confidential entry is assigned to its economic quarter with its later public-disclosure date preserved in case files/ledgers.
- Rows across other managers are summed once per CUSIP; raw other-manager subaccount rows are not separate positions. NEAM separately filed holdings are not mixed in. Reported puts/calls would be separated; none were parsed in this parent series.
- Values through Q3 2022 are reported in thousands, from Q4 2022 in dollars. No artificial 1,000× peak at the transition.
- Investment decider is **not** the 13F other-manager identifier for Warren Buffett. Unknown means ownership verified, originating investor not authenticated. Attribution can change across episodes; see linked cases.
- This census excludes controlled subsidiaries, preferred/credit instruments not reportable on 13F, Treasury bills and most foreign ordinary shares (Japan, Munich Re). Their separate cases are in the [index](INDEX_2010_2026.md).
