## Definitions and source discipline

**Sunday October 25 has no regular-market close. Every price below is October 23's regular close**, US at 4pm EDT, Tokyo at 3:30pm JST; no after-hours substitution. Each ticker links its retrieved statistics source, dated by that page's quote timestamp; its companion overview supplies the 52-week high: US `https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/<lowercase-ticker>/`, Japan `https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/<code>/`. Those same overview pages supply business descriptions underlying the analyst screens below. Dynamic pages must be archived before a later rerun; these transcribed observations are frozen to October 23, not promises that live URLs remain unchanged.

P/Es = vendor trailing / consensus forward, NOT comparable definitions of owner earnings. **Y = vendor trailing pretax income / current market capitalization × 100**; spread = Y minus **5.00% US ten-year** ([Treasury, 2026-10-23](https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?field_tdr_date_value_month=202610&type=daily_treasury_yield_curve)). No arbitrary universal tax gross-up. High/drawdown = observed intraday 52-week high and calculated close/high−1. Net debt = negative of vendor net cash, **$bn** for US-listed names, **¥tn** for Japan; negatives denote net cash. Buyback yield is vendor year-over-year **net share-count shrinkage**, not gross repurchase cash/market cap. Negative means dilution; acquisitions, splits and equity issuance matter.

Financials are the vendor's latest available balance-sheet/TTM snapshot as retrieved October 25, not October daily financial statements; underlying fiscal periods and nonrecurring adjustments have not all been independently audited. In particular **bank net cash is NOT distributable surplus**, insurer debt excludes insurance claims, Deere includes captive finance, and lease treatment can inflate retail debt. Pretax yield is an initial screen, not maintenance-capex-adjusted owner earnings or a recommended discount rate. OXY disposal gains/preferred claims and Alphabet investment gains can distort comparisons; forward P/E exceeding trailing P/E is a warning against capitalizing a windfall. US and yen yields are not currency-hedged equivalent returns.

## Eight 20%+ cloud tests, plus the directly observed housing signal

The following are **hypotheses of potentially fixable worries**, not assertions that a fix is assured. Cutoff sources:

1. **BAC −22.5%:** October 14 trading revenue misses and regulatory penalty; Q3 profit down 17% per [CNBC, 2026-10-14](https://www.cnbc.com/amp/2026/10/14/bank-of-america-bac-q3-2026-earnings.html). Fixable if operating execution/compliance recover without deposit franchise damage; kill if recurrence reveals deficient controls. Berkshire is trimming, an adverse prior.
2. **PEP −20.8%:** weak North America volumes/value proposition despite overseas demand; [AP, 2026-10-08](https://www.newspressnow.com/news/national_news/business/pepsico-sees-strong-overseas-demand-but-a-lackluster-north-american-performance-in-the-third-quarter/article_9b63e7b2-f79c-5189-ae4c-724d67e32dd8.html) reports price increases/cost reductions after disappointing results. Fixable through pricing/pack architecture; kill if brand elasticity structurally worsens.
3. **MCD −27.3%:** consumer budgets and rivals taking spend, [Consumer Edge reporting, 2026-10-12](https://www.briefs.co/news/mcdonald-s-bets-on-chicken-and-drinks-but-rivals-are-poachin/). Chicken/beverages/value can regain traffic; kill if franchisee returns deteriorate persistently. This is not proof of the whole peak-to-current move's cause.
4. **ADBE −27.5%:** AI displacement fear despite record Q3 revenue, [issuer release, 2026-09-10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000147/adbeex991q326.htm); [Bloomberg, same date](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-10/adobe-forecast-misses-estimates-renewing-fears-about-ai-impact) reports the next-quarter revenue outlook narrowly missing consensus. Fixable if AI monetization offsets substitution; kill if paid workflow retention erodes.
5. **NKE −47.0%:** slow turnaround/China and specialty competition, [UBS coverage, 2026-10-21](https://www.investing.com/news/analyst-ratings/ubs-maintains-neutral-on-nike-stock-citing-turnaround-concerns-93CH-4966779). Product/wholesale execution may recover; kill if preference shifts permanently. Forward P/E 28.71 is NOT obviously cheap normalized earnings.
6. **LULU −56.2%:** weak US product response and cautious H2 outlook, [Trefis, 2026-10-19](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/lululemons-next-big-thing-already-170713063.html). New products might restore demand; kill if the premium brand loses relevance. Too small for a normal Berkshire core passive position.
7. **PYPL −28.0%:** weak branded checkout, competition and pricing pressure, [Cleveland Research coverage, 2026-10-14](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/paypal-falls-as-cleveland-research-downgrades-shares-on-weakening-trends-4957358). Fixable execution versus permanent network erosion must be distinguished; share shrinkage alone does not prove moat.
8. **GOOGL −21.6%:** AI economics/large capital commitments specifically flagged by Buffett July 15 [F02]; an [October 24 plaintiff-lawyer release](https://natlawreview.com/press-releases/investor-notification-alphabet-goog-googl-investors-are-reminded-contact-bfa) alleges AI-related disclosure problems. Allegations are **not established findings**, and their causal/financial magnitude is unverified. Fixable thesis: monetization/customer economics survive; kill if capex permanently absorbs the cash franchise.

**LEN −36.5%** is a separate observable accumulation signal rather than an eighth or ninth purported franchise-rescue story: [IBD, 2026-10-06](https://www.investors.com/news/berkshire-hathaway-lennar-housing-mortgage-rates-real-estate/) documents purchases amid rising mortgage rates. Existing housing acquisitions support circle-of-competence, not certainty about demand or intrinsic value.
