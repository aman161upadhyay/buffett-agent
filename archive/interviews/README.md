# Interviews, testimony, op-ed and annual-meeting Q&A

**Cutoff/retrieval: 2026-10-25.** 20 dated source records; some sessions contain multiple decision questions. These notes are paraphrase, not invented verbatim transcripts. Publication/upload dates differ from event dates. Exact timestamps are unknown unless the source exposes them; chapter/question locators are supplied instead.

## 1. 1991-09 (day conflicting) — Salomon congressional testimony

- **Decider / speaker:** Buffett.
- **Specific decisions:** Reputation and compliance.
- **Process explanation:** Lose money versus lose reputation; new management must repair illegal conduct and cooperate with investigators. This is a crisis-management decision, not an endorsement of the original security.
- **Locator and evidence limits:** House opening statement, reproduced by WSJ; later sources disagree September 4 versus 5. Do not invent exact day.
- **Source:** [record](https://www.wsj.com/articles/BL-MB-21380).

## 2. 2008-10-16 online / October 17 print — Buy American. I Am. — signed NYT op-ed

- **Decider / speaker:** Buffett.
- **Specific decisions:** Personal-account equity purchases.
- **Process explanation:** He was moving his personal non-Berkshire account from government bonds into US equities because prices offered attractive long-term prospects during fear, not because he could time the bottom.
- **Locator and evidence limits:** Opening account scope and Why? paragraphs; personal account, not Berkshire’s reported common portfolio.
- **Source:** [record](https://www.nytimes.com/2008/10/17/opinion/17buffett.html).

## 3. 2009-11-13 broadcast; November 16 filing — Charlie Rose

- **Decider / speaker:** Buffett.
- **Specific decisions:** BNSF purchase.
- **Process explanation:** Durable US freight demand, efficiency, long-lived infrastructure and acceptable return explain a large capital-intensive acquisition. It need not be a bargain cigar butt.
- **Locator and evidence limits:** BNSF interview excerpt; SEC 425 transcript is a primary filed reproduction.
- **Source:** [record](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1067983/000119312509235940/d425.htm).

## 4. 2011-11-14 — CNBC Squawk Box, Becky Quick

- **Decider / speaker:** Buffett.
- **Specific decisions:** IBM entry.
- **Process explanation:** IT-department stickiness, understanding gained by reading reports and asking operating subsidiaries, management performance and share repurchases. These are the original stated reasons, not the later competitive reassessment.
- **Locator and evidence limits:** Part 5, IBM discussion; CNBC labels transcript unofficial.
- **Source:** [record](https://www.cnbc.com/2011/11/14/cnbc-transcript-warren-buffett-explains-why-he-bought-107b-of-ibm-stock-part-5.html).

## 5. 2016-02-29 — CNBC interview

- **Decider / speaker:** Buffett.
- **Specific decisions:** IBM uncertainty and Tesco mistake.
- **Process explanation:** Price weakness alone is not proof, but IBM could be a mistake; Tesco demonstrates willingness to recognize wrong business appraisal.
- **Locator and evidence limits:** IBM could be a mistake clip; full trade economics not supplied by clip alone.
- **Source:** [record](https://buffett.cnbc.com/video/2016/02/29/buffett-ibm-could-be-a-mistake.html).

## 6. 2017-02-27 — CNBC Squawk Box, Becky Quick

- **Decider / speaker:** Buffett.
- **Specific decisions:** Apple scaling and airline additions.
- **Process explanation:** Apple evaluated as a consumer product; Berkshire had sharply expanded the holding. Airlines reflected a changed industry assessment rather than a permanent sector ban.
- **Locator and evidence limits:** Full interview: Apple/IBM and airline questions; deputy origin distinguished from Buffett’s additions.
- **Source:** [record](https://www.cnbc.com/2017/02/27/billionaire-investor-warren-buffett-speaks-with-cnbcs-becky-quick-on-squawk-box.html).

## 7. 2017-05-04 — CNBC Becky Quick interview

- **Decider / speaker:** Buffett.
- **Specific decisions:** IBM trim.
- **Process explanation:** He revalued IBM downward after observing stronger competition and results; lower stock price did not compel more buying when business value changed.
- **Locator and evidence limits:** Interview report with direct quotations; not annual-meeting Q&A.
- **Source:** [record](https://www.cnbc.com/2017/05/04/warren-buffett-has-revalued-ibm-downward-cites-big-strong-competitors.html).

## 8. 2017-05-06 — Berkshire annual meeting, morning

- **Decider / speaker:** Buffett / Munger.
- **Specific decisions:** IBM versus Apple; airline reversal; Wells Fargo.
- **Process explanation:** Specific explanations of selling IBM while buying Apple; changed competitive structure in airlines; Wells Fargo failure to correct misconduct promptly.
- **Locator and evidence limits:** Chapters IBM, airlines, Wells Fargo. Do not collapse these into a single quality thesis.
- **Source:** [record](https://buffett.cnbc.com/video/2017/05/06/morning-session---2017-berkshire-hathaway-annual-meeting.html).

## 9. 2017-05-08 — CNBC post-meeting interview

- **Decider / speaker:** Buffett / Munger.
- **Specific decisions:** Apple, IBM, missed Google.
- **Process explanation:** Consumer franchise conviction contrasts with IBM revision; Google omission illustrates knowledge that was available but not acted upon, not a successful investment.
- **Locator and evidence limits:** Buying Apple, selling IBM, missing Google full interview. Munger’s words are not Buffett’s.
- **Source:** [record](https://buffett.cnbc.com/video/2017/05/08/buffett-on-buying-apple-selling-ibm-missing-google-full-interview.html).

## 10. 2017-08-30 — CNBC Squawk on the Street

- **Decider / speaker:** Buffett.
- **Specific decisions:** IBM admission; Bank of America.
- **Process explanation:** IBM was wrong; Apple differs; contemporaneous expectation to own BofA for a long time. Later BofA sales are a change requiring dated evidence, not proof this statement never happened.
- **Locator and evidence limits:** I was wrong on IBM full interview.
- **Source:** [record](https://buffett.cnbc.com/video/2017/08/30/buffett-i-was-wrong-on-ibm-full-interview.html).

## 11. 2018-05-04 — CNBC Becky Quick interview

- **Decider / speaker:** Buffett.
- **Specific decisions:** IBM full exit.
- **Process explanation:** Confirms the IBM position was fully sold after prior trims; separates full exit from the 2017 partial reduction.
- **Locator and evidence limits:** CNBC interview report. Do not invent a total realized loss from exit confirmation alone.
- **Source:** [record](https://www.cnbc.com/2018/05/04/warren-buffett-says-berkshire-hathaway-has-sold-completely-out-of-ibm.html).

## 12. 2019-05-04; posted May 6 — Berkshire annual meeting, morning

- **Decider / speaker:** unknown deputy for Amazon; Buffett explaining.
- **Specific decisions:** Amazon attribution; Apple; buybacks.
- **Process explanation:** Amazon was bought by one of the investment managers; this does not prove Buffett initiated it. Buybacks depend on price, and Apple quotation changes matter to an accumulating owner.
- **Locator and evidence limits:** Amazon buyer and buyback/Apple questions; individual Combs versus Weschler identity unknown here.
- **Source:** [record](https://buffett.cnbc.com/video/2019/05/06/morning-session---2019-berkshire-hathaway-annual-meeting.html).

## 13. 2020-05-02; posted May 4 — Berkshire virtual annual meeting

- **Decider / speaker:** Buffett.
- **Specific decisions:** Airlines full exit; crisis liquidity.
- **Process explanation:** He accepted responsibility for the airlines; changed demand, excess capacity and new financing obligations widened future economics. Cash preserved resilience; Fed-supported financing reduced the need for a Buffett-style rescue.
- **Locator and evidence limits:** Airlines and cash/Fed questions; not a prediction that stocks could not rebound.
- **Source:** [record](https://buffett.cnbc.com/video/2020/05/04/berkshire-hathaway-annual-meeting--may-02-2020.html).

## 14. 2022-04-30 — Berkshire annual meeting

- **Decider / speaker:** Buffett.
- **Specific decisions:** Occidental accumulation.
- **Process explanation:** Reading Vicki Hollub’s capital-allocation discussion produced a view of management acting rationally; market liquidity allowed rapid buying. This is not verified as a takeover plan.
- **Locator and evidence limits:** Occidental discussion, live reporting with direct quotes; lower-evidence than complete transcript for fine detail.
- **Source:** [record](https://www.cnbc.com/2022/04/30/berkshire-hathaway-annual-meeting-live-updates.html).

## 15. 2023-04-12 — CNBC Tokyo interview, Becky Quick; Abel also interviewed

- **Decider / speaker:** Buffett (Japan); Abel separate speaker.
- **Specific decisions:** Five Japanese trading houses.
- **Process explanation:** Attractive earning yields, growing dividends, buybacks and diversified operating similarities; yen financing must be modeled separately. Abel participation does not make each sentence his.
- **Locator and evidence limits:** Full transcript: Japanese investments, banking and Abel sections; differentiated speakers.
- **Source:** [record](https://www.cnbc.com/2023/04/12/full-transcript-berkshire-hathaway-chairman-ceo-warren-buffett-speaks-with-cnbcs-becky-quick-on-squawk-box-today-.html).

- **Decider / speaker:** Buffett.
- **Specific decisions:** Japan and TSMC/geopolitics.
- **Process explanation:** Japan management meetings and long-duration ownership; geopolitical location can alter risk even for a technically excellent business.
- **Locator and evidence limits:** Published interview; publication date not assumed exact interview recording date.

## 17. 2023-05-06; posted May 8 — Berkshire annual meeting, afternoon

- **Decider / speaker:** Buffett.
- **Specific decisions:** Occidental no-control intention; Apple durability.
- **Process explanation:** No offer for Occidental control despite share/warrant ownership; Apple’s consumer economics differ from generic technology forecasting.
- **Locator and evidence limits:** Occidental and Apple questions; do not convert regulatory permission into intent.
- **Source:** [record](https://buffett.cnbc.com/video/2023/05/08/afternoon-session---2023-meeting.html).

## 18. 2024-05-04; posted May 6 — Berkshire annual meeting, morning

- **Decider / speaker:** Buffett.
- **Specific decisions:** Apple trim, tax and cash.
- **Process explanation:** 21% federal corporate tax on gains versus possible future increases helped justify selling; Apple remained admired. Cash relative to available alternatives looked attractive.
- **Locator and evidence limits:** Apple first question and cash discussion; not a verified BofA rationale.
- **Source:** [record](https://buffett.cnbc.com/video/2024/05/06/morning-session---2024-meeting.html).

## 19. 2024-05-04; posted May 6 — Berkshire annual meeting, afternoon

- **Decider / speaker:** Buffett.
- **Specific decisions:** Paramount, Occidental, succession.
- **Process explanation:** Paramount decision was entirely Buffett’s, not a deputy’s; full sale lost money and forced more thought about leisure economics. Abel should have final investment authority as CEO.
- **Locator and evidence limits:** Paramount question; investment insight and future Abel authority chapters.
- **Source:** [record](https://buffett.cnbc.com/video/2024/05/06/afternoon-session---2024-meeting.html).

## 20. 2025-05-03; posted May 5 — Berkshire annual meeting

- **Decider / speaker:** Buffett; Abel on his own answers.
- **Specific decisions:** CEO succession and cash.
- **Process explanation:** Buffett proposed year-end CEO retirement and Abel succession. Statements about the cash asset do not promise a deployment date. Distinguish proposal at meeting from board approval and January 2026 effectiveness.
- **Locator and evidence limits:** Meeting closing announcement and cash questions.
- **Source:** [record](https://buffett.cnbc.com/2025-berkshire-hathaway-annual-meeting).
