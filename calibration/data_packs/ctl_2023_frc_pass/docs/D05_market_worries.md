Affluent relationships can imply large uninsured balances that leave quickly. Long-duration mortgages remain creditworthy yet lose economic value when market rates rise; replacing cheap deposits with expensive wholesale funding compresses earnings. The fourth-quarter margin decline precedes the acute stress. New institutional deposits are liabilities, not new common equity, and do not themselves absorb losses. If restoring confidence requires permanent expensive funding or asset sales below carrying value, the historical tangible-book discount can overstate the common shareholder's protection. [S1,S2; analytical inference]

A reliable March 16 close or last-preceding close is not recovered in the assembled contemporaneous sources, so no precise price-to-book or P/E is invented. The available benchmarks are $8.25 annual EPS and $74.19 tangible book per share at December 31. Both are stale for a fast-moving deposit run. The valuation should restate earnings at replacement funding rates and mark long-duration assets before using a book discount as a margin of safety. The $30 billion support is a deposit liability, not an equity valuation or government guarantee for stockholders. [S1,S2; analytical framework]

### Dated operating baseline

Full-year revenue was $5.9 billion, up 16.5%; net interest income $4.8 billion, up 17.5%; and net income approximately $1.7 billion, up 12.7%. Diluted EPS was $8.25, up 7.4%. Common book value was $75.38 and tangible common book value $74.19 per share. Loans were $166.9 billion, up 23.6%, while deposits were $176.4 billion, up 12.9%. Wealth-management assets were $271.2 billion; they belong to clients and are not bank liquidity. Wealth revenues were $877 million. Tier 1 leverage ratio was 8.51%. Reported nonperforming assets were just five basis points of total assets, and annual net charge-offs $2.9 million. Fourth-quarter net interest margin fell to 2.45% from 2.71% in the preceding quarter. Fourth-quarter EPS was $1.88 and net income $386 million, down 3.6% year on year. Historical credit quality and current funding resilience are different questions. [S1]

### Underwriting distinction

This objection is intended to be capable of defeating the affirmative case. Distinguish a reversible mark-to-market loss from permanent impairment, and a temporary earnings interruption from a permanently more capital-hungry business. The investor must survive the adverse scenario without relying on the next buyer, a friendly regulator or unrestricted external financing. A long holding period cannot restore capital that has been diluted away or consumed by obligations senior to the purchased security. [Analytical interpretation of S1]

Observable thesis-breakers include customer economics deteriorating despite continued spending, new funding coming only on punitive terms, or reported cash failing to reconcile to recurring obligations. These are analytical monitoring conditions, not claims that such outcomes have occurred. The record below supplies the current scale of exposure against which to test them. No later failure, recovery or management admission is used to strengthen the bear case. [Analytical interpretation of S1]
