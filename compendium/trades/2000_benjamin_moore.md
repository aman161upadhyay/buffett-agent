## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**2000:** A longstanding paint producer distributed through independent dealers. Bob Mundheim, known to Buffett through Salomon, introduced the possibility in July. Exact entry valuation multiple unknown. [1]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
“We liked them; we liked the business.” The letter calls thousands of independent dealers “a vital asset.” **February 2001 explanation after closing.** [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Buffett and Munger met past/present CEOs Richard Roob and Yvan Dupuy in late August and offered $1 billion cash. Board approval came in October; transaction completed December. [1]

## What happened after
No realized return or quantified dealer-network improvement is established here. [1]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Business:** Familiar, established paint demand. [1]
- **Distribution:** Independent dealers mattered to competitive position. [1]
- **People:** Trusted referral plus direct management assessment. [1]
