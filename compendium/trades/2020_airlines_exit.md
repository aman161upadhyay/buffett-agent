## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: 2020-03-31.** Berkshire owned the four large US carriers and the pandemic had grounded much of global flying. This file is a sell decision, not an assertion that Buffett should have known the pandemic at 2016 entry. [1]

**Snapshot gaps:** Entry-day audited valuation, trading range and independently documented consensus: unknown; do not substitute the first 13F mark for purchase cost. Financial/consensus gaps are not filled with later outcomes.

## Buffett's thesis in his own words

Buffett, May 2: “The world has changed for the airlines.” He questioned whether travel would return to the same level and pointed to excess planes and debt. He explicitly accepted responsibility for the initial investment rather than blaming a deputy. [1,2]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did

Entire AAL, DAL, LUV and United positions sold during April. April total stock-sale figures in coverage include more than airlines and must not be labeled the exact airline proceeds or realized loss. [1,2]

## What happened after

Airlines later rebounded, but a subsequent market rally is not evidence that the April balance-sheet and demand uncertainty did not exist. Exact basket total-return/P&L remains unknown in this pack. Delta reappears only in Q1 2026 as a separate new episode.
