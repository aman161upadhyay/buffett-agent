# 1987 Berkshire annual letter

**Information date:** 1988-02-29 (month-only where precise public release day was not independently established). **Era:** B. **Author:** Buffett. **Primary source:** [letter](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1987.html). All rules below cite sections of this source; section names are used rather than fabricated PDF page numbers. Annual-period labels are not publication dates.

## Process rules and decision explanations

- Marketable Securities—Permanent Holdings: Mr. Market supplies quotations; business economics, not mood, establish value.
- Marketable Securities—Other: opportunistic arbitrage requires analysis of event probability, financing/time, payoff and downside if it fails.
- Salomon preferred: record the specific negotiated security and management relationship, not a claim that all investment banks meet a franchise filter.
- Insurance Operations: willingness to let business shrink when prices inadequate; financial capacity must accompany underwriting judgment.
- See’s operator Chuck Huggins illustrates delegation after acquisition. Good operators do not require Omaha to run daily operations.

## Verbatim evidence anchors

- **Marketable Securities - Other; Purchase of $700 million in Salomon Inc preferred stock.** “By far our largest - and most publicized - investment in 1987 was a $700 million purchase of Salomon Inc 9% preferred stock.”
- **Fechheimer Bros. Company; Acquisition of a controlling interest in Fechheimer.** “In the year that Berkshire acquired its controlling interest in Fechheimer - 1986 - profits were a record. The Heldmans didn't slow down after that.”
- **See's Candies; Delegation of management to Chuck Huggins upon the purchase of See's.** “Chuck Huggins continues to set new records at See's, just as he has ever since we put him in charge on the day of our purchase some 16 years ago.”
- **Marketable Securities - Other; Delegation of strategy to perform opportunistic arbitrage on a limited scale.** “We have practiced arbitrage on an opportunistic basis for decades and, to date, our results have been quite good.”

## Attribution and point-in-time boundary

Buffett is the source author, but an acquired subsidiary’s bolt-on or operating action is not automatically his personally originated investment. Individual decider unknown where not identified. Historical narratives are available only from this information date. A section discussing earlier purchases is retrospective; do not load its later performance, confession or corrected valuation into the original entry replay.
