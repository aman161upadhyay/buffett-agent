Title: Berkshire's public starting portfolio and capital constraints
Source: https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2024ltr.pdf ; https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/qtrly/3rdqtr25.pdf ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1067983/000119312525282901/0001193125-25-282901.txt ; https://www.cnbc.com/2020/05/02/warren-buffett-says-berkshire-sold-its-entire-position-in-airlines-because-of-the-coronavirus.html ; https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/news/nov0125.pdf ; https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/boards-policy-regulation/berkshire-profit-rises-cash-soars-record-2025-11-01
Date: 2025-12-31

Berkshire is an insurance and operating-business owner, not a fully invested mutual fund. Insurance float finances assets but is also a liability to future claimants; it is not a second pool of free equity. A reported cash balance includes precautionary reserves and must not be equated with a mandate to buy securities immediately. Operating subsidiaries, minority equities, Treasury bills and own-share repurchases compete for capital. The annual letter emphasizes per-share intrinsic value, understandable businesses and financial strength. [fund sources]

Equity-portfolio value is a sizing reference, not total corporate net worth. The 13F covers specified U.S.-reportable securities, omits many wholly owned businesses and foreign holdings, and reports quarter-end marks rather than purchase costs. Manager codes identify reporting arrangements, not who originated each investment idea. A changed share count is observable only after the relevant filing is public. A mark-to-market change without a share change is not a trade.

The analyst should consider concentration, capital-gains taxes, liquidity and opportunity cost separately. Exact tax lots, private execution dates and subsidiary capital availability are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered here. No current order or future shareholder action is supplied. Prior public operating and ownership information below is context, not an instruction about the decision under assessment.

The November 14 public 13F for September 30 totals approximately $267.335 billion and lists no Delta position. Berkshire sold its airline holdings in the spring of 2020 after the industry outlook changed; there is no continuous old position to assume here. That historical experience is a caution about predictability and operating leverage, not a categorical public prohibition on examining the business at a different price and with a different balance sheet.

The November 1 update reports September float of approximately $176 billion; the contemporaneous Reuters account gives $381.7 billion gross cash/Treasury liquidity. Those are released September measures, not a live December balance. Buffett remains CEO at the December cutoff, with Abel's succession already public. There is no verified pre-cutoff statement assigning a new Delta idea to Buffett, Abel or a deputy. The metadata uses the public September 13F total rather than all corporate assets. A potential re-entry must be treated as a fresh underwriting question, not as an automatic reversal of a past manager's rationale.
