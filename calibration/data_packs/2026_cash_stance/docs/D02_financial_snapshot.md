Title: Operating cash generation versus mark-to-market net income
Source: https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2025ltr.pdf and https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/2025ar/202510-k.pdf (2026-02-28); https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/2023ar/202310-k.pdf (2024-02-24); https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/2021ar/2021ar.pdf (2022-02-26), historical financial statements
Date: 2026-05-01

The annual letter emphasizes operating earnings rather than short-term GAAP swings from equity prices. Operating earnings of $44.5 billion are below $47.4 billion a year earlier but above the cited five-year average. Operating cash flow of $46 billion supports continued capital allocation. That cash is not all excess: operating businesses also need reinvestment, and insurers must maintain capital and claims-paying ability. [1]

The 2021–2022 income/EPS comparatives use the 2023 filing's restated insurance-accounting basis, not the original annual-release figures. Historical book-equity/liquidity amounts are rounded disclosed endpoint observations; their scope is not a common-share investment fund NAV.

The broad 2025 liquidity figure adds $47.719bn insurance/other cash, $321.434bn bills and $4.158bn railroad/utility cash. It is NOT the $369.0bn insurance/other balance net of unsettled purchases. Bills and cash equivalents are separate balance-sheet lines; do not count either twice. Gross liquidity is not freely distributable shareholder capital, and float is a liability-funded investment source, not equity NAV.

Operating earnings divided by year-end parent book is approximately 5.5%, 6.5%, 6.7%, 7.3% and 6.2%; these are arithmetic proxies, not operating ROIC. The 2025 operating-earnings/revenue proxy is 12.0% versus 12.8% in 2024; revenue was nearly flat while operating earnings declined 6.2%. Consolidated 2025 D&A was $13.476bn versus $12.855bn; simple FCF is not owner earnings because insurance working capital, growth capex and regulated investment remain material.

The annual report distinguishes $369.0 billion cash/Treasury holdings net of unsettled purchases in insurance and other businesses from broader cash headlines. BNSF and BHE debt is structurally different from parent borrowings, so total consolidated debt should not be treated as immediately callable Omaha financing. Conversely, strong subsidiary assets cannot be assumed instantly liquid. There is no single mutual-fund NAV denominator for all Berkshire decisions. Equity portfolio value, total corporate equity, cash and float answer different sizing questions. This pack supplies those distinctions rather than inventing a current live AUM or a precise amount that can be committed without any reserve. [1,2]

Evidence ledger from the February 28 letter and annual report: 2025 operating earnings were $44.5 billion versus $47.4 billion in 2024; operating cash flow was $46 billion. Float was approximately $176 billion versus $171 billion a year earlier. The insurance-and-other cash, cash-equivalent and Treasury-bill balance, net of unsettled purchases, was $369.0 billion; broader headline cash definitions differ. The letter says total cash and U.S. Treasury holdings exceed $370 billion. Equity securities were $297.8 billion at year end, including holdings outside the U.S. 13F perimeter. There were no own-share repurchases during 2025. Holding-company/BHFC-type borrowings excluding BNSF and BHE were $45.8 billion. The report emphasizes redundant liquidity and independent financing capacity, not a guaranteed forecast of stock-market direction. [1,2]

Accounting discipline: all dollar amounts in this document retain the period and basis given in the evidence ledger. Revenue is not owner earnings; neither adjusted profit nor operating cash flow alone measures distributable cash. Maintenance investment, working capital, compensation dilution and financing claims must be considered separately. A quarter is not a full economic cycle. Where a stand-alone balance sheet, maintenance-capital split or historical consensus series is absent, it is unavailable rather than assumed to be zero or inferred from a later filing.
