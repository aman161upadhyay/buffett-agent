# Contemporary reception: COBF, VIC, Seeking Alpha and press

**Cutoff/retrieval: 2026-10-25.** Qualitative, dated argument archive, not a representative sentiment poll. Forum posts are assertions by participants, not verified issuer facts or Buffett's reasons. A thread creation date is not the date of every comment on a later page. Exa search text exposed excerpts; access-limited VIC and SA pages often exposed only headers/summaries. No paywalled thesis is reconstructed from imagination. Reddit has a separate record.

## 1999 — “lost his touch” and the technology refusal

**Critic / contemporary source:** Barron's “What's Wrong, Warren?” challenged Buffett amid weak relative performance and the internet boom. [Original archive URL, December 1999, exact original day not independently established in this pass](https://www.barrons.com/articles/SB945992010127068546). A later [Morningstar retrospective](https://www.morningstar.in/posts/59787/warren-buffett-lost-touch.aspx) identifies the original December 1999 context; its later return comparison is **not** contemporary evidence and is excluded from a 1999 replay.

**What was contested:** whether refusing fast-changing technology reflected disciplined knowledge boundaries or inability to adapt. **Primary contrast:** Buffett's 1999 report explains his inability to forecast which technology companies have durable economics, and acknowledges Gen Re's major underwriting loss. [2000-03-01, Investments and opening results](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1999htm.html). This report itself is later than the December criticism. **Decider: Buffett.** Do not write a victorious bubble-bursting account into the 1999 information set.

## 2011–2018 — IBM, stickiness versus secular competitive change

**Contemporary press at entry:** WIRED described the $10.7bn IBM position as defying a technology allergy. [2011-11-14](https://www.wired.com/2011/11/warren-buffett-ibm). That headline is not proof Buffett adopted a generic technology-sector view. His own [2011-11-14 CNBC explanation](https://www.cnbc.com/2011/11/14/cnbc-transcript-warren-buffett-explains-why-he-bought-107b-of-ibm-stock-part-5.html) emphasizes IT-department position/stickiness and capital allocation.

**COBF, May 2017:** a general-news page links the May 4 downward-revaluation interview and contains criticism of repeated revenue decline, management credibility, missed trends and underestimated new entrants. [General news, page 19; thread began 2014-07-17, relevant discussion around 2017-05-04](https://thecobf.com/forum/topic/10943-buffettberkshire-general-news/page/19/). The visible user headings do not unambiguously attribute every sentence; do not assign all criticism to the nearest username without full-page confirmation.

**Press at reversal:** ZDNet frames the sale as the end of the love affair, discusses the roughly one-third reduction and the failure to meet initial expectations. [2017-05-10](https://www.zdnet.com/article/4-billion-stock-sale-suggests-warren-buffetts-love-affair-with-ibm-is-over). [CNBC, 2017-05-04](https://www.cnbc.com/2017/05/04/warren-buffett-has-revalued-ibm-downward-cites-big-strong-competitors.html) supplies the actual business-value revision. **Decider: Buffett.** Criticism of the error should be retained; do not rebrand the sale as tax-loss harvesting absent evidence.

## 2016–2024 — Apple, the consumer-franchise interpretation and the trim

**Entry:** [Forbes, 2016-05-16](https://www.forbes.com/sites/laurengensler/2016/05/16/warren-buffett-berkshire-hathaway-new-stake-apple) reports the initially disclosed Apple holding. This is not proof Buffett personally originated it. **Decider: initial deputy unknown in this archive; later scaling Buffett.** [2017-02-27 CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2017/02/27/billionaire-investor-warren-buffett-speaks-with-cnbcs-becky-quick-on-squawk-box.html) gives his consumer-product interpretation.

**COBF at the big trim:** the [2024-08-03 Q2 earnings thread](https://thecobf.com/forum/topic/20874-brk-q2-24-earnings/) immediately focused on much larger cash/T-bills, Apple sales, weaker repurchases and the coming tax bill. Visible responses include enthusiasm for de-risking/possible tax foresight or an elephant, but also a bearish-market reading. These are competing interpretations, not confirmed motives.

**COBF criticism:** [page 4, posts dated 2024-08-05](https://thecobf.com/forum/topic/20874-brk-q2-24-earnings/page/4/) quotes Eldad alleging inconsistency with Berkshire's financial maxims; charlieruane compares earlier objections to excess cash while explicitly acknowledging higher current cash yields and a larger company. This is a substantive opportunity-cost/consistency critique, not simply name-calling.

**Seeking Alpha:** Gary Gambino, [2024-08-04](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4710205-berkshire-hathaway-whats-buffett-thinking-holding-all-that-cash), considers valuation, a large deal and legal reserves, while retaining a Buy opinion on Berkshire and cash optionality. Ben Short, [2024-08-27](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4717459-apple-is-a-great-business-and-well-valued), distinguishes Apple's business quality from valuation and reads the sale partly through tax and cash attractiveness. The latter exposed text contains internally inconsistent share-count shorthand; do not use it to override primary filings.

**Press:** [WSJ, 2024-08-03](https://www.wsj.com/finance/stocks/berkshirehathaway-brk-q2-earnings-report-2024-372e11a7) emphasizes the dramatic reduction; [Reuters, 2024-08-03](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/berkshires-cash-hits-277-bln-buffett-slashes-apple-stake-operating-profit-sets-2024-08-03) uses a defensive framing. Buffett's actual [May 4 tax discussion](https://buffett.cnbc.com/video/2024/05/06/morning-session---2024-meeting.html) predates the larger Q2 reduction. **Decider: Buffett for his stated trim; attribution of each execution is not independently known.**

## 2019–2020 — criticism of underdeployment, airline risk and inaction

**Before the pandemic:** [COBF Wedgewood-exit discussion, 2019-10-14 posts](https://thecobf.com/forum/topic/17183-wedgewood-partners-on-selling-their-brk-stake/page/2/) contains Gregmal's criticism that admiration of businesses and a benign market view were inconsistent with inability to find anything to buy; he speculated about obsession with a final elephant. Other posts challenge selection of Microsoft as an omission given Buffett's Gates relationship. These are participant arguments, not a verified explanation of Buffett's purchases or passes.

**Before the airline exit:** [COBF acquisition-speculation page 5, 2020-03-12 discussion](https://thecobf.com/forum/topic/16995-next-berkshire-acquisition-speculation/page/5/) debated buying an airline outright and whether regulatory complications implied unlikely versus impossible. [Page 6, 2020-03-22](https://thecobf.com/forum/topic/16995-next-berkshire-acquisition-speculation/page/6/) raises the risk that government would expect Berkshire rather than taxpayers to finance a wholly owned airline; other participants identify operating leverage, labor, safety and pandemics as tail risks. These are **pre-sale** arguments, not hindsight explanations of the April exit.

**At the meeting:** [COBF “Buffett AGM Comments,” 2020-05-03](https://thecobf.com/forum/topic/17828-buffett-agm-comments/) records Nomad reading Buffett as very cautious, perhaps bearish; it cites wide outcome ranges, too many planes and Fed funding on terms Berkshire would not offer. Responses also read the tone as bearish. The forum explicitly labels the notes fallible. “DEFCON” shorthand in a post is not an audited measure of economic risk.

**After the rebound:** Dave Portnoy criticized the airline sale and Buffett as past his prime/washed up while holding recovering airline/cruise positions. [Business Insider interview, 2020-06-23](https://www.businessinsider.com/dave-portnoy-interview-why-warren-buffett-airline-stock-sale-wrong-2020-6?IR=T&r=AU). This is genuine contemporary criticism of opportunity cost, but a short rebound is not proof of superior long-term underwriting. Conversely, Berkshire's later actions do not erase the inaction debate. **Decider for airline exit: Buffett; individual originators of every basket purchase not reconstructed here.**

## 2022–2024 — Occidental, a cash-flow thesis or a looming takeover?

**Seeking Alpha:** [Envision Research, 2022-07-14](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4523289-occidental-enterprise-products-re-engineer-warren-buffett-thinking) compares Occidental with Enterprise Products, warns that dividend/payout metrics can mislead in a cyclical business, and interprets Buffett's interest through capital return capacity. Its reverse-engineering is the author's inference, not Buffett's own full valuation model.

**Press speculation versus correction:** [Barron's, 2022-11-08](https://www.barrons.com/articles/warren-buffett-bets-big-on-energy-51667929870) highlights large Chevron/Occidental exposure. [Barron's, 2024-02-24](https://www.barrons.com/articles/warren-buffetts-annual-letter-berkshire-wont-buy-occidental-827afda6) emphasizes his explicit no-purchase/no-management statement. Regulatory permission to own more was not proof of an intended takeover. [2023 letter, 2024-02-24, Non-controlled Businesses](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2023ltr.pdf). **Decider: Buffett for expressed common-stock thesis and no-control intention.** Specific contemporaneous COBF bearish valuation analysis at initial 2022 entry remains unextracted here.

## 2024–2025 — cash hoard, BofA sales, buyback halt and succession

**November 2024 COBF:** [Q3 results thread, 2024-11-02](https://thecobf.com/forum/topic/21007-brk-q3-results/) discusses another Apple reduction, large taxable gains and how to reconcile cash including/excluding railroad/utility balances and T-bill payables. Forum tables use different perimeters; do not compare them as identical cash series. Some users read impressive market timing into the sales. That is outside interpretation, not a Buffett statement predicting a crash.

**Reuters, 2024-11-02:** [cash, Apple/BofA sales and operating profit](https://www.reuters.com/markets/us/berkshires-cash-sets-record-buffett-sells-apple-bofa-operating-profit-falls-2024-11-02) supplies contemporaneous reporting and an investor defense of readiness when others become capital-constrained. **Correction:** the verified tax explanation is for Apple at the May meeting; a same-rationale statement about BofA was not located. **BofA individual decider: unknown for each filing-level sale absent a direct attribution.**

**COBF before the 2024 annual report:** [2025-02-20/21 posts](https://thecobf.com/forum/topic/21207-2024-q4-and-annual-report/) discuss the possibility of retirement, cash as buyback capacity after such news, frustration with silence/inactivity and hope for a more positive letter. Parsad's retirement/buyback-war-chest suggestion is explicitly speculation; it must not be converted into the reason the cash was accumulated.

**SA quarterly tracking:** [John Vincent, 2025-05-15](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4787393-tracking-warren-buffetts-berkshire-hathaway-portfolio-q1-2025-update) tracks increases in Occidental, Constellation, Domino's, Pool and other holdings alongside reductions. A 13F-based list verifies reported exposures, not who made each decision. **Decider for smaller new positions: unknown unless separately sourced.** This is not a Buffett quote endorsing all these businesses.

**At succession announcement:** [COBF, 2025-05-04](https://thecobf.com/forum/topic/21297-what-does-the-2026-agm-look-like/) debates whether Buffett will still appear on stage, the role of Abel/Ajit and likely lower attendance. A linked director quote likens Buffett's possible role to Munger for Abel. These expectations are not a board resolution. [2026 proxy](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/meet01/2026proxystatement.pdf) supplies the actual January 1 effective date.

## 2026 — Abel-era accountability, early buybacks and forum source quality

**COBF annual-meeting thread:** [2026-03-03 schedule discussion and later April/May posts](https://thecobf.com/forum/topic/21680-berkshire-hathaway-annual-meeting-2026/) welcomes operating executives on stage and discusses changed attendance; [page 3, 2026-05-03](https://thecobf.com/forum/topic/21680-berkshire-hathaway-annual-meeting-2026/page/3/) discusses resumed small buybacks and a Tokio Marine agreement. Importantly, gfp identifies the long pasted analysis as an **AI-generated summary with errors**, and explicitly flags an erroneous claim that buybacks were below book value. Its further numeric claims are **not promoted to verified corporate facts** here. Primary 10-Q/contract verification remains required.

**Buyback expectations:** [COBF poll, 2026-05-11](https://thecobf.com/forum/topic/21754-berkshires-expected-buybacks-in-2026/) shows a wide range of expected full-year repurchase amounts and discussion of a return to buybacks. A poll is neither a company plan nor realized 2026 spending. Distinguish Abel's personal purchases, corporate repurchases and A-to-B conversions; share-count differences alone do not identify buybacks.

**Press on first letter:** [AP reproduction, 2026-02-28](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2026/02/28/greg-abel-praises-warren-buffett-and-promises-berkshire-hathaway-wont-retreat-from-investing/) and [Irish Times/FT, letter dated 2026-02-28; displayed update March 2](https://www.irishtimes.com/business/2026/02/28/greg-abel-tells-berkshire-hathaway-investors-its-cash-pile-is-not-a-retreat-from-deal-making) emphasize continuity and no retreat from investing. Primary evidence is [Abel’s 2026-02-28 letter, Capital Discipline and Pilot](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2025ltr.pdf), including a concrete criticism of Pilot's delayed management control. **Speaker/CEO: Abel. Original decider for inherited OxyChem/Bell deals: unknown here.** No unsupported late-2026 trade or rationale is invented.
