Title: A lower price does not settle normalized earnings
Source: https://ml.globenewswire.com/Resource/Download/3ef69300-da43-4e07-9468-985051ddf0ed
Date: 2026-03-31

The March 31 unadjusted close was $150.00. Against comparable annual EPS guidance of $11.30-$11.60, that corresponds to approximately 12.93-13.27 times; against reported guidance of $9.72-$10.02, approximately 14.97-15.43 times. Guidance concerns the fiscal year ending in February, but a completed fiscal period is not a public final result until released. These are guidance-based illustrations, not consensus next-year valuations. [1; historical price below]

Using management's roughly 176 million guided diluted shares gives an illustrative equity capitalization of $26.4 billion. Against $1.3-$1.4 billion guided free cash flow, the yield is about 4.92%-5.30%; both denominator and numerator are approximate guidance inputs. The 3.0-times leverage target shows why common equity is not the entire enterprise claim. No purchase tax-lot record, historical multiple distribution or precise required reinvestment estimate is recovered. An existing owner must assess future economics rather than buy or sell solely because the stock is below an old price.

Evidence ledger from the January 7 release, covering the November quarter: total net sales were $2.223 billion, down 10% reported but 2% organically; reported EPS $2.88 and comparable EPS $3.06. Beer net sales of $2.0097 billion fell 1%; shipments fell 2.2%, depletions 3.0%, and beer operating income was $763.5 million. Beer operating margin was 38.0%. Modelo Especial depletions fell about 4%, Corona Extra nearly 9%, while Pacifico and Victoria grew over 15% and 13%. Year-to-date operating cash flow was $2.1 billion and free cash flow $1.45 billion. Annual comparable EPS guidance remained $11.30-$11.60; reported guidance was $9.72-$10.02. Guided capital spending was approximately $1.2 billion, including approximately $1.0 billion for Mexican beer operations. Comparable net leverage target was approximately 3.0 times. [1]

Valuation discipline: the calculations in this document are analyst arithmetic, not management targets or a Berkshire underwriting model. Earnings multiples, book multiples and cash-flow yields answer different questions and are not interchangeable. A low multiple offers no protection if normalized earning power has been overstated. Conversely, growth has value only after the incremental capital required to obtain it. No verified contemporary consensus forecast or complete historical multiple distribution is supplied; an exact intrinsic value should therefore not be reverse-engineered from a subsequent transaction.
