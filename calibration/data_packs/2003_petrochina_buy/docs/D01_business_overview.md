Title: Business economics and ownership claim

Date: 2003-04-01

PetroChina is an integrated Chinese producer, refiner and distributor, controlled by China National Petroleum Corporation. Upstream reserves and production support earnings, while refining and domestic product distribution add exposure to administered prices. A minority investor owns economic claims on the listed subsidiary, not the parent group's entire international portfolio. State control makes capital allocation and minority treatment material parts of the business appraisal. [Source above.]

Technical context: petroleum accounting profits depend on realized prices, volumes, royalties, taxes and the treatment of exploration and development costs. A reserve is not cash in the bank: it needs extraction, transport and often replacement investment. Distinguish upstream earnings from refining and chemical margins rather than assuming they move together. For owner earnings, separate maintenance needed to sustain economic production from expansion capital. Check whether reported free cash flow deducts preferred distributions and whether acquisitions are being called capital expenditure. Proved reserves, production and reserve life use different units and assumptions. A conservative analysis should explicitly test a lower realized-price case without claiming to forecast the commodity. [Source above; analytical accounting framework, not additional reported figures.]

Underwriting questions: identify the actual paying customer, repeat-purchase or replacement mechanism, and what prevents a capable competitor from taking the profit. Distinguish an enduring economic advantage from an asset that is merely expensive to build. The ownership claim being evaluated must match the economics described: operating control, common shares and a preferred claim confer different rights. Do not infer honest or capable management solely from a famous name or a long corporate history. Where direct customer-retention or pricing evidence is absent, leave that criterion uncertain rather than supply a generic franchise label. Record what observable deterioration would invalidate the business thesis before considering position size.

Evidence boundary: missing figures are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered for this digest. Analytical questions are not additional reported facts or claims about the investor's private thinking. Read the financial, risk and fund-context documents together; no individual accounting ratio supplies a complete investment conclusion.
