## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**1997 reporting cohort:** Long zeros offered no coupon and substantial interest-rate sensitivity. Exact inception date is unknown; year-end ownership does not prove that every security was first purchased in 1997. [1]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
“We will occasionally make an unconventional move when we believe the odds favor it.” He explicitly called this a “macro-based commitment,” not a certainty. **Following-year explanation.** [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Held $4.6 billion at amortized cost at December 1997, choosing long-duration Treasury zeros instead of cash equivalents. [1]

## What happened after
Falling rates generated $598.8 million of unrealized pretax gains in 1997. Ultimate sale proceeds and total return are unknown. [1]
