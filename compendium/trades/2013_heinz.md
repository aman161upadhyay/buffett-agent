## Buffett's thesis in his own words

The 2013 letter says “Berkshire never intends to sell a share of the company.” That is a February 2014, post-entry statement of intention, not a contractual guarantee or proof later business values could not deteriorate. [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did

Berkshire invested $8B in 9% preferred and $4.25B common in 2013; 3G ran operations. The 2015 Kraft combination created a different balance sheet and public issuer; see [Kraft Heinz](2015_kraft_heinz.md). [1,2]

## What happened after

Buffett’s February 2019 post-mortem distinguished Heinz from Kraft: he said Berkshire overpaid for Kraft, not Heinz. The two purchase decisions must not be merged into one generic failed-brand thesis. [3]
