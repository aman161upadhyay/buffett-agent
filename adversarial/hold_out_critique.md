# The sealed collapse: post-Buffett is a partial explanation, not an acquittal

## Verdict

**Partially correct as a distribution-shift warning; an excuse when offered as the principal causal account of 0/12 named, 1/12 masked.** Those are the scoreboard's grades, not twelve clean, identically defined experiments.

Three direct contradictions matter:

- `calibration/answer_keys/holdout/2025_googl_buy.json` records Buffett's “I initiated it,” with Abel approving. Calling the entire new universe non-Buffett is contradicted by the key.
- `2026_tmhc_acq.json` records $6.8bn equity/$8.5bn enterprise value and Abel. The scoreboard calls Taylor Morrison “deputy-sized.” That confuses a whole-company purchase with a small public-equity sleeve.
- `prompts/07_decision_memo.md` already says “for a deputy idea, BUY with deputy sizing.” `AGENT.md` operating rule 7 says apply deputy rules and “the direction is still graded.” Treating both deputy controls' PASS as success redefines the task after failure. In fact there is no separate executable deputy business-quality/valuation path before the hard fail.

There is nevertheless a real information problem: seven sealed keys have no investor-stated drivers, and several say selector unknown. Dated filings establish ownership changes, not decision rationale, private terms or Buffett's counterfactual choice. Neither zero accuracy nor an exculpatory story resolves that identification problem.

## Specific key/grader problems that distort the conclusion

1. `2025_googl_buy.json` has one positive criterion, one relative-ranking qualification and one AI-capex risk qualification, all retrospectively stated July 2026. Prompt 07 instructs BUY drivers to be positive and put risks elsewhere. A grader that searches only driver slots disadvantages exactly the qualified reasoning the key asks for.
2. `2025_oxychem_acq.json` explicitly warns that asset/team statements are two aspects of one announcement sentence, not independent evidence of three drivers. Reporting 0/3 does not mean three independent mechanisms were missed.
3. `2026_googl_add_q2.json` labels the later private discount causally unscorable. Counting it equally in overlap rewards hindsight.
4. `2026_cash_stance.json` accepts SELECTIVE, while prompt 10 demands exact stance equality. The score files give masked MATCH and named PARTIAL; the named note cites missing Abel attribution, although the stance output schema has no decider field. Fix the contract, not the market rule's cutoff to fit this one result.
5. `2026_stz_exit_q2.json` and `2026_unh_exit_q1.json` say “Do not ... ascribe the sale to Abel merely because he is CEO.” The scoreboard's broad “2026 actions are Abel's” goes beyond those keys.

## Legitimate v1.7 changes—without copying the sealed answers

- **Route by mandate and instrument before valuation.** Historical common-stock, structured-financing, whole-business and delegated cases already justify distinct branches. Require evidence for authority; allow unknown originator/approver separately. Do not hardcode healthcare or Alphabet.
- **Separate price inadequacy from information inadequacy.** An absent term sheet produces conditional terms and `not_actionable`, not a fictional fair-value estimate or a forecast that Berkshire will never buy.
- **Reconcile owner earnings and capital requirements.** Preserve fiscal period, share basis, tax basis, capex composition and uncertainty. Compute thresholds in code; do not infer maintenance capex as all AI expenditure or zero.
- **Test thesis deterioration before HOLD.** An intact brand is not an intact margin model. Calculate taxes only on gains with known lots or a range, not 21% of every sale. This follows calibration Apple and the uncalibrated KHC/textile contrasts, not a UNH-specific sell rule.
- **Score observable behavior separately from retrospectively explained motives.** Permit semantic stance equivalence prospectively; exclude unknowable deal terms from causal recall; grade unknown attribution as unknown.
- **Replace hard-coded spread labels with opportunity-set evidence.** Do not change −1 to −0.4 to make this hold-out match. Require the same policy to cope with missing CAPE, high inflation, changing earnings and no qualifying individual opportunities.

## Generalization test and firewall

Freeze a new specification **before** a separate curator assembles a balanced 24-case set from issuers/episodes not used in calibration, this review or prompt examples: six new common-equity decisions, four structured transactions, four whole-company opportunities, six existing reviews, four stance decisions. Include genuine declines and unchanged positions, not just buys/sells. Separate public-only forecasts from simulated private-desk underwritings with authenticated terms.

Run both v1.6 and v1.7, three seeds and two identity conditions: **288 decision runs** (24×2×3×2). This is a proposed budget, not an observed result. Pre-register action-only accuracy, explanation sufficiency, false-buy rate on fatal risks, exit recall, schema consistency and calibration of abstention. Success means improvement on balanced fresh data without increased unsafe BUYs—not recovery of these already-open twelve labels. Keep the original hold-out untouched and call any later replay a regression test.
