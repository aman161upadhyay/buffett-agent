Usage grows even as revenue declines. Currency explains part of the weakness: constant-currency revenue grew 2%, versus a 4% reported decline. If the main applications retain relevance, today's valuation could capitalize depressed profits rather than permanent decline. [S1; bull interpretation]

Third-quarter revenue was $27.714 billion, down 4%; operating income was $5.664 billion, down 46%; net income was $4.395 billion, down 52%; diluted EPS was $1.64, down 49%. Operating margin fell from 36% to 20%. Family monthly active people were 3.71 billion, up 4%, and daily active people 2.93 billion, up 4%. Facebook alone had 2.96 billion monthly active users. Ad impressions grew 17% while average price per ad fell 18%. Quarterly capital expenditure including finance-lease principal was $9.52 billion. Cash and marketable securities totaled $41.78 billion and long-term debt $9.92 billion. Buybacks consumed $6.55 billion. Headcount reached 87,314, up 28%. Management projected full-year capital expenditure of $32–33 billion and next-year capital expenditure of $34–39 billion; projected next-year expenses were $96–101 billion, including office-consolidation costs. These are forecasts, not completed investments or realized savings. [S1]

### Underwriting distinction

A results release contains at least three kinds of evidence: completed-period accounting, operating indicators and management expectations. They should not be merged. Customer activity can be healthier than reported earnings without proving that the earnings decline is temporary; conversely, a good quarter can coexist with emerging competitive or funding risks. The bullish reading of the latest report is set out above, but the financial bridge must preserve the less favorable observations as well. [Analytical interpretation of S1]

For a decision on this date, ask which part of the changed result is volume, price, mix, investment, financing or accounting timing. A recovery case should identify the actual variable that must improve. The record does not include a fresh post-cutoff quarter or an independent forecast proving that improvement. Reconcile the operating evidence below before treating a management target as an earnings base. [Analytical interpretation of S1]
