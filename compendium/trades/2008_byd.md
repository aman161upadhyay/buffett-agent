## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**September 2008:** BYD combined batteries, mobile-phone components and developing vehicle technology. Munger originated the idea and Sokol supplied crucial understanding; this was not Buffett independently forecasting EV adoption. [1,4]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
“Charlie Munger and ... Dave Sokol are smart guys, and they do understand it.” **April 2009 reported interview, after agreement but before closing.** Buffett also liked that Wang refused an initial proposed 25% investment. [4]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
MidAmerican agreed in September 2008; regulatory approval preceded subscription completion **July 30, 2009**. It acquired 225 million new H shares representing 9.89% of total enlarged capital, not 9.89% of the H-share class. Sokol's board role was separately described in the announcement. [1–3]

## What happened after
The stock appreciated substantially before legal completion. Paper gain is not realized return. Subsequent exits are outside this verified checkpoint. [3]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Attribution:** `Munger` represents origination; Sokol's diligence and Buffett's approval remain essential. [4]
- **Founder:** Technical problem solving and execution. [4]
- **Capabilities:** Batteries/clean transport, not a generic Chinese stock bet. [1]
- **Alignment:** Wang's reluctance to sell control impressed Buffett. [4]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: delegated competence and founder assessment explain the technology exception; do not train sole-Buffett authorship. [4]

## Public discussion
CNBC questioned simplistic cover-story framing and explicitly credited Munger. [3–4]
