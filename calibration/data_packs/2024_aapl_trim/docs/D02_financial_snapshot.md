Tangible operating capital is much smaller than cumulative earnings because customers and suppliers finance working capital and repurchases reduce book equity. A conservative gross-capital proxy, EBIT × 79% divided by year-end equity plus debt without subtracting cash, gives approximately 25%, 29%, 46%, 55% and 52% respectively. This is explicitly an analyst return proxy, not a reported ROTC; subtracting excess financial assets would raise it sharply and can make the denominator unstable.

The May 2 release reports fiscal second-quarter revenue of $90.8 billion, down 4%, and diluted EPS of $1.53. Services reached a record. The board authorized an additional $110 billion in repurchases and raised the quarterly dividend to $0.25 per share. A repurchase authorization is not an executed purchase and does not establish intrinsic value at every trading price. [Source above.]

Technical context: a consumer ecosystem may monetize both a device and recurring services, but they are linked rather than independent annuities. Customer retention, replacement frequency, pricing and the required cost of innovation determine owner economics. Separate hardware sales, installed-base activity and services revenue, avoiding double-counting value from the same customer relationship. Cash returned through buybacks must be compared with share issuance and purchase price; authorization is not execution. Foreign-held cash, taxes and operating liquidity needs affect deployable excess capital. Use one split basis for price, shares and EPS, since mismatched adjustments can make an otherwise correct earnings multiple meaningless. [Source above; analytical framework.]

Accounting discipline: the figures stated above are reported observations unless expressly labeled arithmetic. A period-end balance is not a flow and a market mark is not purchase cost. Do not mix fiscal years, currencies, share bases or enterprise and equity values. To construct owner earnings, begin with the relevant reported profit, reconcile noncash items and subtract the investment required to maintain economic position. Changes in working capital and recurring compensation remain economic costs even when a convenient adjusted metric excludes them. If maintenance investment, leverage or the proper share denominator is missing, state that the yield cannot be completed rather than present false precision.

Evidence boundary: the historical record uses a consistent split basis. Return proxies are denominator-explicit calculations, not company guidance or a measurement of maintenance capital expenditure.
