### Valuation cross-check

D04's three dated peer observations span roughly 32x–59x trailing GAAP P/E versus the target's 23.7x at its explicitly dated $81.50 checkpoint. This is not an arbitrage approval probability: fiscal periods, revenue deferrals, acquisition accounting and compensation differ. The target's 3.61x common book and 8.64x tangible book further demonstrate that book is not a stand-alone downside floor. Cash realization after development and compensation matters more than a superficial sector multiple.

Shares are share counts rather than gaming market shares. The platform conglomerate is an opportunity-cost comparator, not a pure publisher. Revenue deferrals and game release timing make a single-quarter margin a poor perpetual assumption.

Compare with other announced cash mergers by financing certainty, regulatory complexity, termination rights, outside date and downside if the deal breaks. Do not compare the arbitrage yield directly with a perpetual franchise earnings yield. Microsoft's existing console and distribution interests make competition review economically meaningful. Target operating results can change break value even when the promised $95 cash consideration is fixed. [Source above.]

Technical context: merger arbitrage is a contingent cash-flow problem. Separate the promised consideration, financing, approvals, termination rights, outside date and possible remedies. Gross spread is not expected return: completion probability, time and the break-price loss all matter. A bidder's ability to pay does not settle regulatory approval. Stand-alone valuation is important even if the intended holding period ends at closing, because the target remains a public business if the transaction fails. Do not treat an original pre-announcement stock purchase and a post-announcement workout as the same investment thesis merely because they use the same ticker. [Source above; analytical framework.]

Comparison discipline: compare businesses on the source of their cash and capital required to earn it, rather than treating the nearest ticker as an exact substitute. Normalize financing and accounting differences before interpreting relative multiples. A peer can have a better franchise at an unattractive price. Relative cheapness alone cannot prove absolute value. The numeric table is an operating comparison, not a model of merger-completion probability.

Evidence boundary: business quality, current quarterly performance and transaction risk are separate dimensions. Approximate share counts and calculated margins do not establish private investor intent.
