Title: A profitable record with a broken earnings bridge
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176625000022/a2024q4exhibit991.htm ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176625000123/a2025q1exhibit991.htm ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176625000134/exhibit991.htm ; https://www.unitedhealthgroup.com/newsroom/2025/2025-05-14-response-may-14-wsj-article.html
Date: 2025-06-30

The pre-existing record is substantial: 2024 revenue increased 8%, with operating earnings exceeding $32 billion. The difference between $15.51 reported EPS and $27.66 adjusted EPS is material. Cyberattack response and South American effects are among the adjustments; it would be misleading to call the adjusted number clean cash income without examining those costs. Operating cash flow of $24.2 billion is real reported cash generation but includes claims, receivable and payment timing. [1]

First-quarter EPS improved on a reported basis, yet the company reduced its annual expectation because late-quarter care activity exceeded plans. The earnings record therefore supports economic scale, not confidence in the old forecast. No maintenance-capital expenditure split, insurer statutory-capital roll-forward or full subsidiary debt schedule has been recovered in this evidence set. These omissions prevent a precise owner-earnings reconstruction and a confident measure of freely distributable cash at the parent. Revenue growth alone does not bridge that gap. [1,2]

Evidence ledger, with dollar amounts in billions unless stated otherwise: the January 16 release reports 2024 revenue of $400.3, versus $371.6 in 2023, operating earnings of $32.3, operating cash flow of $24.2, GAAP EPS of $15.51 and adjusted EPS of $27.66. The April 17 release reports first-quarter revenue of $109.6, up $9.8, GAAP EPS of $6.85 and adjusted EPS of $7.20. UnitedHealthcare added 780,000 consumers year to date. April's reduced adjusted annual EPS range was $26.00-$26.50; the May leadership announcement suspended the outlook, so that range is no longer current guidance. The May statement disputes the press characterization of a supposed criminal investigation. These are issuer statements, not independent legal findings. [1-4]

Accounting discipline: all dollar amounts in this document retain the period and basis given in the evidence ledger. Revenue is not owner earnings; neither adjusted profit nor operating cash flow alone measures distributable cash. Maintenance investment, working capital, compensation dilution and financing claims must be considered separately. A quarter is not a full economic cycle. Where a stand-alone balance sheet, maintenance-capital split or historical consensus series is absent, it is unavailable rather than assumed to be zero or inferred from a later filing.
