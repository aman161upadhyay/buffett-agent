Title: A different multiple after the earnings reset
Source: https://www.unitedhealthgroup.com/content/dam/UHG/PDF/investors/2025/unh-reports-third-quarter-2025-results-and-raises-full-year-2025-earnings-outlook.pdf ; https://www.unitedhealthgroup.com/newsroom/2025/2025-07-24-uhg-responds-to-doj-investigation.html ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176625000022/a2024q4exhibit991.htm ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176625000123/a2025q1exhibit991.htm
Date: 2025-12-31

The December 31 unadjusted stock close was $330.11. Against management's annual adjusted EPS floor of $16.25, that implies 20.31 times; against the $14.90 GAAP floor, approximately 22.16 times. The floors are not point forecasts, so those figures describe multiples at the minimum indicated earnings, not definitive forward valuations. The stock-price observation is a historical daily close, not a Berkshire execution price. [1; historical price below]

Using old adjusted earnings of $27.66 instead would make the multiple look about 11.93 times, but would assume a recovery not established in the current release. That gap is exactly what an existing owner needs to resolve. Operating cash flow does not by itself settle normalized profitability or subsidiary capital needs. No independently recovered December consensus bridge, purchase tax-lot basis or precise permanent-loss probability is available. The relevant opportunity cost is the future return on today's value, not an attempt to recover an unknown original purchase price. [1,3]

Evidence ledger: the October 28 release reports third-quarter revenue of $113.2 billion, up 12%; operating earnings of $4.3 billion, down approximately 50% year on year; net margin of 2.1%; GAAP EPS of $2.59; adjusted EPS of $2.92; and operating cash flow of $5.9 billion. Management increased its annual floor to $14.90 GAAP EPS and $16.25 adjusted EPS. That floor is substantially below April's superseded $26.00-$26.50 adjusted range and 2024 adjusted EPS of $27.66. On July 24 the company said it was complying with formal criminal and civil Department of Justice requests and expressed confidence in its practices. No legal settlement or completed annual financial result is assumed. [1-4]

Valuation discipline: the calculations in this document are analyst arithmetic, not management targets or a Berkshire underwriting model. Earnings multiples, book multiples and cash-flow yields answer different questions and are not interchangeable. A low multiple offers no protection if normalized earning power has been overstated. Conversely, growth has value only after the incremental capital required to obtain it. No verified contemporary consensus forecast or complete historical multiple distribution is supplied; an exact intrinsic value should therefore not be reverse-engineered from a subsequent transaction.
