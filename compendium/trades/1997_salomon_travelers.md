## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**1997:** Berkshire's Salomon investment predated the 1991 crisis. The Travelers merger supplied a corporate transaction rather than an ordinary open-market sale of an unchanged security. [1]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
The transaction “finally rewarded its long-suffering shareholders.” **February 1998 retrospective:** Buffett credited Deryck Maughan and Bob Denham both for saving Salomon in 1991 and restoring enough vitality to make it attractive to Travelers. An original merger-acceptance valuation memorandum remains unknown. [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Participated as a Salomon holder in its 1997 merger into Travelers. Exact election terms, settlement day and subsequent Travelers disposition require merger documents and subsidiary-level reconciliation. [1]

Its table lists 23,733,198 Travelers shares, $604.4 million **tax-basis cost** and $1.2786 billion market value at December 1997. Those are a successor-holding checkpoint, not final total Salomon realized proceeds. [1]

## What happened after
