Title: Share gains while absolute volumes decline
Source: https://ml.globenewswire.com/Resource/Download/3ef69300-da43-4e07-9468-985051ddf0ed
Date: 2026-03-31

The January release presents a challenging environment rather than an outright collapse of the portfolio. Beer depletions fell 3%; Modelo Especial and Corona Extra declined, while Pacifico and Victoria grew strongly. Pricing partly offset shipment weakness. The margin improved 10 basis points to 38%, with favorable pricing and lower depreciation offset by tariff-related aluminum costs and unfavorable fixed-cost absorption. [1]

Total sales fell 10% reported, a decline amplified by wine divestitures; organic sales fell 2%. Comparable annual EPS guidance remained $11.30-$11.60, while the reported range was updated to $9.72-$10.02. This distinction matters because a headline about changed GAAP guidance is not the same as a fresh reduction in comparable guidance. The company continued repurchases and declared a $1.02 quarterly dividend. No subsequent annual result is available at March 31. A current assessment must weigh strong relative brand performance against weaker absolute consumption and the investment needed to sustain the franchise. [1]

Evidence ledger from the January 7 release, covering the November quarter: total net sales were $2.223 billion, down 10% reported but 2% organically; reported EPS $2.88 and comparable EPS $3.06. Beer net sales of $2.0097 billion fell 1%; shipments fell 2.2%, depletions 3.0%, and beer operating income was $763.5 million. Beer operating margin was 38.0%. Modelo Especial depletions fell about 4%, Corona Extra nearly 9%, while Pacifico and Victoria grew over 15% and 13%. Year-to-date operating cash flow was $2.1 billion and free cash flow $1.45 billion. Annual comparable EPS guidance remained $11.30-$11.60; reported guidance was $9.72-$10.02. Guided capital spending was approximately $1.2 billion, including approximately $1.0 billion for Mexican beer operations. Comparable net leverage target was approximately 3.0 times. [1]

Interpretation: the issuer's latest released quarter is the information boundary, not the quarter containing this assessment date. Forward guidance is management's expectation, not a realized result. Growth percentages must be matched to the stated comparison period and accounting basis. Where adjusted measures differ from GAAP, both are retained rather than mixing the favorable numerator from one with the denominator from the other. A favorable quarter does not settle the durability question; the operational causes and cash conversion still require scrutiny.
