The small 2006 discontinued-operation gain explains the difference between continuing and total EPS. High book ROE partly reflects a repurchase-reduced equity denominator.

The 2010 annual report reports revenue of approximately $99.87 billion and net income of $14.833 billion. Operating cash flow was approximately $19.549 billion; capital expenditure about $4.185 billion. The mechanical difference is $15.364 billion before deciding how financing receivables, acquisitions and working-capital movements should be treated. It is not a company-independent estimate of sustainable owner earnings. [Source above.]

Technical context: enterprise technology can contain long contractual relationships and recurring maintenance but still require persistent innovation. Customer switching costs are economic only if the supplier keeps delivering acceptable value. Distinguish backlog from cash, software licenses from support, and services revenue from labor-adjusted profit. Financing receivables can distort industrial cash-flow comparisons. Research expenditure, acquisitions, pension contributions and stock compensation belong in a conservative assessment of owner economics. A share repurchase increases continuing ownership only at a cost, and is beneficial only relative to the value acquired. Per-share growth produced by fewer shares must be reconciled with total earnings and capital spent. [Source above; analytical framework.]

Accounting discipline: the figures stated above are reported observations unless expressly labeled arithmetic. A period-end balance is not a flow and a market mark is not purchase cost. Do not mix fiscal years, currencies, share bases or enterprise and equity values. To construct owner earnings, begin with the relevant reported profit, reconcile noncash items and subtract the investment required to maintain economic position. Changes in working capital and recurring compensation remain economic costs even when a convenient adjusted metric excludes them. If maintenance investment, leverage or the proper share denominator is missing, state that the yield cannot be completed rather than present false precision.

Evidence boundary: missing figures are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered for this digest. Analytical questions are not additional reported facts or claims about the investor's private thinking. Read the financial, risk and fund-context documents together; no individual accounting ratio supplies a complete investment conclusion.
