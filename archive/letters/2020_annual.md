## Process rules and decision explanations

- Opening: Precision Castparts write-down is a capital-allocation error; Buffett paid too much because normalized earning power was overestimated. Do not assign the entire economic error to the pandemic.
- The Family Jewels and How We Increase Your Share of These Gems: $24.7bn Berkshire buybacks increased continuing owners’ interest without another contribution.
- Same section: Apple repurchases plus Berkshire repurchases increase indirect ownership. A partial sale can coexist with higher percentage ownership.
- Same section: Buffett’s own Apple purchase chronology starts late 2016; initial Berkshire buying by a deputy is distinct.
- A Tale of Two Cities: NFM and National Indemnity show relationship/manager evaluation in acquisitions. This is historical reflection, not current due-diligence permission to skip verification.
