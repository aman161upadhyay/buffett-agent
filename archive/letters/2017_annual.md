# 2017 Berkshire annual letter

**Information date:** 2018-02-24 (month-only where precise public release day was not independently established). **Era:** D. **Author:** Buffett. **Primary source:** [letter](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2017ltr.pdf). All rules below cite sections of this source; section names are used rather than fabricated PDF page numbers. Annual-period labels are not publication dates.

## Process rules and decision explanations

- Acquisitions: unusually rich prices prevented most stand-alone acquisitions; leverage and adjusted-earnings presentations should not lower the hurdle.
- Same section: Pilot initial 38.6% interest was a staged acquisition, not immediate full management control. Abel’s later criticism is separately dated.
- Insurance: large AIG long-tail reinsurance brought float and long-duration obligations; premium intake is not immediate economic profit.
- The Bet is Over: the decision to swap low-yield Treasury collateral for Berkshire stock reflects comparative valuation, not a change to the original fund-selection wager.
- Bolt-ons at Shaw, HomeServices and Clayton show decentralized deployment despite few large parent acquisitions.

## Verbatim evidence anchors

- **Acquisitions; Acquisition of a 38.6% partnership interest in Pilot Flying J.** “We were able to make one sensible stand-alone purchase last year, a 38.6% partnership interest in Pilot Flying J (“PFJ”).”
- **Insurance; Reinsuring up to $20 billion of long-tail losses for AIG.** “Our 2017 volume was boosted by a huge deal in which we reinsured up to $20 billion of long-tail losses that AIG had incurred.”
- **“The Bet” is Over and Has Delivered an Unforeseen Investment Lesson; Selling Treasury bonds to purchase 11,200 Berkshire “B” shares due to a valuation mismatch.** “In November 2012, as we were considering all this, the cash return from dividends on the S&P 500 was 21⁄2% annually, about triple the yield on our U.S. Treasury bond.”
- **Acquisitions; Acquisition of U.S. Floors by Shaw Industries.** “Shaw Industries, our floor coverings business, acquired U.S. Floors (“USF”), a rapidly growing distributor of luxury vinyl tile. USF’s managers, Piet Dossche and Philippe Erramuzpe, came out of the gate fast”
