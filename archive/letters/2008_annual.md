# 2008 Berkshire annual letter

**Information date:** 2009-02-28 (month-only where precise public release day was not independently established). **Era:** C. **Author:** Buffett. **Primary source:** [letter](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2008ltr.pdf). All rules below cite sections of this source; section names are used rather than fabricated PDF page numbers. Annual-period labels are not publication dates.

## Process rules and decision explanations

- Investments: Goldman, GE and Wrigley purchases combined fixed-income return with equity participation. Distinguish negotiated liquidity financing from common-stock market timing.
- Same section: ConocoPhillips bought near peak oil/gas prices; Buffett explicitly failed to anticipate the commodity-price decline.
- Same section: Irish banks appeared cheap but were heavily written down. Low price is not proof the underlying equity is solvent.
- Derivatives: contractual settlement dates, premiums, collateral and counterparty terms explain why Berkshire can write some derivatives while criticizing systemic derivatives risk.
- Liquidity discussion: preserve ability to meet obligations without relying on hostile capital markets; 2008 personal-account op-ed is a separate vehicle.

## Verbatim evidence anchors

- **Investments; Purchase of ConocoPhillips stock** “I bought a large amount of ConocoPhillips stock when oil and gas prices were near their peak. I in no way anticipated the dramatic fall”
- **Investments; Purchase of shares in two Irish banks** “During 2008, I spent $244 million for shares of two Irish banks that appeared cheap to me. At yearend we wrote these holdings down to market”
- **Investments; Purchase of fixed-income securities in Wrigley, Goldman Sachs, and General Electric** “we made purchases totaling $14.5 billion in fixed-income securities issued by Wrigley, Goldman Sachs and General Electric. We very much like these commitments, which carry high current”

## Attribution and point-in-time boundary

Buffett is the source author, but an acquired subsidiary’s bolt-on or operating action is not automatically his personally originated investment. Individual decider unknown where not identified. Historical narratives are available only from this information date. A section discussing earlier purchases is retrospective; do not load its later performance, confession or corrected valuation into the original entry replay.
