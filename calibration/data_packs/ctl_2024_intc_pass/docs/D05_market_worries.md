Title: The strongest contemporaneous bear case
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/50863/000005086324000122/q224_earningsrelease.htm; https://www.cnbc.com/2024/08/02/intel-share-plunge-drags-down-global-chip-stocks-from-tsmc-to-samsung.html
Date: 2024-08-02

The company is losing money despite substantial scale and continues spending heavily to regain competitiveness. A 0.2% adjusted operating margin offers little cushion. Dividend suspension indicates cash has higher priority than shareholder distributions, while next-quarter guidance remains loss-making. Cutting headcount and spending can either improve returns or undermine the research and yield improvements essential to the thesis. A factory's accounting cost is not its liquidation value; technical obsolescence and low utilization can destroy book value. [S1; analytical inference]

The August 2 close was $21.48, down approximately 26% in one session. An ordinary trailing earnings multiple is not meaningful against the reported loss. Annualizing two cents of adjusted quarterly EPS would produce eight cents and a mechanical 268.5 times multiple; that is not normalized valuation. A genuine bull instead values recoverable earning power, foundry options and assets after debt and ongoing cash needs. A low stock price and a suspended dividend are not themselves a margin of safety. Gross and net capex must not be mixed when estimating owner cash. [S1,S2; calculations]

### Dated operating baseline

Second-quarter revenue was $12.8 billion, down 1%. GAAP net loss attributable to Intel was $1.6 billion and EPS negative $0.38; non-GAAP EPS was positive $0.02. GAAP gross margin was 35.4%, versus 35.8%, while non-GAAP gross margin was 38.7%, versus 39.8%. GAAP operating margin was negative 15.3%; adjusted operating margin was 0.2%. Operating cash flow was $2.3 billion and dividends paid $0.5 billion. The company guided next-quarter revenue to $12.5–13.5 billion, GAAP EPS to negative $0.24 and adjusted EPS to negative $0.03. It announced more than $10 billion of operating and capital spending reductions against prior plans, including headcount reductions exceeding 15%. Gross capex guidance was $25–27 billion for the year, while net capital spending after partner contributions and offsets was $11–13 billion. Dividend suspension begins in the fourth quarter. [S1]

### Underwriting distinction

This objection is intended to be capable of defeating the affirmative case. Distinguish a reversible mark-to-market loss from permanent impairment, and a temporary earnings interruption from a permanently more capital-hungry business. The investor must survive the adverse scenario without relying on the next buyer, a friendly regulator or unrestricted external financing. A long holding period cannot restore capital that has been diluted away or consumed by obligations senior to the purchased security. [Analytical interpretation of S1]

Observable thesis-breakers include customer economics deteriorating despite continued spending, new funding coming only on punitive terms, or reported cash failing to reconcile to recurring obligations. These are analytical monitoring conditions, not claims that such outcomes have occurred. The record below supplies the current scale of exposure against which to test them. No later failure, recovery or management admission is used to strengthen the bear case. [Analytical interpretation of S1]
