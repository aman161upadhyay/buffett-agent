## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**February 2009:** Harley sought debt financing amid scarce credit. Notes supporting financing needs should not be represented as buying distressed motorcycles or Harley common equity. [1]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
Unknown: no direct original purchase quotation recovered. The 15% rate is documented; calling it attractive is research interpretation, not a fabricated Buffett statement. [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Agreed $300 million alongside another $300 million from Davis Selected Advisers, for $600 million total. Notes were senior unsecured. **Correction:** Berkshire did not supply the entire issuance, and “senior” does not mean secured. [1]
