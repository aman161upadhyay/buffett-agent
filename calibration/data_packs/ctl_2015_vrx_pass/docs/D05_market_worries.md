Title: The strongest contemporaneous bear case

Date: 2015-03-02

Repeated acquisitions can replace internally developed assets while amortization and recurring integration spending are excluded from headline EPS. The fourth-quarter operating-cash comparison benefits from an investment transaction; adjusted cash growth is much slower than GAAP cash growth. A leveraged acquisition model depends on credit-market access and the price of the next target. Drugs can lose exclusivity and payers can resist price increases. The February presentation's inability to reconcile forward non-GAAP guidance makes a cash-EPS multiple less informative than it first appears. No later distribution scandal or restatement is assumed. [S1,S2; analytical inference]

The March 2 close and a reliable last-preceding-session quote were not recovered from contemporaneous sources, so no exact entry multiple is invented. The reported $2.58 cash EPS annualizes to $10.32 but excludes costs and is not a forecast. Quarterly adjusted operating cash flow of $624 million is not a substitute for annual owner earnings or financing capacity after Salix. Enterprise value must include acquisition debt and distinguish announced transactions from completed balance-sheet assets. Valuation upside from cost savings should be probability-weighted and not counted twice in both the earnings base and growth multiple. [S1,S2; calculations]

### Dated operating baseline

The February 23 presentation reports fourth-quarter revenue of $2.3 billion versus $2.1 billion, up 10%; management's cash EPS was $2.58 versus $2.15, up 20%. GAAP operating cash flow was $816 million versus $280 million. That cash figure included a $287 million net Allergan transaction gain; management's adjusted operating cash flow was $624 million versus $607 million, only 3% higher. Restructuring and integration costs were $47 million. Reported same-store organic sales growth was 16%; Bausch + Lomb organic growth was 8% for the quarter and 11% for the year. The presentation explicitly warns that forward cash-EPS and adjusted-cash-flow guidance is not reconciled to GAAP because excluded items are difficult to forecast. The contemplated Salix transaction and financing are additional commitments, not part of the historical quarter's recurring cash earnings. Cash EPS is a management-defined earnings measure, not distributable owner cash. [S1]

### Underwriting distinction

This objection is intended to be capable of defeating the affirmative case. Distinguish a reversible mark-to-market loss from permanent impairment, and a temporary earnings interruption from a permanently more capital-hungry business. The investor must survive the adverse scenario without relying on the next buyer, a friendly regulator or unrestricted external financing. A long holding period cannot restore capital that has been diluted away or consumed by obligations senior to the purchased security. [Analytical interpretation of S1]

Observable thesis-breakers include customer economics deteriorating despite continued spending, new funding coming only on punitive terms, or reported cash failing to reconcile to recurring obligations. These are analytical monitoring conditions, not claims that such outcomes have occurred. The record below supplies the current scale of exposure against which to test them. No later failure, recovery or management admission is used to strengthen the bear case. [Analytical interpretation of S1]
