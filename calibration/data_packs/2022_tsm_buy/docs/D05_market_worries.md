Title: Adverse case and permanent-loss risks
Source: https://investor.tsmc.com/chinese/encrypt/files/encrypt_file/reports/2022-10/a924f47cae2629ecb5f8801629cf7fb8773e5444/3Q22EarningsRelease.pdf (published 2022-10-13)
Date: 2022-11-14

The strongest bear case combines demand cyclicality with a non-diversifiable geopolitical interruption to Taiwan production. Even a technically superior foundry may be unable to ship during a blockade or conflict. Overseas expansion can require more capital and lower returns. Customer concentration and export controls create additional risks. A modest earnings multiple cannot compensate for every low-frequency, high-severity scenario. [Source above.]

Technical context: foundry manufacturing has scale and process barriers but requires continual expensive reinvestment. Gross margin is not owner earnings after fabs, equipment, research and working capital. Distinguish leading-edge from mature processes, production location from customer location, and booked revenue from durable through-cycle utilization. A technically excellent company can have concentration risks outside management control. Overseas facilities require time, qualification and capital; they are not immediately interchangeable with an existing production cluster. An ADR may represent several ordinary shares, so EPS and price must use the same unit. A low multiple on a peak quarter is not automatically conservative. [Source above; analytical framework.]

Risk discipline: the adverse cases above are analyst stress tests grounded in the disclosed business and current circumstances, not quotations attributed to an unnamed contemporary short seller. No authenticated bearish price target was recovered. Separate a temporary quotation decline from permanent impairment of earning power or of the investor's claim. Ask whether the issuer can fund necessary operations without assuming favorable capital markets. Identify which changes are within management control and which are not. A large cash balance at the investor does not cure an issuer's leverage, a faulty operating thesis or an excessive purchase price. Do not soften a serious downside merely because an admired investor might be interested.

Evidence boundary: missing figures are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered for this digest. Analytical questions are not additional reported facts or claims about the investor's private thinking. Read the financial, risk and fund-context documents together; no individual accounting ratio supplies a complete investment conclusion.
