Title: An advertising franchise is not a uniform technology basket
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204425000056/googexhibit991q22025.htm ; https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/department-justice-wins-significant-remedies-against-google ; https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/LSB/PDF/LSB11362/LSB11362.1.pdf
Date: 2025-09-30

The useful peer distinctions are between advertising-funded discovery, social engagement, enterprise software and infrastructure computing. Meta competes for advertising budgets but does not replicate search intent. Microsoft competes in enterprise software, Cloud and AI, while Amazon's computing and commerce mix differs. These are economic comparisons, not synchronized peer-multiple observations. No date-matched enterprise-value table is available in the recovered evidence. [1,2]

Within Alphabet, Services produces most profit, Cloud grows faster and Other Bets consumes cash. Treating the whole group as a high-growth Cloud company would misweight the business. Conversely, ignoring Cloud would miss a profitable second engine. The proper moat test concerns advertiser outcomes, customer switching and the full costs of distribution and computing. September's court-ordered access and contractual restrictions directly challenge distribution advantages; they do not tell the analyst that every rival can instantly reproduce the entire product or advertiser ecosystem. [1-3]

Evidence ledger: the July 23 release reports second-quarter revenue of $96.428 billion, up 14%; operating income of $31.271 billion; net income of $28.196 billion; and diluted EPS of $2.31. Services revenue was $82.543 billion, Cloud $13.624 billion and Other Bets $373 million. Services operating income was $33.063 billion, Cloud $2.826 billion, Other Bets a $1.246 billion loss and central activities a $3.372 billion cost. Cash plus marketable securities totaled $95.148 billion, versus $23.607 billion long-term debt. Trailing operating cash flow was $133.708 billion, capital expenditures $66.980 billion and free cash flow $66.728 billion. Management increased planned annual capital spending to approximately $85 billion. September court remedies prohibit specified exclusivity arrangements and require access to certain data/services. [1-3]

Comparison discipline: industry membership does not make two businesses economically identical. Geography, customer mix, contract duration, capital intensity, leverage and accounting adjustments can dominate headline growth comparisons. No synchronized peer-price and consensus-earnings dataset was recovered for this cutoff; relative cheapness is therefore not asserted numerically. The useful comparison is between sources of durable return and exposure to common pressures. A competitor's weakness is not automatically this company's moat, and an industry recovery is not required to be uniform across participants.
