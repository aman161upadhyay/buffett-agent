## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: 2019-02-20.** Berkshire already held Kraft Heinz. This file’s 2019 year is disclosure/post-mortem year; the underlying impairment relates to 2018 accounts. It is not an additional common-share purchase. [1,2]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words

On February 25 Buffett told CNBC: “We overpaid for Kraft.” He specifically did not say Berkshire overpaid for Heinz. The short quotation is retrospective, not a thesis that existed at the 2015 decision. [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did

KHC announced $15.4B of goodwill/intangible impairments and a dividend reduction; Berkshire’s annual letter described roughly $3B non-cash loss arising almost entirely from its KHC interest. These are different reporting entities, so do not add them as Berkshire losses. [1,2]

## What happened after

Berkshire continued to hold the stock in the SEC series. The June 2026 report identifies a separate $5B KHC impairment recognized in Q2 2025, not a restatement of the 2019 admission. [3]
