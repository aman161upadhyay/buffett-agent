Title: The evidence set closes before the next earnings release
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204425000056/googexhibit991q22025.htm ; https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/department-justice-wins-significant-remedies-against-google ; https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/LSB/PDF/LSB11362/LSB11362.1.pdf
Date: 2025-09-30

July 23: second-quarter results show continued growth, a larger capital budget and meaningful Cloud profit. September 2: the court's search remedies become public. September 10: the CRS analysis explains why behavioral restrictions, rather than the requested immediate structural divestitures, were adopted. September 30: the Class A stock closes at $243.10 after a strong quarter. Those publication dates are the relevant sequence. [1-3; price source below]

There is no third-quarter earnings release in the information set. Quarter-end revenue, newly completed investment totals, private capital discussions and any particular institutional investor's stock selection are not facts available merely because the calendar quarter has ended. The case must be evaluated from the latest published economics and the legal situation already known. The timeline supplies no future announcement as a catalyst and makes no claim that a favorable court outcome fully insulates the business from AI competition. Current price must be assessed separately from an unknown earlier accumulation price.

Evidence ledger: the July 23 release reports second-quarter revenue of $96.428 billion, up 14%; operating income of $31.271 billion; net income of $28.196 billion; and diluted EPS of $2.31. Services revenue was $82.543 billion, Cloud $13.624 billion and Other Bets $373 million. Services operating income was $33.063 billion, Cloud $2.826 billion, Other Bets a $1.246 billion loss and central activities a $3.372 billion cost. Cash plus marketable securities totaled $95.148 billion, versus $23.607 billion long-term debt. Trailing operating cash flow was $133.708 billion, capital expenditures $66.980 billion and free cash flow $66.728 billion. Management increased planned annual capital spending to approximately $85 billion. September court remedies prohibit specified exclusivity arrangements and require access to certain data/services. [1-3]

Timing discipline: the chronology separates publication dates from the economic periods discussed. A result for an earlier quarter only becomes an input when released. The market close is an observation available on the stated assessment date, not evidence of a particular investor's execution. This timeline includes no presumed private conversations or unannounced agreement. There is no catalyst completion forecast here: the analyst must decide whether the available business evidence is sufficient without relying on a future disclosure to reveal someone else's decision.
