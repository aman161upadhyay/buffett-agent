## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**2005:** Berkshire established the brewer position. Exact entry multiple and a Buffett-specific original rationale were not recovered. The later InBev bid was not the documented purchase thesis. [1–2]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
**Unknown at entry.** The annual letter identifies the new position but does not supply a recovered standalone brand/valuation quotation. [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Held a substantial position by 2005. CNBC later reported major sales during June 2008 before deal certainty, with 13.845 million shares remaining September 30. Those shares converted into $969.15 million cash at the $70 merger price on November 18. [1–2]

## What happened after
The takeover ended the holding. Contemporary estimates using the earlier full share count overstate closing proceeds if they ignore the prior sales. Do not report all merger cash as profit. [2]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Entry gap:** Consumer-brand logic is plausible but not authenticated as Buffett's exact thesis. [1]
- **Catalyst:** InBev transaction monetized the residual holding. [2]
- **Sequence:** Earlier disposals and closing consideration must both be counted. [2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
