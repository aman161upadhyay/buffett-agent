## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**2008:** Buffett bought shares of two Irish banks he believed cheap. The cited letter does not identify them; naming specific issuers without further evidence would invent precision. [1]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
The banks “appeared cheap to me.” He called these mistakes “unforced errors.” **February 2009 post-mortem**, not contemporaneous purchase advocacy. [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Spent $244 million during 2008; wrote the positions down to $27 million at year-end. The paired disclosure is retained as one aggregate case because issuer identities and separate allocations remain unknown. [1]

## What happened after
The 89% loss was a year-end mark, with further declines already occurring by the letter date. Final realized disposition and recovery unknown. [1]
