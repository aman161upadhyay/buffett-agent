Title: March growth and a tariff-qualified outlook
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019324000123/aapl-20240928.htm ; https://www.apple.com/newsroom/pdfs/fy2025-q2/FY25_Q2_Consolidated_Financial_Statements.pdf ; https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/05/apple-reports-second-quarter-results/ ; https://www.cnbc.com/2025/05/01/apple-has-managed-tariffs-so-far-says-tough-to-predict-beyond-june.html
Date: 2025-06-30

The May 1 release reports quarterly revenue growth of 5% and EPS growth of 8%. Services growth was double digit, and the CFO attributed capital returns to cash generation and the installed base. Operating cash flow for the quarter was approximately $24 billion; shareholder returns approximately $29 billion. First-half cash flow is a different period and must not be mixed into that quarterly comparison. [2,3]

The board authorized up to $100 billion additional common-share repurchases and increased the dividend 4%. An authorization is not an obligation to buy a fixed amount at any price. CNBC's May 1 reporting of Cook's remarks puts approximately $900 million of incremental tariffs into the June-quarter expectation, assuming policy is unchanged. Management said conditions beyond that period were difficult to predict. No June-quarter results are yet public. The same release therefore contains evidence of durable cash production and a concrete near-term cost cloud, not a promise that margins are immune to trade policy. [3,4]

Evidence ledger: fiscal revenues in 2022, 2023 and 2024 were $394.328 billion, $383.285 billion and $391.035 billion. Fiscal 2024 diluted EPS was $6.08. In the March quarter, revenue was $95.359 billion, operating income $29.589 billion, net income $24.780 billion and diluted EPS $1.65. Products supplied $68.714 billion sales and Services $26.645 billion. First-half operating cash flow was $53.887 billion and capital spending $6.011 billion. March cash and marketable securities totaled $132.922 billion; commercial paper and term debt totaled $98.186 billion. The board authorized another $100 billion repurchase program and a $0.26 quarterly dividend. Cook estimated about $900 million additional tariff costs for the June quarter, conditional on unchanged policy. [1-4]

Interpretation: the issuer's latest released quarter is the information boundary, not the quarter containing this assessment date. Forward guidance is management's expectation, not a realized result. Growth percentages must be matched to the stated comparison period and accounting basis. Where adjusted measures differ from GAAP, both are retained rather than mixing the favorable numerator from one with the denominator from the other. A favorable quarter does not settle the durability question; the operational causes and cash conversion still require scrutiny.
