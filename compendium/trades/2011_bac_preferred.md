## Buffett's thesis in his own words

The 2011 letter: “Brian Moynihan is nurturing a huge and attractive underlying business that will endure.” The warrants could be exercised before September 2, 2021; the preferred produced $300M annual distributions on $5B. [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did

2011: $5B preferred and warrants. 2017: used the preferred as consideration when exercising the warrants; see [conversion](2017_bac_conversion.md). Preferred and common exposures are not additive $10B cash investments. [1,2]

## What happened after

The exercise transformed a credit-like income stream plus option into 700M common shares. Market value on conversion exceeded original funding, but it was not a realized sale profit. [2,3]
