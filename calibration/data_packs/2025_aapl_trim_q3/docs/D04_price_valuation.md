Title: Price versus operating earning power and tax cost
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019324000123/aapl-20240928.htm ; https://www.apple.com/newsroom/pdfs/fy2025-q2/FY25_Q2_Consolidated_Financial_Statements.pdf ; https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/05/apple-reports-second-quarter-results/ ; https://www.cnbc.com/2025/05/01/apple-has-managed-tariffs-so-far-says-tough-to-predict-beyond-june.html
Date: 2025-06-30

The June 30 unadjusted stock close was $205.17. Against fiscal 2024 diluted EPS of $6.08, that is approximately 33.75 times reported trailing-year earnings. Against March quarterly EPS of $1.65 multiplied by four, it is about 31.09 times; the latter is only a mechanical run rate and ignores holiday seasonality. Neither is verified next-twelve-month consensus. The historical daily-price source is cited separately below; financial inputs come from the dated filings. [1,2]

First-half cash flow less all capital expenditure was $47.876 billion. Doubling it would produce $95.752 billion, but the half-year includes seasonality and unusual tax-payment timing, so this is not a defensible stand-alone forecast. Price must be considered alongside the capital-gains tax that an existing low-basis owner would pay when selling. A tax cost makes continued ownership economically different from a new purchase, without making an outsized position untouchable. Exact Berkshire tax-lot basis and June-date consensus estimates are unavailable. [1,2]

Evidence ledger: fiscal revenues in 2022, 2023 and 2024 were $394.328 billion, $383.285 billion and $391.035 billion. Fiscal 2024 diluted EPS was $6.08. In the March quarter, revenue was $95.359 billion, operating income $29.589 billion, net income $24.780 billion and diluted EPS $1.65. Products supplied $68.714 billion sales and Services $26.645 billion. First-half operating cash flow was $53.887 billion and capital spending $6.011 billion. March cash and marketable securities totaled $132.922 billion; commercial paper and term debt totaled $98.186 billion. The board authorized another $100 billion repurchase program and a $0.26 quarterly dividend. Cook estimated about $900 million additional tariff costs for the June quarter, conditional on unchanged policy. [1-4]

Valuation discipline: the calculations in this document are analyst arithmetic, not management targets or a Berkshire underwriting model. Earnings multiples, book multiples and cash-flow yields answer different questions and are not interchangeable. A low multiple offers no protection if normalized earning power has been overstated. Conversely, growth has value only after the incremental capital required to obtain it. No verified contemporary consensus forecast or complete historical multiple distribution is supplied; an exact intrinsic value should therefore not be reverse-engineered from a subsequent transaction.
