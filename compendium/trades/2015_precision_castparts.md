## Buffett's thesis in his own words

## What Berkshire Hathaway did

## What happened after

In the 2020 letter Buffett acknowledged overpaying and misjudging average future earnings; the roughly $11B Berkshire write-down was almost entirely PCC. The pandemic aggravated the result but did not absolve his purchase-price error. See [impairment](2020_pcc_impairment.md). [2]
