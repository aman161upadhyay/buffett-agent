Revenue is net of interest expense; net margin equals net income/revenue, not net interest margin. JPMorgan's quarter includes a large equity-exchange gain and is not a clean recurring earnings benchmark. Share counts are common equity shares, not deposit market shares. The target's Q2 net interest yield was 1.93% FTE versus 2.06% a year earlier; deposit repricing can dominate loan-volume growth (July 16 supplemental tables).

D04's 1.73x target tangible-book multiple lies between JPMorgan's 2.28x and Wells Fargo's 1.56x, with Citigroup at 0.78x. These observed prices should be evaluated against target ROTCE 13.6%, not assumed equivalent peer franchises. The 13-basis-point decline in target net interest yield is an operating observation; neither a market premium nor a peer's low book multiple explains it. No uniform fair-value multiple or independently archived forward consensus is implied.

Compare other major US banks on deposits, net interest margins, credit provisioning, duration risk, regulatory capital and market-facing exposures. A security below book can still be expensive if normalized returns are poor. Conversely, accounting marks on securities need cash-flow and funding context. Different ownership and disclosure thresholds affect an investor's reporting obligations, not the borrower's operating earning power. [Source above.]

Technical context: a financial institution's earning assets are supported by deposits, wholesale funding, creditors and equity with different seniority. Regulatory capital cannot all be distributed to investors. Analyze credit quality, liquidity and capital separately: a solvent institution can still face a funding run, and liquid assets do not prove all credit losses are recognized. For a preferred claim, identify whether dividends are cumulative, the issuer's redemption rights and the creditor cushion ahead of the security. For common, normalize provisioning and assess return on tangible equity rather than using industrial free-cash-flow conventions. Reported book value, tangible common capital and market capitalization are different denominators. [Source above; analytical framework rather than additional issuer measurements.]

Comparison discipline: compare businesses on cash generation and required capital rather than treating the nearest ticker as an exact substitute. Normalize accounting differences and unusual items before interpreting relative multiples. A book discount need not imply value when sustainable returns are low. The numerical comparison supplies a starting point, not a fictitious uniform industry average or an instruction to trade.

Evidence boundary: current margins, CET1 and historical stock multiples are separate measurements. None reveals the investor's intended position size or a binding ownership-limit disposal rule.
