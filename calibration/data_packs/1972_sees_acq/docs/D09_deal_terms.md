## Instrument and consideration — reconstructed

Acquisition-history reconstructions describe approximately $29–30 million annual sales, roughly $4–4.2 million pretax earnings, approximately $2 million after-tax earnings and about $7–8 million net tangible operating capital. These are rounded entry-scale references, not an audited January 3 trailing-year statement. Source accounts use different periods and rounding; exact pre-purchase reconciliation is not available in contemporaneous sources recovered here. In particular, a full-year table for the year just beginning is not substituted for information available today. The figures imply high operating returns on tangible capital, subject to verifying the denominator, normal working capital and the amount of excess cash transferred with the business.

## Valuation units

The contemplated purchase price is $25 million for the business, reconstructed from the transaction history. Against rounded $4–4.2 million pretax earnings that is approximately 6.0–6.25 times pretax earnings, a 16–16.8% pretax earnings yield before purchase adjustments. It is approximately 3.1–3.6 times the rounded $7–8 million tangible-capital range. These are analyst calculations on reconstructed figures, not an original valuation model. Paying above book value requires economic goodwill: customers must produce earnings in excess of the return available on commodity assets. No value is assigned to nationwide expansion, to an unverified amount of excess cash, or to a particular future selling price per pound.

## Ownership and contractual boundaries

The security and the operating business are separate objects of analysis. A whole-company buyer obtains the residual economics after the business meets its obligations, while a minority shareholder cannot unilaterally direct distributions. Preferred stock normally ranks ahead of common for its specified claims, but its legal priority is not a guarantee of payment and does not supersede all creditors. Conversion changes the form and ranking of exposure rather than creating a second independent asset that can be counted twice.

Where the complete original agreement is unavailable, this summary does not imply that an unmentioned covenant exists or that an unmentioned restriction is absent. Proposed consideration can change in dollar value before closing when the exchange uses shares. Regulatory conditions, issuance date, payment date and accounting acquisition date should be verified separately. No subsequent financial result or investor recommendation is supplied by this terms summary.
