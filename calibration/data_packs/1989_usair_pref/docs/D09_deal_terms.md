## Instrument and consideration — reconstructed

The proposed investment is $358 million of 9.25% convertible preferred, providing $33.115 million annual contractual dividends by multiplication. Conversion is at $60 a share after two years, implying about 5.967 million shares before any contractual adjustment if all principal converts. The instrument has ten-year mandatory redemption and dividend-arrearage provisions; the complete original agreement is not recovered. Preferred ranking is senior to common equity but does not place Berkshire ahead of secured lenders and other senior creditors. A detailed lease-adjusted balance sheet, unit-cost comparison and fleet-capital budget are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered here. The contractual dividend is not the same as a guaranteed economic yield.

## Valuation units

The negotiated cash yield is 9.25% on $358 million. Conversion at $60 provides upside if common value rises enough, and ten-year redemption offers a contractual endpoint subject to issuer solvency. The exact August 7 common close, the conversion premium and an independently priced option are not recovered. Thus the security should be evaluated as credit plus contingent equity participation, not as a discounted common-stock earnings yield. The financing may help stabilize ownership but that benefit to management does not itself enhance value for the preferred buyer. No assumption of an immediately profitable conversion or a certain takeout is embedded in the valuation.

## Ownership and contractual boundaries

The security and the operating business are separate objects of analysis. A whole-company buyer obtains the residual economics after the business meets its obligations, while a minority shareholder cannot unilaterally direct distributions. Preferred stock normally ranks ahead of common for its specified claims, but its legal priority is not a guarantee of payment and does not supersede all creditors. Conversion changes the form and ranking of exposure rather than creating a second independent asset that can be counted twice.

Where the complete original agreement is unavailable, this summary does not imply that an unmentioned covenant exists or that an unmentioned restriction is absent. Proposed consideration can change in dollar value before closing when the exchange uses shares. Regulatory conditions, issuance date, payment date and accounting acquisition date should be verified separately. No subsequent financial result or investor recommendation is supplied by this terms summary.
