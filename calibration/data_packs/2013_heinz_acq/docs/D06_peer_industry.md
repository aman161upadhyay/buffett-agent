The rounded peer figures are historical reported fiscal-year measures, not organic-growth estimates. Heinz's latest quarter had reported growth of only 0.5%, despite higher organic growth, because currency translation mattered. Fiscal calendars, restructuring and pension costs differ; this table is a numeric scale comparison, not a claim of comparable normalized profitability.

Compare branded-food peers on category strength, geographic mix, organic growth, gross margin, cash conversion and working capital. A ketchup franchise is not interchangeable with every packaged-food brand. A financially leveraged acquisition partner may seek a different holding period or dividend policy from a permanent owner. Evaluate the operating partner's incentives alongside the preferred-versus-common capital stack. [Source above.]

Technical context: branded food can generate recurring consumer demand, yet retailers control shelf space and private labels create alternatives. Evaluate price, volume and mix independently; a price increase with collapsing units is not unambiguous pricing power. Commodity inflation, foreign exchange, promotion and working-capital seasonality affect cash conversion. A control purchase can use several layers of debt, preferred and common, each with different return rights. The same enterprise can offer an attractive preferred coupon while leaving common owners exposed to leverage. Operating cost reduction should be evaluated alongside brand investment and customer retention, not counted as recurring growth automatically. [Source above; analytical framework.]

Comparison discipline: compare businesses on the source of their cash and the capital required to earn it, rather than treating the nearest ticker as an exact substitute. Normalize financing and accounting differences before interpreting relative multiples. A peer can have a better balance sheet but a worse franchise, or a better franchise at an unattractive price. Relative cheapness alone cannot prove absolute value. Consider the opportunity cost of adding to an existing understandable holding, retaining liquidity, owning an entire business or buying the investor's own shares. Where a numerical peer table was not recovered, keep the comparison qualitative rather than inventing an industry average.

Evidence boundary: missing figures are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered for this digest. Analytical questions are not additional reported facts or claims about the investor's private thinking. Read the financial, risk and fund-context documents together; no individual accounting ratio supplies a complete investment conclusion.
