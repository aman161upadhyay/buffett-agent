# Apple

Research cutoff: **2026-10-25**. Month-format dates are documented windows, not invented execution days. Unknown means an evidence gap. Where return is unknown, outcome class `flat` is an **unscored schema sentinel, not a claim of zero return**.

## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: start of Q1 2016; actual first purchase day unknown.** Berkshire initially reported about 9.8M Apple shares before Apple’s 2020 four-for-one split. May 2016 reporting explicitly said a deputy initiated the position, not Buffett. Buffett’s subsequent scaling is a different decision. [1,2]

**Snapshot gaps:** Entry-day audited valuation, trading range and independently documented consensus: unknown; do not substitute the first 13F mark for purchase cost. Financial/consensus gaps are not filled with later outcomes.

## Buffett's thesis in his own words

Buffett subsequently explained Apple through consumer attachment rather than semiconductor forecasting. The 2020 letter describes share repurchases raising Berkshire’s percentage ownership despite partial sales. These are later explanations, not statements available to an outsider on the first 2016 purchase day. [2,3]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did

Initial deputy purchase in Q1 2016; Buffett later built a much larger position, openly acknowledging the transition in August 2017. Later reporting identifies Weschler as initializer, but contemporary Buffett did not name the deputy. The frontmatter remains unknown for the combined episode; later Buffett adds are attributable to him. [1,2,4]
