These differing sectors illustrate why a single index earnings multiple is not a uniform offer. March financial-company profits and dividends require fresh solvency scrutiny; no assumption is made that those payouts survive.

The 20.60x sensitivity deliberately mixes a current quote with a prior-month historical earnings estimate, which can be revised and need not be sustainable. It is not the eventually reported trough multiple. Using the September dividend run rate is also not guidance that all constituents can maintain payouts. The index has no issuer cash/debt/share count; those fields are not silently mapped to a fund. Sustainable owner cash flow, required liquidity and government-security income remain the relevant comparison, without importing a subsequent market bottom.

Technical context: cash, bills, long bonds and productive assets serve different purposes. Short government instruments preserve nominal liquidity but expose holders to reinvestment rates and purchasing-power erosion. A long bond fixes nominal payments while adding duration risk. A public equity is a residual ownership claim whose value depends on sustainable distributable cash, not tomorrow's quote. A controlled operating business adds capital-allocation rights and illiquidity. Compare after-tax expected economics on consistent horizons, retaining enough liquidity for obligations before allocating discretionary capital. The size of a cash balance alone does not identify a market forecast or explain every trade. [Source above; comparative investment framework, not a new macroeconomic estimate.]

Valuation discipline: use a consistent claim and denominator. Enterprise value should be compared with cash generation before financing; common value should be compared with cash available after senior claims. Explicitly distinguish sustainable earnings from annualizing one quarter. A current quote from a different date, an eventual transaction price and an investor's later disclosed average cost are not substitutes for the cutoff price. State a range of operating assumptions, compare the result with liquid alternatives and require a margin of safety that survives a less favorable case. When a current input is unavailable, leave the numerical valuation incomplete instead of embedding an untraceable estimate.

Evidence boundary: missing figures are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered for this digest. Analytical questions are not additional reported facts or claims about the investor's private thinking. Read the financial, risk and fund-context documents together; no individual accounting ratio supplies a complete investment conclusion.
