## Buffett's thesis in his own words

Buffett wrote “I’m going quiet. Sort of.” He would stop writing the annual report letter and leading the long annual-meeting discussion, while continuing Thanksgiving communications. Do not say he ceased every public comment or immediately sold all Berkshire holdings. [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did

November 10 letter discussed succession and gifts following conversion of 1,800 A into 2.7M B shares. Those gifts are personal philanthropy, not Berkshire corporate capital allocation. The transaction is classified other for explicit non-trade context. [1]

## What happened after

Greg Abel became CEO January 1, 2026. Todd Combs’ departure was a later, separately announced December event. Buffett’s September 2026 chairman transition is also later, not something the November letter had already completed. [2–4]
