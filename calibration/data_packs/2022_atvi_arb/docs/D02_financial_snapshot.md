The 2021 cash conversion is less impressive than the 37.0% operating margin: operating earnings increased faster than FCF. This is historical cash-statement arithmetic, not an adjustment excluding compensation or development costs, and does not set a stand-alone failure price.

FY2021 tangible book is $7.35bn after $9.80bn goodwill and $0.45bn intangibles. Net cash exceeds tangible book, making a net-debt-adjusted tangible-capital return denominator negative: such a ratio is economically not meaningful, not evidence of infinite earning power. Software amortization was separately $0.324bn in 2021 and $0.249bn in 2020; it must not be lost when normalizing investment requirements.

The announced purchase price is $95 cash per share and the headline transaction value is $68.7 billion inclusive of net cash. The headline is not a pure common-equity capitalization and should not be divided blindly by a share count. The contemporaneous $81.50 observation and its dated spread are in D04. The bidder says financing is available from its substantial resources. [Source above.]

Technical context: merger arbitrage is a contingent cash-flow problem. Separate the promised consideration, financing, approvals, termination rights, outside date and possible remedies. Gross spread is not expected return: completion probability, time and the break-price loss all matter. A bidder's ability to pay does not settle regulatory approval. Stand-alone valuation is important even if the intended holding period ends at closing, because the target remains a public business if the transaction fails. Do not treat an original pre-announcement stock purchase and a post-announcement workout as the same investment thesis merely because they use the same ticker. [Source above; analytical framework.]

Accounting discipline: the figures stated above are reported observations unless expressly labeled arithmetic. A period-end balance is not a flow and a market mark is not purchase cost. Do not mix fiscal years, currencies, share bases or enterprise and equity values. To construct owner earnings, begin with the relevant reported profit, reconcile noncash items and subtract the investment required to maintain economic position. Changes in working capital and recurring compensation remain economic costs even when a convenient adjusted metric excludes them. If maintenance investment, leverage or the proper share denominator is missing, state that the yield cannot be completed rather than present false precision.

Evidence boundary: historical cash flows are distinct from promised merger consideration. Reported numbers and arithmetic are identified; neither supplies an assumed probability of regulatory approval.
