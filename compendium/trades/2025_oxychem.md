## Buffett's thesis in his own words

Greg Abel’s announcement cites OxyChem’s robust operating assets and accomplished team. The February 2026 Abel letter adds essential chlorine/caustic-soda markets, low-cost raw materials and execution discipline. That fuller rationale was disclosed after entry. [1,2]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did

Agreement entered October 1 and announced October 2, 2025 for $9.7B cash subject to adjustments. Contrary to the initial expected Q4 2025 close, completed January 2, 2026 for approximately $9.4B including post-closing adjustments. Occidental retained legacy environmental liabilities. [1,3]

## What happened after
