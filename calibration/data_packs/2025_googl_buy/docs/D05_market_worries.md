Title: AI substitution, antitrust remedies and capital escalation
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204425000056/googexhibit991q22025.htm ; https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/department-justice-wins-significant-remedies-against-google ; https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/LSB/PDF/LSB11362/LSB11362.1.pdf
Date: 2025-09-30

A skeptic can accept today's high margins while rejecting the terminal value implied by a durable search monopoly. AI assistants may answer queries without sending users through the same advertising surfaces. Computing an answer can be more expensive than showing a conventional result. A fight requiring rising data-center spending could reduce returns even if usage grows. The latest release's increased capital budget is direct evidence that the future model is more physically demanding. [1]

September 2 search remedies prohibit specified exclusive distribution contracts and require access to certain search data and services. The September 10 Congressional Research Service account says the court rejected immediate Chrome divestiture and contingent Android divestiture, but imposed behavioral relief. Avoiding a breakup is not equivalent to having no competitive remedy. Regulatory processes and appeal risk remain. The fact that current search revenue still grows cannot establish what market share, pricing or return on incremental AI capital will look like a decade from now. [2,3]

Evidence ledger: the July 23 release reports second-quarter revenue of $96.428 billion, up 14%; operating income of $31.271 billion; net income of $28.196 billion; and diluted EPS of $2.31. Services revenue was $82.543 billion, Cloud $13.624 billion and Other Bets $373 million. Services operating income was $33.063 billion, Cloud $2.826 billion, Other Bets a $1.246 billion loss and central activities a $3.372 billion cost. Cash plus marketable securities totaled $95.148 billion, versus $23.607 billion long-term debt. Trailing operating cash flow was $133.708 billion, capital expenditures $66.980 billion and free cash flow $66.728 billion. Management increased planned annual capital spending to approximately $85 billion. September court remedies prohibit specified exclusivity arrangements and require access to certain data/services. [1-3]

Risk distinction: a temporary earnings interruption and a permanent impairment can initially produce similar reported numbers. The test is whether the cause can be bounded without assuming that management's preferred recovery path must occur. Position liquidity limits execution risk, not business risk. The adverse mechanisms set out here are scenarios grounded in the cited contemporary evidence, not claims that every adverse outcome has occurred. No independent short-seller target or numerical probability is invented where a dated opposing valuation was not recovered.
