The contemporaneous September 30 UPI transaction report supplies a price and consideration mechanism, not an interim earnings release. Period newspaper coverage and state public-history sources do not replace seller diligence statements. The absence of public quarterly disclosure is not evidence of zero revenue, falling revenue or negligible working-capital requirements. In particular, a quoted purchase price cannot be reverse-engineered into earnings using a guessed acquisition multiple. Any investment memorandum requiring a quarter-to-quarter bridge must request seller financials and check inventory seasonality, receivable collectibility, capital spending and factory utilization. This numerical boundary is retained rather than manufacturing a public-company style quarter. No future owner's consolidated results or retrospective impairment are used to fill it.

## Cutoff evidence — reconstructed

Dexter is represented in the pre-acquisition history as profitable and well run, rather than as a turnaround requiring operational repair. Original latest-quarter results, factory-level costs and inventory-aging schedules were not recovered. Existing H. H. Brown and Lowell experience makes footwear an understandable operating field, but the record for a different business cannot substitute for Dexter's own capital and margins. No subsequent import outcome or later assessment of the business is inserted here. The available operating question is whether current productivity, quality and distribution allow satisfactory returns through price competition. A forecast of unchanged margins would require evidence beyond the reconstructed managerial record.

## Economic reading of the supplied facts

Industry economics: footwear requires design, purchasing, manufacturing or sourcing, distribution and inventory across styles and sizes. Unsold inventory can be worth less than recorded cost. A good domestic factory can still be vulnerable if imports deliver an acceptable product at materially lower cost. Brand, quality and delivery speed may support customer loyalty, but each must be tested against the consumer's alternatives. Owning stores can capture retail margin while adding leases, inventory and fixed expenses. Efficient operations are valuable only to the extent that they sustain economic returns after all capital requirements. This is a description of competitive possibilities at the cutoff, not a prediction that domestic production must succeed or fail.

Reporting discipline: a full fiscal year's results are not available merely because part of that year has elapsed. The observations above are restricted to the reporting periods identified, and no later quarter is backfilled. An annualized partial period would be a calculation, not a reported result; no such annualization is used unless expressly identified.

For decision purposes, the key question is whether the recent change alters recurring earning power or only its timing. Volume, realized price, operating expenses and capital requirements should be considered separately. A price increase that merely offsets higher input costs is not necessarily an improvement in economics. Similarly, a recovery in accounting profit is not proof that competitive advantage has returned. Where a current operating statistic has not been recovered, this document makes no assertion about its direction. The evidence permits a conditional assessment of durability, not a precise estimate of an unavailable earnings announcement.
