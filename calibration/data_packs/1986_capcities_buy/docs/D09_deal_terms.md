Title: Capital Cities/ABC — Security and transaction terms
Source: Capital Cities/ABC transaction announced March 18, 1985; Berkshire 1984 letter subsequent-event appendix, https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1984.html
Date: 1986-01-03

## Instrument and consideration — reconstructed

The minority-financing terms are 3 million shares at $172.50 each, or $517.5 million by multiplication. ABC's combination value is described at roughly $3.5 billion in transaction accounts. A complete opening combined balance sheet, post-deal debt amortization schedule and normalized ABC cash-flow reconciliation are not recovered. The subscription price is specified, but the combined group's immediate per-share earnings are not independently reconstructed. A numerical earnings multiple would therefore be misleading. The size makes operating and managerial quality material to a prospective subscriber, while the minority structure limits unilateral capital-allocation influence. This is a financing-terms review, not a disclosure of a recommended subscription.

## Valuation units

The subscription terms price 3 million shares at $172.50 each, totaling $517.5 million. This is a negotiated minority block associated with the ABC transaction, not a controlling purchase of the entire media group. A precise January 3 market close and comparable closing multiple are unavailable in the recovered record. The contractual price can be assessed separately from the current quote. The central valuation dependency is sustainable combined earning power under the existing management, less integration and financing demands. No control premium, break-up sale or guaranteed short-term rerating is credited. The financing requires patience if earnings at the acquired network are initially unexciting.

## Ownership and contractual boundaries

The security and the operating business are separate objects of analysis. A whole-company buyer obtains the residual economics after the business meets its obligations, while a minority shareholder cannot unilaterally direct distributions. Preferred stock normally ranks ahead of common for its specified claims, but its legal priority is not a guarantee of payment and does not supersede all creditors. Conversion changes the form and ranking of exposure rather than creating a second independent asset that can be counted twice.

Where the complete original agreement is unavailable, this summary does not imply that an unmentioned covenant exists or that an unmentioned restriction is absent. Proposed consideration can change in dollar value before closing when the exchange uses shares. Regulatory conditions, issuance date, payment date and accounting acquisition date should be verified separately. No subsequent financial result or investor recommendation is supplied by this terms summary.
