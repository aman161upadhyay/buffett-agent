## Rates and the equity earnings hurdle

[US Treasury, 2026-10-23](https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?field_tdr_date_value_month=202610&type=daily_treasury_yield_curve) official par yields: 3-month **4.22%**, 6-month **4.30%**, 1-year **4.44%**, 2-year **4.69%**, 5-year **4.82%**, 10-year **5.00%**, 20-year **5.37%**, 30-year **5.33%**. These are indicative par-curve yields, not Berkshire's realized portfolio yield. The long-bond yield exceeds the bill yield; maturity/duration risks differ.

[FactSet, 2026-10-23](https://insight.factset.com/sp-500-earnings-season-update-october-23-2026) gives S&P forward P/E **18.7**, below five-year 19.8 and ten-year 19.1 averages. Reciprocal forward **after-tax accounting earnings yield = 5.35%**, calculated: only **+0.35 percentage point** versus ten-year and **+0.02 point** versus thirty-year. This is not a measured equity risk premium or a pretax owner-earnings comparison. [FactSet's October 16 report](https://go.factset.com/hubfs/Website/Resources%20Section/Research%20Desk/Earnings%20Insight/EarningsInsight_101626.pdf) has trailing P/E 26.0, implying 3.85% yield, but is one week older; do not present it as October 23 trailing data.

FactSet's October 23 growth estimates: Q3 blended EPS growth **29.5%**, revenue **12.7%**, CY2026 EPS **33.1%**; only **24%** of companies have reported, so those are not completed full-year earnings. Modest forward multiples depend on large earnings forecasts. Treasury yields are contractual nominal returns subject to duration/inflation; equities are uncertain growing claims.

## Aggregate valuation and credit

[USMacro, data through 2026-10-22](https://usmacro.com/indicator/buffett_indicator) reports total-market-cap/GDP **234%**, using Wilshire/Dow Jones total-market continuation and BEA GDP. [DailyMarket, refreshed 2026-10-24](https://dailymarket.report/) reports **255%** for corporate-equities/GDP. Definitions and denominator vintages differ; these are **not a reconciled common series**, and 255% is not substituted for the documented 234% method. Neither is a timing rule.

[FRED/ICE high-yield OAS, updated 2026-10-23](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/BAMLH0A0HYM2) latest available observation **October 22: 3.40% (340bp)** versus October 21 327bp. Friday October 23's observation is not assumed available before its next release. Spread widening is evidence of repricing, not evidence of systemic crisis; it is distinct from all-in borrowing yield.

## IPO supply

[Renaissance Capital final Q3 review, 2026-10-01](https://www.renaissancecapital.com/review/3Q26USReview_Public.pdf): **30 US IPOs/$32.8bn**, of which SK hynix $26.5bn; excluding it, $6.2bn. The earlier September 24 preview reported 31/$34.9bn; use the final revision. Complete October/YTD through October 23 totals were **not recovered**. Do not call Q3 totals October issuance. Concentrated proceeds and postponed deals describe a selective window, not universal access to capital or automatic opportunities for Berkshire. Individual business yields and capital needs [F03–04] remain more decision-relevant than an unsupported market forecast.
