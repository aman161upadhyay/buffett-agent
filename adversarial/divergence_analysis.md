# Divergence analysis: rule failure, evidence failure and scoring failure are different

## Classification and evidence convention

**(a)** pack defect/information insufficiency; **(b)** prompt too strict, too loose or internally contradictory; **(c)** key/grader/measurement problem; **(d)** genuine discretionary/idiosyncratic behavior not identifiable from a public rule; **(e)** model execution, memory or variance. Labels are competing hypotheses, not identified causal shares. Named/masked disagreement alone does not prove memory.

For every calibration ID below, `R(id)` denotes the exact paths `calibration/runs/<id>__current__named.json` and `...__masked.json`; `K(id)` denotes `calibration/answer_keys/<id>.json`; `S(id)` denotes the corresponding `calibration/scores/<id>__current__*__score.json`. Hold-out keys/scores are under `answer_keys/holdout/` and `scores/holdout/`. `P(id)/Dnn` denotes the matching file beginning Dnn under `calibration/data_packs/<id>/docs/`. These references expand literally, not to invented artifacts. The scoreboard's divergence table includes sizing/attribution PARTIALs, so those are included, not mislabeled action misses.

## Which amendments overfit, and where they misfire

Case motivation is documented in `prompts/CHANGELOG.md`; an amendment is not invalid merely because a case exposed it. The danger is a narrow patch promoted to a universal rule.

|Version/change|Judgment and concrete transfer failure|
|---|---|
|v1.2 bond-relative pricing|Legitimate normalization of rate regimes; **overfit as a compulsory numeric wall**. Cap Cities full-price managerial compounding and Coke's unmatched net/pretax denominators show the limit. Use total return after reinvestment, not forced historical yield anchors.|
|v1.2 preferred override|Legitimate instrument separation; **overfit if coupon/base-case survival replaces downside credit diligence**. A distressed bank that survives by wiping out preferreds would qualify under a sloppy “government support” reading (red-team pack 6).|
|v1.2 B2 three-part cloud test and LARGE size|**Overfit to AmEx** when renewal/usage is equated with sound unit economics. An insurer retaining customers by underpricing passes demand tests while destroying capital. GEICO requires statutory-capital/reinsurance repair, not merely loyal customers.|
|v1.2 UNSURE never vetoes|Useful for missing size, unsafe for missing solvency or honesty. A pack deleting adverse evidence can convert FAIL into UNSURE and become more buyable. Separate missing critical data from nonessential uncertainty.|
|v1.2 price-first drivers|**Explicit score optimization**, motivated by WaPo overlap. Gillette's predictable franchise versus Champion's terms are not interchangeable; a forced price-first sentence can obscure the causal difference.|
|v1.3 crisis normalization/drawdown trigger|Normalize earnings legitimately; **overfit** if every 35–40% panic assumes the old earning base returns. A permanently impaired index can be falsely labeled BUY_BROADLY (red-team pack 7).|
|v1.3 financial liabilities/float/stock consideration|Sector-specific accounting is legitimate, but “float ... worth ≈ its face” is an unsafe valuation shortcut. Loss-making runoff/reserve-deficient insurers do not gain full float value merely from financing below the bond rate; avoid double-counting invested assets and liability deferral.|
|v1.4 proposed preferred|**BofA-specific patch, structurally contradictory** across 02/03. The First Republic minority false BUY is an observed transfer warning. Proposed, offered and executable terms must be different states.|
|v1.4 B11 package|Broadly legitimate (multiple cases). Overfit wording “senior coupon carries most” can obscure consolidated leverage and common impairment; Gillette also shows protection can sacrifice better common economics.|
|v1.4 B12/new record|Legitimate updating; **overfit** if consolidation itself proves durable pricing. Airlines 2016 Gate 2 adopts B12 while Gate 1 still vetoes; the opposite error is a disciplined-looking basket financed by unsustainable peak cash flows.|
|v1.4 ADD default|Legitimate completion of intended purchases; dangerous when **target is invented** rather than a documented risk budget. The Fannie/Wal-Mart omission dossiers show it deliberately ceases to imitate real errors.|
|v1.5 discount override/B13|**Severe overfit** to PetroChina/Japan: explicit permission to override management criterion 6. A cheap controlled issuer with asset diversion/capital controls can pass (red-team pack 4). Asset appraisal is not enforceable shareholder value.|
|v1.5 sell when B13 gap closes|Reasonable for tactical bargains; not universal. The forward era-E addendum describes Japan as “a continuing long-term capital-allocation relationship,” not merely a gap-closing trade.|
|v1.5 retained-operator/US-tailwind driver mandate|**Overfit to BNSF's key**. An insurer acquisition's decisive reserve adequacy cannot be replaced by saying the country will need insurance; naming an operator is not evidence of trust.|
|v1.6 arb override|Legitimate removal of irrelevant operating-quality gate. It still needs adversarial failure/recovery probabilities; a definitive contract is not a low-risk merger (red-team pack 3).|
|v1.6 energy extension to criteria 2/3/4/8|**OXY-shaped conjunction** risks accepting positive trough operating cash before maintenance capex as resilience. A depleting resource with attractive spot FCF and rapid debt repayment can still destroy normalized owner value.|
|v1.6 S3 numbers/first trim|**Apple/BofA overfit**: “first trim ...10–15%” and “residual stays the largest holding” are nonsensical universal rules for every bank threshold or liquidity sale. Continued BofA reductions below 10% illustrate the narrow trigger's failure; no new holding-specific trigger is justified.|
|v1.6 explicit yield extraction|Legitimate information preservation. Copying every yield “verbatim” without basis reconciliation can promote the most flattering denominator; Coke/TSMC errors show numeric fields are not validated arithmetic.|
