Title: Medical-cost uncertainty, governance and investigation risk
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176625000022/a2024q4exhibit991.htm ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176625000123/a2025q1exhibit991.htm ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176625000134/exhibit991.htm ; https://www.unitedhealthgroup.com/newsroom/2025/2025-05-14-response-may-14-wsj-article.html
Date: 2025-06-30

The strongest contemporary bear case begins with management's inability to forecast its own near-term costs. April's cut did not contain the problem; by May care activity was accelerating across more benefits. Premium schedules cannot necessarily be reset immediately to offset utilization, so a small change in the medical-cost ratio can consume a large fraction of underwriting profit. Optum's reimbursement and patient-profile issues mean the problem is not confined to one insurance product. [2,3]

The Wall Street Journal's May reporting also raised the possibility of a criminal Medicare investigation. The company's May 14 response says it had not been notified of the supposed investigation and stands by its program. That denial is important, but is not proof that no regulatory risk exists. Neither a criminal charge nor an eventual legal resolution is assumed here. Leadership transition adds execution uncertainty. A buyer must distinguish a repairable pricing error from a structural reduction in the returns earned by integrated healthcare intermediaries. [3,4]

Evidence ledger, with dollar amounts in billions unless stated otherwise: the January 16 release reports 2024 revenue of $400.3, versus $371.6 in 2023, operating earnings of $32.3, operating cash flow of $24.2, GAAP EPS of $15.51 and adjusted EPS of $27.66. The April 17 release reports first-quarter revenue of $109.6, up $9.8, GAAP EPS of $6.85 and adjusted EPS of $7.20. UnitedHealthcare added 780,000 consumers year to date. April's reduced adjusted annual EPS range was $26.00-$26.50; the May leadership announcement suspended the outlook, so that range is no longer current guidance. The May statement disputes the press characterization of a supposed criminal investigation. These are issuer statements, not independent legal findings. [1-4]

Risk distinction: a temporary earnings interruption and a permanent impairment can initially produce similar reported numbers. The test is whether the cause can be bounded without assuming that management's preferred recovery path must occur. Position liquidity limits execution risk, not business risk. The adverse mechanisms set out here are scenarios grounded in the cited contemporary evidence, not claims that every adverse outcome has occurred. No independent short-seller target or numerical probability is invented where a dated opposing valuation was not recovered.
