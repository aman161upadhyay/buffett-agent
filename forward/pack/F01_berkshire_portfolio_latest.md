## Observation boundary and balance sheet

Latest ordinary 13F: **June 30, 2026**; Q3 filing is not yet due. June marks/weights, not October prices or costs. Aggregated manager rows total **$299.254bn**. Non-US ordinary shares, subsidiaries, cash and Occidental preferreds are excluded; not Berkshire NAV. [1]

June insurance/other cash/equivalents **$35.096bn** + Treasury bills **$324.905bn** − unsettled-bill payables **$0.771bn** = **$359.230bn** liquidity. Rail/utilities/energy cash $5.513bn brings consolidated net cash/bills to $364.743bn, excluding restricted cash. $3.2bn bills inside equivalents must NOT be double-counted. Float **$177.5bn**, up $1.1bn since December, had negative H1 average cost from underwriting profits. Float is a liability-funded resource, not additional cash. [2, balance sheet/insurance MD&A]

Fair-value equity securities are $323.779bn; equity-method investments $19.948bn. OXY common/Kraft Heinz use equity accounting but also appear in 13F. These are different universes. [2, notes 4–5]

## 2026 deployment and turnover

H1 equity purchases **$39.405bn**, sales **$27.780bn**: net buying $11.625bn, not changes in 13F market value. Alphabet A grew from 17.846m December to 78.791m June; C from zero to 27.188m. Delta/Macy's entered Q1; NYT grew from 5.066m to 15.700m. Lennar A/B increased both quarters. Apple stayed **227.918m** throughout H1; no verified 2026 Apple trim. [1–3]

BofA fell from 517.296m December to 483.394m June; Chevron 130.156m to 84.376m; DaVita 31.759m to 28.880m; Nucor 6.408m to 1.858m. Q2 also reduced Ally, Capital One and Kroger. Q1 eliminated reported UNH, Amazon, Visa, Mastercard, Aon, Domino's, Pool, HEICO, Diageo, Allegion, Lamar, Charter, Atlanta Braves and several Liberty securities; Constellation's small Q1 residual disappeared Q2. No filing supplies a security-specific explanation or proves each sale's individual decider. [1,3]

**Buybacks resumed**, not still halted: equity statements show $235m Q1 and $4.527bn Q2, $4.762bn H1. Cash-flow treasury-stock payments are $4.444bn; different settlement/accounting measures are not silently equated. June policy: CEO, after chairman consultation, must judge price below conservatively determined intrinsic value; consolidated liquidity floor $30bn. No fixed-dollar commitment. [2, note 18 and capital resources]

OxyChem closed **January 2**, about **$9.4bn** after adjustments versus announced $9.7bn; Occidental retained legacy environmental liabilities. Taylor Morrison closed **July 24**, $6.8bn equity/$8.5bn enterprise value. Neither is a Q2 13F position. [2, note 2; F04]

## Decision authority and post-quarter disclosures

Abel became CEO January 1 and has ultimate capital-allocation responsibility; Weschler manages about 6% of investments, including part of Combs' former portfolio. Combs' departure was announced December 8, 2025; he is not a current Berkshire picker. Ajit Jain oversees insurance; Nancy Pierce replaced Combs at GEICO; Adam Johnson oversees 32 consumer/service/retail companies, while Abel directly oversees remaining non-insurance operations. Chuck Chang became CFO June 1. [4–5]

September 18: **Howard Buffett chairman; Warren chairman emeritus/director**. Warren originated Alphabet with Abel approval [6–7].

Lennar Form 4 signed September 30 documents **26.034m A / 553,000 B** following September 28–30 purchases; October 6 press reports about 12% ownership after further buying. Exact later class totals are not independently reconciled here. Bending Spoons' October 14 issuer filing announces another **$330m** subscription, expected Q4 close, after $600m at July IPO. Do not label the pending subscription funded or predict it as a new, undisclosed idea. [8–9]
