## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**1988 takeover contest:** management announced a buyout and several groups competed. Berkshire's Salomon directorships created a genuine information restriction when Salomon considered a large financing commitment. Original acquisition agreement and time-stamped spreads were not recovered. The following annual letter reports the decision sequence and realized outcome before issuance. [1]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
“We do not trade on rumors or try to guess takeover candidates.” **February 28, 1989**, general arbitrage explanation applied to the disclosed RJR case. [1]

“We just read the newspapers, think about a few of the big propositions, and go by our own sense of probabilities.” Same passage. The source does not claim an inside-information edge; it describes restrictions that cost Berkshire opportunities. [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Bought only in two permitted windows: immediately after management's public announcement before Salomon became involved, and much later after the RJR board selected KKR. Ended 1988 with 3.342 million shares, $281.8 million cost/$304.5 million market; increased to roughly 4 million in January 1989. Tendered to KKR, had about 3 million accepted, and promptly sold the returned shares. Completely eliminated the position in February. [1]

## What happened after
Reported $64 million pretax profit, better than expected. Buffett then expected little arbitrage activity in 1989: less spare cash after long-term equity purchases and concerns about excessive buyer/lender optimism. This completed equity event is distinct from the late-1989 purchase of RJR **bonds**, and neither should inherit the other episode's return. [1]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Catalyst:** announced deal probability rather than a durable tobacco moat drove the trade. [1]
- **Allocation:** surplus cash could earn event returns until better long-term investments arose. [1]
- **Compliance:** director knowledge narrowed legal/practical purchase windows. [1]
- **Exit discipline:** accepted tender shares plus prompt sale of returned shares closed the position. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: flexible category selection constrained by ethics, probabilities and available capital. Good results did not cause Buffett to extrapolate indiscriminately into an overheated takeover market. [1]

## Public discussion
The annual letter describes the public contest and the First Boston/Pritzker alternative. Original bidder submissions and press coverage remain unrecovered. Its information-barrier account is management's own account, not an independent compliance audit. [1]
