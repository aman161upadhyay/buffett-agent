## Publicly documented 2026 transactions, not rumors

**OxyChem:** January 2 close for approximately $9.4bn after adjustments; seller Occidental retained legacy environmental liabilities. Basic chemicals have cyclical margins and substantial capital needs; integration leadership is Rebecca Liebert alongside Wade Alleman. Cash generation and operating adjacency are evidenced; a cheap normalized acquisition multiple is **not available in recovered sources**. Source: [10-Q note 2, 2026-08-08](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/qtrly/2ndqtr26.pdf); [Abel letter, 2026-02-28](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2025ltr.pdf).

**Taylor Morrison:** announced May 31, completed July 24, $72.50/share, about $6.8bn equity/$8.5bn enterprise value. Sheryl Palmer remains CEO and oversees combination with Berkshire's site-built housing operations. This is an actual seller accepting permanent ownership, not an unannounced next-deal candidate. Source: [closing release, 2026-07-24](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/news/jul2426.pdf).

**Alphabet/Bending Spoons:** July 15 Buffett interview confirms participation in Alphabet's announced $10bn private placement [F02]. Bending Spoons' [6-K, 2026-10-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2004711/000200471126000018/bsp-20261014x6k.htm) describes a binding agreement for 10m ordinary shares at $33, $330m, expected Q4 close after July IPO investment. **Common equity, not a senior crisis preferred**. No authenticated additional active Berkshire acquisition talks were recovered; absence of public reports does not establish absence of private approaches.

## Seller-permanence hypotheses — analyst inference only

| Where to look | Why a seller might prefer Berkshire | Necessary disqualifier test |
|---|---|---|
| Family-owned industrial components/distribution | Management autonomy and no resale deadline | Accounts, succession and known price; no auction assumptions |
| Housing/building-products platforms | Clayton/Taylor Morrison provide operating familiarity | Affordability, land obligations and credit exposure |
| Chemical carve-outs from indebted parents | Cash buyer can separate an enduring operation | Environmental indemnities, maintenance capex, normalized cycle |
| Regulated utilities/infrastructure | Long-duration capital and operating experience | Wildfire liability, regulatory compact and financing ring-fences |

These are search categories, **not reports of talks with named sellers**. The operating/autonomy premises come from Abel's February letter. Another Class I railroad is specifically contradicted by that letter's current-economics objection.

**Crisis-financing watch:** a temporary liquidity squeeze at an otherwise solvent insurer, industrial or bank could create B3 terms; no named company in this pack is established to need emergency Berkshire financing today. Require cash maturities/collateral and a survivable stress case, seniority, roughly 8–10% coupon and warrants before treating any situation as analogous to old preferred deals. Ordinary 340bp high-yield spreads [F05] do not by themselves establish distressed financing terms. Separate stressed sellers of good assets from rescue of an impaired parent.
