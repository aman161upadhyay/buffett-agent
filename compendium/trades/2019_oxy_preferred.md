## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: 2019-04-29.** Occidental was competing to buy Anadarko. Berkshire’s commitment was contingent on Occidental entering and completing that acquisition; it was not a direct acquisition of Anadarko by Berkshire. [1,2]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words

The verified primary documents establish the contractual economics: $10B preferred liquidation amount, 8% dividends and 80M common-share warrants at $62.50. They do not disclose a Buffett oil-price forecast or a complete underwriting model. [1,2]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did

Commitment announced April 30, 2019; funding and Anadarko acquisition closed August 8. Warrants later adjusted for issuer distributions; original and adjusted exercise prices must not be confused. Common-stock purchases in 2019 and 2022 are separate from this preferred principal. [1,2]

## What happened after

The preferred remained a financing instrument with redemptions governed by its terms. A 2025 Schedule 13G includes warrants in beneficial ownership; the 13F common-share count excludes unexercised warrants. Exact remaining preferred principal at cutoff requires latest note reconciliation. [3]
