## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**Summer 1988:** Buffett's later account says Goizueta's 1981 move to CEO, working with Keough, had refocused policies and accelerated a ubiquitous product's overseas sales. These are facts about pre-entry management actions as interpreted retrospectively; no original summer-1988 valuation model or purchase ledger was recovered. The initial multiple, rather than a model-memory estimate, remains unknown. The first purchase was **not** Black Monday 1987. [2]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
“Only in the summer of 1988 did my brain finally establish contact with my eyes.” **Hindsight**, March 2, 1990. [2]

“With Roberto, the mesh of marketing and finance is perfect and the result is a shareholder's dream.” Same letter, explaining the business/management combination. [2]

“When we own portions of outstanding businesses with outstanding managements, our favorite holding period is forever.” **Post-purchase**, February 28, 1989, discussing Coke and Freddie Mac together. [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Made major purchases in 1988, finishing the year with 14,172,500 shares at approximately $592.5 million cost and $632.4 million market value. Continued buying during 1989, reaching 23,350,000 shares at $1.023920 billion cost and $1.803787 billion market. These are contemporaneous share counts before later splits; don't compare them directly with modern counts. [1–2]

## What happened after
The 1989 mark showed strong appreciation but was not realized profit. Buffett also warned that major investees were no longer as undervalued and that future returns would not repeat the earlier double benefit of business growth plus rerating. The 1991 letter explicitly says he avoided repeating the Fannie Mae error when Coke's price rose during accumulation. [2–3]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Consumer moat:** long-observed product attractiveness, not a new taste discovery. [2]
- **Management change:** the 1981 refocusing was explicit in his explanation. [2]
- **Runway:** overseas expansion supplied substantial growth potential. [2]
- **Owner economics:** financial and marketing skill worked together. [2]
- **Execution:** a rising quotation alone was not enough to abandon an attractive purchase program. [3]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: mature high-quality franchise ownership rather than cheap assets or merger probability. No invented original DCF, earnings multiple or permanent 1988 forecast is required for calibration. `open` reflects retained position in the cited evidence, not a separate 2026 audit. [1–3]

## Public discussion
Original 1988 analyst/press reactions were not recovered. Buffett's retrospective joke about taking fifty years to recognize the opportunity is not an entry-date argument or proof that every familiar consumer product is attractive at any price. [2]
