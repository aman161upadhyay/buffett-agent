Title: Business and the affirmative investment case
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/885590/000119312515057297/d878630dex991.htm; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1009356/000119312515059044/d879266dex991.htm
Date: 2015-03-02

The company owns branded pharmaceutical, dermatology and eye-care businesses and purchases additional products and distribution. The affirmative platform case is decentralized ownership of established medicines and consumer-health brands combined with disciplined overhead, lower taxes and selective development spending. Bausch + Lomb provides a recognizable repeat-purchase consumer/medical franchise. The investor need not believe every laboratory discovery succeeds if acquired products have durable demand and can be purchased at sensible prices. [S1; analyst interpretation]

The latest presentation offers evidence beyond acquisition growth: 16% same-store organic growth and double-digit full-year growth at Bausch + Lomb. Its February 23 Salix discussion presents gastroenterology products as a strategic extension with cost-saving potential. A bull sees rational capital allocation, reduced duplicative costs and operating cash to pay down acquisition debt. Management cash EPS grows faster than revenue. The attractiveness of this thesis depends on recurring cash after integration, rather than treating every excluded charge as irrelevant. [S1,S2; bull interpretation]

### Dated operating baseline

The February 23 presentation reports fourth-quarter revenue of $2.3 billion versus $2.1 billion, up 10%; management's cash EPS was $2.58 versus $2.15, up 20%. GAAP operating cash flow was $816 million versus $280 million. That cash figure included a $287 million net Allergan transaction gain; management's adjusted operating cash flow was $624 million versus $607 million, only 3% higher. Restructuring and integration costs were $47 million. Reported same-store organic sales growth was 16%; Bausch + Lomb organic growth was 8% for the quarter and 11% for the year. The presentation explicitly warns that forward cash-EPS and adjusted-cash-flow guidance is not reconciled to GAAP because excluded items are difficult to forecast. The contemplated Salix transaction and financing are additional commitments, not part of the historical quarter's recurring cash earnings. Cash EPS is a management-defined earnings measure, not distributable owner cash. [S1]

### Underwriting distinction

The economic unit to value is the cash-producing franchise, not its stock-market category. Separate evidence of repeat customer behavior from a claim that those customers cannot leave. The affirmative interpretation here is an analyst's case, not a representation that Berkshire endorsed it. Costs needed to retain customers, renew products and maintain legal or regulatory permission belong in the economics even when reported in different accounting lines. A minority interest also lacks the ability to redesign the business simply because the owner has permanent capital. [Analytical interpretation of S1]

For diligence, identify which activity earns the customer relationship, which assets must be replaced, and which liabilities could consume the cash before it reaches common equity. The facts below define the scale of the existing enterprise. They do not convert management's expectations into a demonstrated ten-year record. [Analytical interpretation of S1]
