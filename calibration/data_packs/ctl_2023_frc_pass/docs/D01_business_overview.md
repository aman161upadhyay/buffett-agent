Title: Business and the affirmative investment case
Source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230113005044/en/First-Republic-Reports-2022-Results; https://www.fdic.gov/news/press-releases/2023/pr23020.html
Date: 2023-03-16

The bank specializes in relationship banking and wealth management for affluent clients, combining mortgages, deposits and advisory services. Low loan losses and referrals can support high customer lifetime value. The affirmative comparison is a service franchise suffering a generalized regional-bank confidence shock rather than an institution with demonstrated widespread borrower defaults. A recapitalization might preserve the relationships while replacing flight-prone funding, if the economics of the new funding are acceptable. [S1; analyst interpretation]

The latest annual release describes record customer satisfaction, low credit losses and growing tangible book value. On March 16, eleven large banks announce $30 billion in deposits, a concrete liquidity bridge that regulators publicly welcome. A bull can argue that institutional support gives a sound lending franchise time to retain clients and restore confidence. The opportunity resembles a capital-and-confidence intervention, but a minority common investment and a senior negotiated security have different payoffs. [S1,S2; bull interpretation]

### Dated operating baseline

Full-year revenue was $5.9 billion, up 16.5%; net interest income $4.8 billion, up 17.5%; and net income approximately $1.7 billion, up 12.7%. Diluted EPS was $8.25, up 7.4%. Common book value was $75.38 and tangible common book value $74.19 per share. Loans were $166.9 billion, up 23.6%, while deposits were $176.4 billion, up 12.9%. Wealth-management assets were $271.2 billion; they belong to clients and are not bank liquidity. Wealth revenues were $877 million. Tier 1 leverage ratio was 8.51%. Reported nonperforming assets were just five basis points of total assets, and annual net charge-offs $2.9 million. Fourth-quarter net interest margin fell to 2.45% from 2.71% in the preceding quarter. Fourth-quarter EPS was $1.88 and net income $386 million, down 3.6% year on year. Historical credit quality and current funding resilience are different questions. [S1]

### Underwriting distinction

The economic unit to value is the cash-producing franchise, not its stock-market category. Separate evidence of repeat customer behavior from a claim that those customers cannot leave. The affirmative interpretation here is an analyst's case, not a representation that Berkshire endorsed it. Costs needed to retain customers, renew products and maintain legal or regulatory permission belong in the economics even when reported in different accounting lines. A minority interest also lacks the ability to redesign the business simply because the owner has permanent capital. [Analytical interpretation of S1]

For diligence, identify which activity earns the customer relationship, which assets must be replaced, and which liabilities could consume the cash before it reaches common equity. The facts below define the scale of the existing enterprise. They do not convert management's expectations into a demonstrated ten-year record. [Analytical interpretation of S1]
