## Cutoff evidence — reconstructed

The March 1 close is now recovered, but a synchronized current diluted share count is not. The tables therefore distinguish the observed price, historical annual earnings multiple and explicit valuation sensitivities using June 1998 balance/share data. The trailing fiscal-year EPS of $1.67 and the latest quarter's $0.73 use their source share bases; later split-adjusted quotes must not be mixed with them. The cash balance is substantial but cannot by itself establish undervaluation without continuing business value. A prospective buyer needs a conservative estimate of cash earnings beyond the present Windows and Office cycle. No valuation is inferred from the stock's future performance or from the investor's friendship with management.

## Economic reading of the supplied facts

Industry economics: software involves substantial development and selling expense but little physical manufacturing cost for another copy. Compatibility, application availability and user familiarity can support a platform. Those advantages are not guarantees: a change in computing architecture or distribution can alter which platform controls demand. Reported margins must also be considered alongside stock compensation and the cost of sustaining a development organization. Customer adoption is evidence of current usefulness, not a complete forecast of profits a decade away. Legal limits on business practices can affect the means by which a platform defends its position. A business can be excellent for customers and employees while its shares still require a careful estimate of future cash flows and purchase price.

Valuation interpretation: the relevant asset is the stream of cash that can be withdrawn over the life of the business without weakening its competitive position. A low multiple is informative only when the earnings denominator is sustainable. A high return on tangible capital can justify a premium to book value, but does not justify an unlimited purchase price.

No closing quotation is inferred from a subsequent transaction average or from the low for a later month. If a dated market price is unavailable, a precise market capitalization, initial earnings yield and implied return are unavailable as well. A negotiated security can instead be evaluated from its proposed cash payments and priority, subject to credit risk. The adverse case should reduce sustainable earnings and allow for additional capital needs rather than simply apply a smaller multiple to optimistic earnings. Interest rates, taxes and reinvestment determine the opportunity cost; no unsupported discount rate or terminal growth estimate is presented as an observed fact.
