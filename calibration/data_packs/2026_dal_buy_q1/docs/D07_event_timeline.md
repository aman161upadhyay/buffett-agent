Title: A current operating record, distinct from the pandemic episode
Source: https://ir.delta.com/news/news-details/2025/Delta-Air-Lines-Announces-September-Quarter-2025-Financial-Results/default.aspx
Date: 2025-12-31

October 9: the company releases September-quarter results, including adjusted EPS of $1.71 and approximately $6 full-year adjusted guidance. The release reports premium and loyalty gains, lower debt and a comparison benefit from the prior outage. December 31: the common share closes at $69.40. There is no completed December-quarter result in this information set. [1; historical price below]

The relevant historical warning is that airline economics can change sharply under a demand shock. That lesson does not predetermine every subsequent valuation decision, nor does a profitable present quarter erase it. There is no assumption of continuity with an old shareholder position. The analyst should weigh improved franchise mix and resilience against operating leverage, capital requirements and the price of the common stock. No later ownership announcement is included as validation, and no private portfolio-manager assignment is inferred from who runs the corporate parent. This is a fresh underwriting assessment using the latest public economics, not a replay of a past purchase.

Evidence ledger from the October 9 release: September-quarter GAAP revenue was $16.673 billion, operating income $1.684 billion, pre-tax income $1.777 billion, net income $1.417 billion and diluted EPS $2.17. Adjusted revenue was $15.2 billion, pre-tax income approximately $1.5 billion and EPS $1.71. Operating cash flow was $1.8 billion; reported free cash flow $833 million; gross capital spending approximately $1.1 billion. Debt and finance-lease obligations were $14.9 billion, adjusted net debt $15.6 billion, and liquidity $6.9 billion including $3.1 billion undrawn revolvers. Premium revenue grew 9%, loyalty revenue 9% and American Express remuneration 12% to $2 billion. Full-year adjusted EPS guidance was approximately $6 and free cash flow $3.5-$4.0 billion. [1]

Timing discipline: the chronology separates publication dates from the economic periods discussed. A result for an earlier quarter only becomes an input when released. The market close is an observation available on the stated assessment date, not evidence of a particular investor's execution. This timeline includes no presumed private conversations or unannounced agreement. There is no catalyst completion forecast here: the analyst must decide whether the available business evidence is sufficient without relying on a future disclosure to reveal someone else's decision.
