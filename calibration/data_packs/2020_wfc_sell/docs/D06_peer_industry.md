The target's 10.8% Q3 net-income/revenue ratio compares with the peer panel's materially higher margins, while its 11.4% CET1 is a separate solvency metric. D04's approximately 0.67x target tangible book versus peer 1.48x, 1.14x and 0.58x shows that a discount is not unique to this institution. These are cutoff observations, not a fair-value ranking; regulatory restrictions and remediation costs can justify different sustainable returns on the same unit of book.

Compare JPMorgan, Bank of America and other US lenders using operating efficiency, deposit retention, credit risk, capital and restrictions. General banking exposure and this bank's specific conduct record are different questions. A sector-wide recession does not erase company-specific management failures; a cheap share price does not cure them. Observable remediation, governance and customer outcomes are needed to judge recovery. [Source above.]

Technical context: a financial institution's earning assets are supported by deposits, wholesale funding, creditors and equity with different seniority. Regulatory capital cannot all be distributed to investors. Analyze credit quality, liquidity and capital separately: a solvent institution can still face a funding run, and liquid assets do not prove all credit losses are recognized. For a preferred claim, identify whether dividends are cumulative, the issuer's redemption rights and the creditor cushion ahead of the security. For common, normalize provisioning and assess return on tangible equity rather than using industrial free-cash-flow conventions. Reported book value, tangible common capital and market capitalization are different denominators. [Source above; analytical framework rather than additional issuer measurements.]

Comparison discipline: compare businesses on the source of their cash and capital required to earn it, rather than treating the nearest ticker as an exact substitute. Normalize financing and accounting differences before interpreting relative multiples. Relative cheapness alone cannot prove absolute value. Consider adding to an understandable holding, retaining liquidity, owning a business or buying the investor's own shares. The table supplies observations, not a mechanically investable industry average.

Evidence boundary: approximate share counts and calculated margins are identified in the table. Analytical comparisons do not establish any investor's private reasoning.
