## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**First purchase unknown:** 2006 year-end disclosure does not establish a 2006 initiation. Reuters reported even POSCO did not know when Berkshire had bought. Do not backfill later steel prices into the initial model. [1,3]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
October 2007 press reporting attributes the purchase to good balance sheets relative to cheap shares. This is a **later reported explanation**, not a recovered verbatim purchase-date quotation. [2]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Reported 4% ownership, $572 million cost and $1.158 billion market value at December 2006. PKX is an ADR identifier; the table's share count must not be casually assumed to be ADR units. [1]

## What happened after
Interim appreciation verified; ultimate sale/return unknown here. [1]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Price:** Cheap against financial/business value. [2]
- **Balance sheet:** Financial strength mattered. [2]
- **Geography:** Korean domicile did not preclude investment. [1–2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: cyclical commodity exposure can qualify without being a permanent consumer-brand moat. [1–2]

## Public discussion
Contemporary Reuters coverage framed the disclosure amid steel-sector consolidation and rising profits; those analyst explanations are not automatically Buffett's reasons. [3]
