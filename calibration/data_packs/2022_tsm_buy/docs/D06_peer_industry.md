The share-count column is not foundry market share. The pure-play foundry's reported Q3 advanced-node mix was 28% from 5nm and 26% from 7nm, or 54% combined (October 13 release). These wafer-revenue shares measure technology mix rather than a share of competitors' sales. A licensing and design business and an analog manufacturer differ substantially in competitive reinvestment needs.

Samsung and Intel provide comparisons for advanced manufacturing, but their product businesses and customer relationships differ from a pure foundry. Compare yields, process leadership, capacity commitments, customer trust and returns after competitive reinvestment. Geographical diversification changes cost structure as well as risk. A chip designer's capital-light margin is not a valid substitute for a foundry's owner-earnings calculation. [Source above.]

Technical context: foundry manufacturing has scale and process barriers but requires continual expensive reinvestment. Gross margin is not owner earnings after fabs, equipment, research and working capital. Distinguish leading-edge from mature processes, production location from customer location, and booked revenue from durable through-cycle utilization. A technically excellent company can have concentration risks outside management control. Overseas facilities require time, qualification and capital; they are not immediately interchangeable with an existing production cluster. An ADR may represent several ordinary shares, so EPS and price must use the same unit. A low multiple on a peak quarter is not automatically conservative. [Source above; analytical framework.]

Comparison discipline: compare businesses on cash generation and required capital rather than treating the nearest ticker as an exact substitute. Normalize financing and accounting differences before interpreting relative multiples. Relative cheapness cannot prove absolute value. The numeric table supplies observations, not an assumption that analog manufacturing, licensing and leading-edge foundry assets should earn identical returns.

Evidence boundary: shares are equity counts and node-mix shares describe the target's revenue. Neither is a measurement of industry capacity or a forecast of competitive outcomes.
