## Method and interpretation

- Work proceeded in groups of three (WFC/ATVI/OXY), three (both TSM desks/AAPL), and the remaining two (BAC/cash).
- Historical US/ADR closes and 52-week intraday ranges were recovered from Yahoo's date-filtered chart/history series. Quotes are not dividend-adjusted. The delisted target required a contemporaneous Reuters quotation instead.
- Financial records use five completed fiscal years. SEC companyfacts were checked against annual-report and earnings-release tables; later historical sources are labeled as retrievals of earlier reported periods. In particular, the November 2022 TSM desk contains no FY2022 annual results.
- FRED's historical CSV/ZIP download supplied DGS10 and, for the cash desk, DGS3MO/DGS2/HY OAS. S&P index closes come from Yahoo; Multpl monthly trailing P/E observations are explicitly dated. Midmonth desks use a preceding completed monthly observation, not that month's eventual valuation.
- Market capitalizations, enterprise values, yields and peer multiples are approximate desk arithmetic. Bank EV and industrial FCF are not meaningful substitute metrics; bank tables use NII, provisions, CET1, credit quality, P/B, P/TBV and ROTCE. Tangible-capital proxies elsewhere state their denominators and are not misrepresented as standardized reported company metrics.
- No current forward estimate was backdated. Each equity desk explicitly records unsuccessful archival-consensus checks at three distinct publishers: Yahoo analyst estimates, Macrotrends P/E history and StockAnalysis statistics. These pages supplied no independently usable cutoff consensus; this does not mean no analyst consensus existed at the time.
