## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**Early 1988, reconstructed from the 1991 letter:** Buffett and Munger understood Fannie Mae from earlier ownership, judged David Maxwell to have solved inherited problems, and wanted a large commitment. Buffett visited Maxwell to ensure the company would be comfortable with the stake. No original valuation model or then-public financial snapshot was recovered. The later government-sponsored-enterprise crisis must not contaminate this dated decision. [1]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
“In early 1988, we decided to buy 30 million shares (adjusted for a subsequent split) ... which would have been a $350-$400 million investment.” **Hindsight**, February 28, 1992; ellipsis removes the company-name clause, not a qualifier. [1]

“In frustration, I stopped buying.” Buffett says the stock began rising after about 7 million shares had been acquired. “I wish I could give you a halfway rational explanation for my amateurish behavior vis-a-vis Fannie Mae. But there isn't one.” [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Bought about 7 million shares, stopped as the price climbed, and then sold the small position because Buffett disliked small holdings. Exact purchase/sale months and realized gain on those actual shares are unknown. **This was not a pure zero-purchase omission**: the error was incomplete sizing followed by an illogical exit. [1]

## What happened after
Buffett estimated about $1.4 billion gain not earned as of year-end 1991 relative to the intended investment. That is a dated counterfactual, not an actual loss or a promise that holding indefinitely through future decades would remain desirable. He contrasted the mistake with continuing Coca-Cola purchases when its price rose during accumulation. [1]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Knowledge:** earlier ownership made the business familiar rather than unknowable. [1]
- **Manager:** Maxwell's record was a specific reason for confidence. [1]
- **Sizing:** the intended 30 million shares show conviction; the actual 7 million must not be reported as the planned size. [1]
- **Failure mechanism:** anchoring and distaste for small positions explain the deviation; they are not valid sell drivers. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: a first-class divergence case. A faithful model should complete a still-attractive intended position rather than reproduce an admitted irrational error as if it were sound discipline. The `loss` label is opportunity-cost failure, not an asserted negative realized return. [1]

## Public discussion
No contemporary 1988 public pitch was recovered. The 1991 letter is an explicit self-postmortem, stronger evidence than later viral summaries that confuse the estimated amount or imply no shares were ever bought. [1]
