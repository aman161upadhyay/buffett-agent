Title: Business economics and ownership claim
Source: https://www.ibm.com/investor/att/pdf/IBM_Annual_Report_2010.pdf (published 2011-02-22)
Date: 2011-03-31

IBM supplies enterprise information technology: services, middleware, systems, maintenance and financing. Its relationships with large organizations extend across critical installed systems and ongoing operational needs. Replacing an integrated supplier can entail retraining, migration and execution risk. Those switching frictions are evidence for analysis, not proof that all technology spending is permanently captive or that cloud architectures cannot alter the economics. [Source above.]

Technical context: enterprise technology can contain long contractual relationships and recurring maintenance but still require persistent innovation. Customer switching costs are economic only if the supplier keeps delivering acceptable value. Distinguish backlog from cash, software licenses from support, and services revenue from labor-adjusted profit. Financing receivables can distort industrial cash-flow comparisons. Research expenditure, acquisitions, pension contributions and stock compensation belong in a conservative assessment of owner economics. A share repurchase increases continuing ownership only at a cost, and is beneficial only relative to the value acquired. Per-share growth produced by fewer shares must be reconciled with total earnings and capital spent. [Source above; analytical framework.]

Underwriting questions: identify the actual paying customer, repeat-purchase or replacement mechanism, and what prevents a capable competitor from taking the profit. Distinguish an enduring economic advantage from an asset that is merely expensive to build. The ownership claim being evaluated must match the economics described: operating control, common shares and a preferred claim confer different rights. Do not infer honest or capable management solely from a famous name or a long corporate history. Where direct customer-retention or pricing evidence is absent, leave that criterion uncertain rather than supply a generic franchise label. Record what observable deterioration would invalidate the business thesis before considering position size.

Evidence boundary: missing figures are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered for this digest. Analytical questions are not additional reported facts or claims about the investor's private thinking. Read the financial, risk and fund-context documents together; no individual accounting ratio supplies a complete investment conclusion.
