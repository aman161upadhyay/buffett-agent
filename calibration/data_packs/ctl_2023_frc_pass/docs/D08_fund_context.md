### Dated operating baseline

Full-year revenue was $5.9 billion, up 16.5%; net interest income $4.8 billion, up 17.5%; and net income approximately $1.7 billion, up 12.7%. Diluted EPS was $8.25, up 7.4%. Common book value was $75.38 and tangible common book value $74.19 per share. Loans were $166.9 billion, up 23.6%, while deposits were $176.4 billion, up 12.9%. Wealth-management assets were $271.2 billion; they belong to clients and are not bank liquidity. Wealth revenues were $877 million. Tier 1 leverage ratio was 8.51%. Reported nonperforming assets were just five basis points of total assets, and annual net charge-offs $2.9 million. Fourth-quarter net interest margin fell to 2.45% from 2.71% in the preceding quarter. Fourth-quarter EPS was $1.88 and net income $386 million, down 3.6% year on year. ### Underwriting distinction

The denominator is capital available to Berkshire, not the issuer's enterprise value or an outside asset manager's client AUM. Insurance float carries obligations; reported cash is not all surplus. A new security should be compared with existing portfolio opportunities, retaining liquidity for operating subsidiaries and negotiated whole-business purchases. No leverage at the parent is assumed to make the proposal fit. The question is an incremental allocation within a large permanent-capital enterprise. [Analytical interpretation of S3]
