Title: Comparable profit does not eliminate capital demands
Source: https://ml.globenewswire.com/Resource/Download/3ef69300-da43-4e07-9468-985051ddf0ed
Date: 2026-03-31

The quarter's comparable EPS of $3.06 excludes items that affect comparability; reported EPS is $2.88. Organic sales exclude divested businesses, while reported sales include their comparison-period contribution. Mixing organic growth with reported absolute earnings would distort the economic bridge. Beer remains highly profitable at a 38.0% operating margin, yet absolute operating income declined approximately 1%. [1]

Year-to-date free cash flow of $1.45 billion is reported after the company's investment definition, not a claim that annual cash flow is already complete. The company guides to $1.3-$1.4 billion full-year free cash flow and $1.2 billion capital expenditure. It also reports share repurchases and an investment-grade rating with a roughly 3.0-times comparable net leverage target. Those commitments compete for cash. A brand franchise with brewery expansion requirements is different from a royalty stream requiring little capital. No precise maintenance/growth split or independently reconstructed normal-cycle owner-earnings figure is supplied. [1]

Evidence ledger from the January 7 release, covering the November quarter: total net sales were $2.223 billion, down 10% reported but 2% organically; reported EPS $2.88 and comparable EPS $3.06. Beer net sales of $2.0097 billion fell 1%; shipments fell 2.2%, depletions 3.0%, and beer operating income was $763.5 million. Beer operating margin was 38.0%. Modelo Especial depletions fell about 4%, Corona Extra nearly 9%, while Pacifico and Victoria grew over 15% and 13%. Year-to-date operating cash flow was $2.1 billion and free cash flow $1.45 billion. Annual comparable EPS guidance remained $11.30-$11.60; reported guidance was $9.72-$10.02. Guided capital spending was approximately $1.2 billion, including approximately $1.0 billion for Mexican beer operations. Comparable net leverage target was approximately 3.0 times. [1]

Accounting discipline: all dollar amounts in this document retain the period and basis given in the evidence ledger. Revenue is not owner earnings; neither adjusted profit nor operating cash flow alone measures distributable cash. Maintenance investment, working capital, compensation dilution and financing claims must be considered separately. A quarter is not a full economic cycle. Where a stand-alone balance sheet, maintenance-capital split or historical consensus series is absent, it is unavailable rather than assumed to be zero or inferred from a later filing.
