## Cutoff evidence — reconstructed

A precise July 1 close and original issue price for a contemplated convertible preferred were not recovered. The investment opportunity includes distressed common and potential preferred participation; terms must be evaluated separately. Common value is residual after claims, creditors and new capital. A conversion right does not protect principal unless the preferred's dividend, seniority and solvency are credible. No numerical yield or conversion premium is invented. The old direct-distribution earning power can inform a recovery case only after subtracting the reserve and capital deficiencies. A complete rescue valuation therefore requires an explicit survival financing assumption and dilution estimate, both currently unresolved in the recovered record.

## Economic reading of the supplied facts

Industry economics: an insurer receives premiums before all associated losses are known or paid. The investment balance generated by that delay is economically valuable only when the eventual underwriting cost is acceptable. Inadequate prices or underestimated reserves can create reported profits that do not exist economically. A direct seller can avoid agency commissions, but saving distribution costs does not compensate for accepting unsuitable risks at inadequate rates. Regulatory capital and claims-paying ability constrain the amount that can be invested in equities or distributed to owners. Reinsurance adds counterparty exposure and can transfer risk without eliminating uncertainty about its measurement. This is a description of insurance mechanics, not a claim that any reported reserve balance is sufficient.

Valuation interpretation: the relevant asset is the stream of cash that can be withdrawn over the life of the business without weakening its competitive position. A low multiple is informative only when the earnings denominator is sustainable. A high return on tangible capital can justify a premium to book value, but does not justify an unlimited purchase price.

No closing quotation is inferred from a subsequent transaction average or from the low for a later month. If a dated market price is unavailable, a precise market capitalization, initial earnings yield and implied return are unavailable as well. A negotiated security can instead be evaluated from its proposed cash payments and priority, subject to credit risk. The adverse case should reduce sustainable earnings and allow for additional capital needs rather than simply apply a smaller multiple to optimistic earnings. Interest rates, taxes and reinvestment determine the opportunity cost; no unsupported discount rate or terminal growth estimate is presented as an observed fact.
