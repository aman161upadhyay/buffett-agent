## Peer multiples

The four carrier rows above are the relevant peer set: for each member there are three independently reported competitors. No synthetic single basket multiple or net-debt figure is asserted. Lease obligations must be reconciled with debt before comparing enterprise values across these carriers.

Exact October 31 closes for all four names and consistently adjusted earnings denominators are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered here. The basket must not be valued by simply averaging four P/E ratios. Use market-value-weighted sustainable earnings, include leases and debt, and subtract fleet-maintenance investment. An apparently cheap multiple can be a warning about peak margins or unavoidable future aircraft expenditure. [Source above.]

Technical context: airline economics have high fixed and semi-fixed costs and perishable capacity. Load factor, fare, ancillary revenue and unit cost should be read together; fuller aircraft can still produce weak returns if prices fall. Aircraft leases, pension liabilities and committed fleet purchases belong in the capital-risk assessment. Low fuel prices improve a period's profit but may induce competitive fare cuts. A four-company basket reduces single-carrier concentration but retains shared exposure to travel demand, fuel, labor and credit availability. Earnings before aircraft ownership costs can exaggerate cash available to common owners. [Source above; analytical framework, not an aggregate forecast.]

Valuation discipline: use a consistent claim and denominator. Enterprise value should be compared with cash generation before financing; common value should be compared with cash available after senior claims. Explicitly distinguish sustainable earnings from annualizing one quarter. A current quote from a different date, an eventual transaction price and an investor's later disclosed average cost are not substitutes for the cutoff price. State a range of operating assumptions, compare the result with liquid alternatives and require a margin of safety that survives a less favorable case. When a current input is unavailable, leave the numerical valuation incomplete instead of embedding an untraceable estimate.

Evidence boundary: missing figures are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered for this digest. Analytical questions are not additional reported facts or claims about the investor's private thinking. Read the financial, risk and fund-context documents together; no individual accounting ratio supplies a complete investment conclusion.
