## Cutoff evidence — reconstructed

The reconstructed 1990 purchase setting is less than five times after-tax earnings and less than three times pretax earnings; these are purchase-window descriptions, not a verified October 24 close. A low multiple can be meaningless if provisions are inadequate. An analyst stress calculation on the rounded figures is illustrative: trouble in 10% of $48 billion loans with 30% principal loss implies about $1.44 billion loss exposure before precise timing, interest and existing provision adjustments. The arithmetic is a scenario, not a forecast or a claim that every loan is independently marked. Exact original stress-model inputs at this cutoff and the current quote remain unrecovered.

## Economic reading of the supplied facts

Industry economics: a bank's assets consist largely of promises to repay. Small changes in the value of loans can have large effects on a leveraged common-equity base. Deposits provide funding, but do not make credit losses disappear. A bank's expense discipline matters because pre-provision earnings can absorb losses before capital is impaired. Commercial-property lending requires attention to borrower equity, collateral and refinancing rather than merely the last reported interest payment. Reported earnings can be overstated when loss provisions lag actual deterioration. Comparing banks therefore requires both operating efficiency and a conservative estimate of asset quality. These mechanics explain why a low price-to-earnings ratio alone is not sufficient evidence of a bargain, and why depositor funding is not a substitute for adequate capital.

Valuation interpretation: the relevant asset is the stream of cash that can be withdrawn over the life of the business without weakening its competitive position. A low multiple is informative only when the earnings denominator is sustainable. A high return on tangible capital can justify a premium to book value, but does not justify an unlimited purchase price.

No closing quotation is inferred from a subsequent transaction average or from the low for a later month. If a dated market price is unavailable, a precise market capitalization, initial earnings yield and implied return are unavailable as well. A negotiated security can instead be evaluated from its proposed cash payments and priority, subject to credit risk. The adverse case should reduce sustainable earnings and allow for additional capital needs rather than simply apply a smaller multiple to optimistic earnings. Interest rates, taxes and reinvestment determine the opportunity cost; no unsupported discount rate or terminal growth estimate is presented as an observed fact.
