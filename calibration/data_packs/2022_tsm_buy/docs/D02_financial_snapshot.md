A transparent tangible-capital proxy is net PP&E plus inventory plus receivables less trade payables, excluding cash and goodwill. Using 2021 balances of approximately 1,975 + 193 + 198 − 49 gives NT$2,317bn; EBIT × 90% / that year-end denominator is approximately 25.2%. This is not a reported company ROTC metric and omits other working-capital accounts. One ADR represents five ordinary shares; neither a 25.93bn ordinary share count nor NT$23.01 EPS can be paired directly with the dollar ADR price.

The October 13 release reports third-quarter revenue of NT$613.14 billion and net income of NT$280.87 billion. US-dollar revenue was $20.23 billion, up 35.9% year on year. Gross margin was 60.4%, operating margin 50.6% and net margin 45.8%. Diluted EPS was NT$10.83, or $1.79 per ADR unit. Ordinary-share and ADR earnings must not be mixed. [Source above.]

Technical context: foundry manufacturing has scale and process barriers but requires continual expensive reinvestment. Gross margin is not owner earnings after fabs, equipment, research and working capital. Distinguish leading-edge from mature processes, production location from customer location, and booked revenue from durable through-cycle utilization. A technically excellent company can have concentration risks outside management control. Overseas facilities require time, qualification and capital; they are not immediately interchangeable with an existing production cluster. An ADR may represent several ordinary shares, so EPS and price must use the same unit. A low multiple on a peak quarter is not automatically conservative. [Source above; analytical framework.]

Accounting discipline: the figures stated above are reported observations unless expressly labeled arithmetic. A period-end balance is not a flow and a market mark is not purchase cost. Do not mix fiscal years, currencies, share bases or enterprise and equity values. To construct owner earnings, begin with the relevant reported profit, reconcile noncash items and subtract the investment required to maintain economic position. Changes in working capital and recurring compensation remain economic costs even when a convenient adjusted metric excludes them. If maintenance investment, leverage or the proper share denominator is missing, state that the yield cannot be completed rather than present false precision.

Evidence boundary: financial records end in 2021; no annual 2022 results are backdated. Return proxies and historical reported figures are distinguished, and no individual accounting ratio supplies a complete investment conclusion.
