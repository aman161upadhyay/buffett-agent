## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**December 2000:** An LBO transaction had failed December 8, creating an opportunity for another buyer. The original purchase model and legal allocation of historic asbestos obligations require additional documents; do not assume uncapped liability acceptance. [1]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
The business “has earned good, if cyclical, returns.” **February 2001 account:** Buffett and Charlie offered cash with no financing contingency immediately after the prior LBO fell through. They persuaded CEO Jerry Henry to stay rather than retire. Exact entry multiple and complete liability model remain unknown. [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Agreed in late December 2000 to buy Johns Manville for about $1.8 billion. The 2001 letter explicitly says the transaction agreed in 2000 was completed in 2001. [1–2]

The prior deal failed Friday December 8; Buffett and Munger called trust chairman Bob Felise the following Monday and obtained tentative acceptance Tuesday. The victim trust's desire to diversify and Berkshire's funding certainty were important transaction conditions. [1]
