# Warren Buffett / Berkshire: decision-maker profile

**Research cutoff: 2026-10-25.** This archive distinguishes Buffett's own explanation, a Berkshire disclosure, and an outside analyst's interpretation. No holding is assigned to Buffett merely because it appears on Berkshire's 13F. Dates in companion notes are information dates, not necessarily trade dates. See letters/README.md for the primary-source register.

## Identity and mandate

Buffett describes Berkshire shareholders as owner-partners and management as managing partners, not a fund collecting assets for their own sake. The objective is growth in **intrinsic value per share**, not corporate size, reported EPS, or a target stock price. Whole businesses and minority stocks compete for capital; accounting consolidation does not make the former economically superior. [1983 letter, 1984-03-14, opening owner principles](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1983.html).

Insurance makes Berkshire different from a closed-end equity fund: policyholder liabilities create float, but claims must be payable through extreme stress. Underwriting discipline and liquidity are part of the investment process, not cash drag to optimize away. National Indemnity's 1967 acquisition and the contrast between favorable insurance economics and textile headwinds are explained in the [1977 letter, 1978-03-14, Insurance Underwriting](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1977.html). Float is **not free equity**; see the [1992 letter, 1993-03-01, Insurance Operations](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1992.html).

## Decision rights, not celebrity attribution

- **Buffett:** Sanborn/Dempster controls; Berkshire acquisition; IBM thesis and revision; Apple scaling; airlines responsibility; Paramount responsibility. For the latter he explicitly said he was responsible: [2024-05-04 meeting, afternoon, Paramount question; posted May 6](https://buffett.cnbc.com/video/2024/05/06/afternoon-session---2024-meeting.html).
- **Combs / Weschler:** separate delegated portfolios. The 2011 letter says each would manage a few billion dollars in 2012, not that they were joint co-CEOs. Initial Apple buying preceded Buffett's own late-2016 buying; do not assign the initial deputy purchase to an identified individual without a source. [2011 letter, opening developments](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2011ltr.pdf); [2020 letter, Family Jewels](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2020ltr.pdf).
- **Abel:** CEO from 2026-01-01; Buffett remained chairman at the time of the first Abel letter. Author of the 2026 letter is Abel, **not Buffett**. A purchase announced in 2025 and discussed by Abel in 2026 is not thereby an Abel-originated trade. [2026 proxy](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/meet01/2026proxystatement.pdf); [2025 annual letter, 2026-02-28, opening and Capital Discipline](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2025ltr.pdf).
- **unknown:** default for unauthenticated 13F-level attribution, including small newer names. Portfolio size or a news headline is not evidence of the individual decider. This is an archive labeling convention, not a claim that Berkshire lacks internal records.

## Size, concentration, holding period: no invented universal limits

The 1963 letter reports 71.7% ownership of Dempster; **issuer ownership is not portfolio weight**. The 1977 letter accepts very large selected equity positions without a control intention. The 2011 buyback policy had a 110%-of-book purchase ceiling; the 2018 policy removed the then-120%-of-book ceiling. None supplies a permanent 10%-position rule. [1963 interim, Dempster](https://www.buffett-letters.com/en/02-letters/partnership/1963-Partnership-Letter-(1)); [1977, Insurance Investments](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1977.html); [2018-07-17 release](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/news/jul1718.pdf).

Comparable phase-average AUM, number of positions, median holding period and independently defined trade hit rate are **unknown in this archive**. Berkshire net worth, consolidated cash, insurance float and 13F equity market value are not interchangeable AUM measures. Permanent ownership language for controlled businesses does not prevent selling public equities, exiting a mistaken thesis, or shutting textiles. [1983 owner principles](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1983.html); [1989 Mistakes of the First Twenty-five Years](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1989.html).

## Replay contract

Retrieve evidence by publication date. A 1993 Dexter replay must see the favorable acquisition account, not the 2007 admission that its advantage disappeared. A 2011 IBM replay must see stickiness and buyback arithmetic, not the 2017 reassessment. A 2024 Apple trim replay may cite the contemporaneous tax explanation but may not attribute that explanation automatically to Bank of America. The later account is a labeled postmortem, never an entry input. [1993 letter, Dexter Shoe](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1993.html); [2007, Great/Good/Gruesome](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2007ltr.pdf); [2011-11-14 IBM interview](https://www.cnbc.com/2011/11/14/cnbc-transcript-warren-buffett-explains-why-he-bought-107b-of-ibm-stock-part-5.html); [2017-05-04 IBM revision](https://www.cnbc.com/2017/05/04/warren-buffett-has-revalued-ibm-downward-cites-big-strong-competitors.html); [2024 meeting, Apple question](https://buffett.cnbc.com/video/2024/05/06/morning-session---2024-meeting.html).
