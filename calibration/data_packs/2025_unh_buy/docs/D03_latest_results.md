Title: April results, then a May suspension
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176625000022/a2024q4exhibit991.htm ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176625000123/a2025q1exhibit991.htm ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176625000134/exhibit991.htm ; https://www.unitedhealthgroup.com/newsroom/2025/2025-05-14-response-may-14-wsj-article.html
Date: 2025-06-30

The latest complete quarterly release available is April 17. Management cut its outlook to $24.65-$25.15 GAAP EPS and $26.00-$26.50 adjusted EPS. It attributed the reset to heightened physician and outpatient utilization in Medicare Advantage, and to unexpectedly limited prior beneficiary engagement affecting Optum Health reimbursement. These are concrete operating explanations, rather than a generic claim of market volatility. The company initially described the problems as addressable. [2]

On May 13 the board appointed Stephen Hemsley CEO after Andrew Witty stepped down, effective immediately; the related SEC exhibit was filed May 14. Hemsley previously ran the company from 2006 to 2017 and remained chairman. More importantly for an analyst, the company suspended its outlook because care activity continued accelerating and broadened beyond the benefit categories evident in the first quarter. April guidance is therefore superseded. There is no second-quarter earnings release in the information set. Any estimate of restored profitability must be labeled an assumption. [3]

Evidence ledger, with dollar amounts in billions unless stated otherwise: the January 16 release reports 2024 revenue of $400.3, versus $371.6 in 2023, operating earnings of $32.3, operating cash flow of $24.2, GAAP EPS of $15.51 and adjusted EPS of $27.66. The April 17 release reports first-quarter revenue of $109.6, up $9.8, GAAP EPS of $6.85 and adjusted EPS of $7.20. UnitedHealthcare added 780,000 consumers year to date. April's reduced adjusted annual EPS range was $26.00-$26.50; the May leadership announcement suspended the outlook, so that range is no longer current guidance. The May statement disputes the press characterization of a supposed criminal investigation. These are issuer statements, not independent legal findings. [1-4]

Interpretation: the issuer's latest released quarter is the information boundary, not the quarter containing this assessment date. Forward guidance is management's expectation, not a realized result. Growth percentages must be matched to the stated comparison period and accounting basis. Where adjusted measures differ from GAAP, both are retained rather than mixing the favorable numerator from one with the denominator from the other. A favorable quarter does not settle the durability question; the operational causes and cash conversion still require scrutiny.
