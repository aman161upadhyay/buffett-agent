# Activision Blizzard — Microsoft merger arbitrage

Research cutoff: **2026-10-25**. Month-format dates are documented windows, not invented execution days. Unknown means an evidence gap. Where return is unknown, outcome class `flat` is an **unscored schema sentinel, not a claim of zero return**.

## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: 2022-01-17 for the pre-deal investment; post-January 18 for the arbitrage overlay.** A deputy had already bought about 15M shares in Q4 2021 before Microsoft announced the transaction. Those original shares were not Buffett’s merger-arbitrage purchase. [1]

**Snapshot gaps:** Entry-day audited valuation, trading range and independently documented consensus: unknown; do not substitute the first 13F mark for purchase cost. Financial/consensus gaps are not filled with later outcomes.

## Buffett's thesis in his own words

At the April 30 meeting Buffett explicitly said the later decision was his and described merger arbitrage. The offered $95 cash versus approximately $75.60 market price created a spread, not a risk-free return: government/regulatory intervention could block completion. [1,2]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did

Buffett disclosed about 9.5% ownership April 30. Berkshire later reduced the position; the last parent 13F is June 2023, with absence by September, before the October close. Therefore do not assume the entire peak stake earned the full closing spread. [1; filings]
