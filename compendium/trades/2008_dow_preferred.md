## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**2008 commitment / April 2009 funding:** Dow's acquisition financing occurred across the crisis boundary. This record's exact original commitment announcement and initial conversion formula remain gaps; do not date the cash investment to 2008. [1–2]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
The 2009 letter describes the five nontraded crisis securities as providing substantial income and “significant equity potential.” That is a **group-level later assessment**, not a recovered original Dow-only quotation. [2]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Invested $3 billion April 1, 2009 in 8.5% cumulative convertible perpetual preferred. The instrument is not an ordinary bond and has no ordinary scheduled maturity. [1]

## What happened after
The 2009 letter says some stock sales raised cash for Dow and Swiss Re purchases. Exact subsequent conversion/redemption return unknown here. [2]
