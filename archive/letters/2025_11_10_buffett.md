# Buffett’s 10 November 2025 shareholder message

**Information date:** 2025-11-10 (month-only where precise public release day was not independently established). **Era:** E. **Author:** Buffett. **Primary source:** [letter](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/news/nov1025.pdf). All rules below cite sections of this source; section names are used rather than fabricated PDF page numbers. Annual-period labels are not publication dates.

## Process rules and decision explanations

- Opening: Buffett says he will no longer write the annual report or talk endlessly at meetings; Thanksgiving messages continue. Going quiet does not mean all communication stops.
- Greg Abel discussion: confidence rests on operating knowledge, management and communication; succession is a transfer of responsibility, not cloning the founder’s every trade.
- Governance: incapacitated CEOs require board vigilance; Buffett admits he and Munger had failed to act in some cases. Decentralization requires deserved trust and oversight.
- Foundation gifts: distinguish personal Berkshire-share donations and estate planning from corporate stock repurchases or a view on Berkshire’s valuation.
- Historical Omaha relationships: National Indemnity, the Sun/Buffalo papers and MidAmerican are recollections, not new 2025 acquisitions.
