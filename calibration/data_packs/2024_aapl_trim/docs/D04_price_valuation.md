These platform firms are opportunity-cost comparisons, not identical hardware businesses. An existing low-basis position has a substantial tax hurdle: the federal corporate tax rate is 21%, before state and lot-specific effects. A hypothetical future tax-rate rise is a scenario, not enacted law. Compare deferral with present after-tax redeployment and concentration risk. [Source above.]

Technical context: a consumer ecosystem may monetize both a device and recurring services, but they are linked rather than independent annuities. Customer retention, replacement frequency, pricing and the required cost of innovation determine owner economics. Separate hardware sales, installed-base activity and services revenue, avoiding double-counting value from the same customer relationship. Cash returned through buybacks must be compared with share issuance and purchase price; authorization is not execution. Foreign-held cash, taxes and operating liquidity needs affect deployable excess capital. Use one split basis for price, shares and EPS, since mismatched adjustments can make an otherwise correct earnings multiple meaningless. [Source above; analytical framework.]

Valuation discipline: use a consistent claim and denominator. Enterprise value should be compared with cash generation before financing; common value should be compared with cash available after senior claims. Explicitly distinguish sustainable earnings from annualizing one quarter. A current quote from a different date, an eventual transaction price and an investor's later disclosed average cost are not substitutes for the cutoff price. State a range of operating assumptions, compare the result with liquid alternatives and require a margin of safety that survives a less favorable case. When a current input is unavailable, leave the numerical valuation incomplete instead of embedding an untraceable estimate.

Evidence boundary: historical price and trailing accounting multiples are recovered; numerical intrinsic value and a future corporate-tax regime are not inferred from them. No later price or operating result enters the table.
