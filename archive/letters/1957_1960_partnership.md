# Partnership 1957–1960: Sanborn evidence note

**Information date:** 1961-01-30 (month-only where precise public release day was not independently established). **Era:** A. **Author:** Buffett. **Primary source:** [letter](https://www.buffett-letters.com/en/02-letters/partnership/1960-Partnership-Letter). All rules below cite sections of this source; section names are used rather than fabricated PDF page numbers. Annual-period labels are not publication dates.

## Process rules and decision explanations

- Sanborn: distinguish map-operating value from quoted securities value; realizable portfolio economics, not a generic low-P/E thesis.
- Governance determines realization. Buffett and associates accumulated influence, then negotiated an exchange allowing holders to take securities at fair value.
- Tax and retained reserves matter: a gross portfolio discount is not net distributable value.
- Dempster’s later operating/cash conversion story belongs to July 1963, not the January 1961 information set.

## Verbatim evidence anchors

- **Sanborn Map; Purchase of a large block of stock from the widow of a deceased Sanborn president.** “Shortly thereafter we made a bid to his mother for her block of stock, which was accepted. At the time there were two other large holdings”
- **Sanborn Map; Open market purchases to increase stake and facilitate a separation of the investment portfolio from the map business.** “Subsequently our holdings (including associates) were increased through open market purchases to about 24,000 shares and the total represented by the three groups increased to 46,000 shares.”
- **Sanborn Map; Strategic decision to separate the investment portfolio from the map business to unlock value.** “We hoped to separate the two businesses, realize the fair value of the investment portfolio and work to re-establish the earning power of the map business.”
- **Sanborn Map; Execution of an exchange plan allowing stockholders to exit at fair value using portfolio securities.** “a plan was evolved taking out all stockholders at fair value who wanted out. The SEC ruled favorably on the fairness of the plan.”

## Attribution and point-in-time boundary

Buffett is the source author, but an acquired subsidiary’s bolt-on or operating action is not automatically his personally originated investment. Individual decider unknown where not identified. Historical narratives are available only from this information date. A section discussing earlier purchases is retrospective; do not load its later performance, confession or corrected valuation into the original entry replay.

## Coverage limitation

This is a decision-process extraction, not a full transcription or a complete portfolio ledger. Numbers outside the source passage are not estimated. The Sanborn analysis is specifically supported by the 1960 report. The 1957 letter independently states: “I make no attempt to forecast the general market - my efforts are devoted to finding undervalued securities.” [1957 report, “The General Stock Market Picture in 1957,” exact release day unverified; Ivey compilation](https://www.ivey.uwo.ca/media/2975913/buffett-partnership-letters.pdf). This separates a valuation judgment about the market from an ability to forecast its direction. The 1958–1959 letters were not fully extracted in this pass.
