## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**1996:** KBS served banks across 22 states. A January social encounter with Roy Dinsdale led to financial information February 12 and Buffett's offer the next day. [1]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
An “extraordinary underwriting record” under an “extraordinary manager.” Towle thought of the business as “his,” an attitude Buffett valued. **February 1997 retrospective.** [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Offered $75 million February 13 and subsequently bought the insurer, placing it with Wesco given its smaller size and Wesco's insurance ambitions. [1]

## What happened after
