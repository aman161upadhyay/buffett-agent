# Buffett / Berkshire compendium — 2010–2026

**As of 2026-10-25.** 73 case files; 174 distinct reported 13F CUSIPs across all 66 quarter-ends from Q1 2010 through Q2 2026; six complete 2025–26 quarterly holdings/change ledgers. This index is a source map, not a claim that every historical rationale or investment return is knowable.

## Attribution and date rules

**Ownership ≠ thesis ≠ stock picker.** The SEC other-manager name Warren Buffett is not evidence that he originated every security. Combs is directly documented for Mastercard/Visa (Carol Loomis) and Snowflake execution (signed agreement); Weschler for later DaVita stewardship; joint DirecTV attribution cannot be squeezed into one name. Apple begins with an unnamed deputy in contemporary reporting, then Buffett scales; later Weschler reporting is clearly retrospective. Paramount is explicitly Buffett’s mistake, not a deputy’s by default.

**Combs is not a current Berkshire picker in 2026.** December 8, 2025 Berkshire release says he resigned to join JPMorgan. JPMorgan’s April 2026 proxy says he joined January 2026; the exact last Berkshire working day is not independently specified. December 7 was the effective JPMorgan **board** resignation, not proof of Berkshire employment termination that day. Abel’s February letter says Weschler manages a portion formerly overseen by Combs. [Governance case](trades/2026_abel_capital_allocation.md).

**Buffett remained involved.** July 2026 he said he initiated Alphabet, with Abel approval. September 18, 2026 Howard Buffett became chairman; Warren became chairman emeritus and remained a director. Neither event makes every 2026 stock trade personally Abel’s. [Alphabet](trades/2025_alphabet.md), [transition](trades/2026_abel_capital_allocation.md).

Exact execution days and costs are unknown unless directly disclosed. A 13F quarter-end mark is not invested capital, a sale receipt or P&L. First/last census quarters are censored observations, not necessarily first-ever purchase/final execution. A spin-off or share-class change is not automatically a discretionary trade.

## Honest gaps / research queue

1. **Entry-day market snapshots** remain incomplete for most historical cases: independently verified trading ranges, multiples, historical financials and contemporaneous consensus. Do not use later letters as if published at entry. The point-in-time sections state the gaps.
2. **Returns**: full cash/dividend transaction ledgers are not available for most public-stock round trips; quarter-end marks cannot establish realized gains. Subsidiary IRRs are usually unknown. KHC and PCC impairment amounts are not sale proceeds.
3. **Own-words drivers**: many verified small holdings have none stated. Fewer than 3–6 drivers is intentional when the evidence is thin; they are not suitable as a complete reasoning grading key.
4. **Independent debate**: press coverage is supplied, but X/Reddit/VIC/COBF threads and independently reproduced bear models are not systematically authenticated. No made-up handles or consensus.
5. **Foreign ordinary holdings / filings**: Japan thesis and aggregate cost/value sourced, but complete 2026 five-issuer voting reports remain to be reconciled. Munich Re reductions verified through 2015; final zero balance unknown.
6. **Corporate actions**: all reported identities are preserved, but exact ratios for Liberty/Sirius, spin-offs and old CUSIP transformations are not fully mapped. Do not score CUSIP entry/exit as a trade without checking.
7. **Deal dates/scope**: exact closing-day sources for some older deals (Heinz, Alleghany, Pilot early stages, Bell) and Dominion/Questar final consideration need further reconciliation. Known windows stay month-format or unknown.
8. **Older permanently held positions** (KO, AXP, MCO, etc.) are in the all-position census, not falsely labeled 2010 initial investments. Earlier original theses belong in the pre-2010 corpus; this pass captures observed post-2010 holdings without inventing new decisions.

## Research method / source hierarchy

SEC submissions and original company letters/releases take priority, followed by full meeting transcripts and attributable interviews, then contemporaneous Reuters/CNBC/Fortune/Morningstar reporting. Direct web fetch/Firecrawl were rate-limited; Exa contents retrieved primary documents and SEC submission JSON identified filings. All 66 quarter-end reports and material amendments were parsed. Unit transitions and manager-row aggregation were explicitly reconciled. Primary/secondary conflicts (notably 2026 Lennar classes) are disclosed. No synthesized “Buffett would have” statements are presented as his words.
