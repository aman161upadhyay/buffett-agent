## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**2007 agreement:** The Pritzker family was reorganizing holdings. Marmon operated numerous industrial businesses, including Union Tank Car. File year refers to the 2008 ownership transaction, not an invented 2008 first negotiation. [1]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
“I knew that the business would be exactly as the Pritzkers represented.” **Following-year explanation:** price was negotiated from financial statements without advisors or nit-picking. [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Agreed to purchase 60% initially for $4.5 billion and virtually the balance within six years, with later prices linked to earnings. Exact initial close date unknown here. [1]

## What happened after
The 2009 letter reports a 13.5% pretax margin despite sales falling 27%, crediting Ptak's cost management. This is operating performance, not acquisition IRR. [2]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Trust:** Seller reliability and Berkshire closing certainty. [1]
- **Assets:** Established diversified industrial operations. [1]
- **People:** Ptak/Nichols's relevant operating experience. [1]
- **Structure:** Staged pricing, not one fixed price for all shares. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: a complex group can fit if management and operating economics are understandable; subsidiary count alone is not disqualifying. [1]

## Public discussion
The letter contrasts honoring this deal with other contemporaneous transactions renegotiated or abandoned. Independent opposing valuation work remains a gap. [1]
