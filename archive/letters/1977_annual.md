# 1977 Berkshire annual letter

**Information date:** 1978-03-14 (month-only where precise public release day was not independently established). **Era:** A. **Author:** Buffett. **Primary source:** [letter](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1977.html). All rules below cite sections of this source; section names are used rather than fabricated PDF page numbers. Annual-period labels are not publication dates.

## Process rules and decision explanations

- Insurance Investments: evaluate fractional stocks like whole businesses; understanding, long-term prospects, honest/competent management and very attractive price are the actual checklist.
- Capital Cities: minority stock was cheaper than buying comparable properties and retained better management than Buffett could supply. No control catalyst was required.
- Textile Operations: two years of mistaken recovery forecasts; employment, labor relations and straightforward management explain continued operation despite inadequate expected returns. This is a genuine non-maximizing qualification.
- Insurance Underwriting: National Indemnity entry and float growth; inadequate rates should cause volume contraction, not promotional growth.
- Blue Chip Stamps: See’s increased profits with little incremental capital; the operational evidence for the quality shift predates its later famous summary.

## Verbatim evidence anchors

- **Insurance Underwriting; Purchase of National Indemnity Company and National Fire and Marine Insurance Company** “It was early in 1967 that we made our entry into this industry through the purchase of National Indemnity Company and National Fire and Marine Insurance Company”
- **Insurance Underwriting; Mistakes in initiating surety operations and expanding urban auto marketing** “We experienced significant problems from (1) a surety operation initiated in 1969, (2) the 1973 expansion of Home and Automobile’s urban auto marketing into the Miami”
- **Insurance Investments; Purchase of Capital Cities Communications stock as a non-control investment** “For example, we invested $10.9 million in Capital Cities Communications during 1977. Earnings attributable to the shares we purchased totaled about $1.3 million last year.”
- **Blue Chip Stamps; Purchase of See's Candies by Blue Chip Stamps** “Since See’s was purchased by Blue Chip Stamps at the beginning of 1972, pre-tax operating earnings have grown from $4.2 million to $12.6 million with little additional capital”

## Attribution and point-in-time boundary

Buffett is the source author, but an acquired subsidiary’s bolt-on or operating action is not automatically his personally originated investment. Individual decider unknown where not identified. Historical narratives are available only from this information date. A section discussing earlier purchases is retrospective; do not load its later performance, confession or corrected valuation into the original entry replay.
