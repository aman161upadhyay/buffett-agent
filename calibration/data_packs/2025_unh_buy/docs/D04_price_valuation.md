Title: A market price against withdrawn guidance
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176625000022/a2024q4exhibit991.htm ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176625000123/a2025q1exhibit991.htm ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176625000134/exhibit991.htm ; https://www.unitedhealthgroup.com/newsroom/2025/2025-05-14-response-may-14-wsj-article.html
Date: 2025-06-30

The June 30 unadjusted close was $311.97, using the historical daily-price observation rather than a dividend-adjusted series. At that price, 2024 adjusted EPS of $27.66 gives 11.28 times earnings, while reported EPS of $15.51 gives 20.11 times. Those two calculations describe different accounting bases and neither is a verified forward multiple. The price observation is cited separately below; no subsequent Berkshire filing is used as a price source.

Dividing by April's $26.00-$26.50 adjusted forecast would give roughly 11.77-12.00 times, but that range was withdrawn in May and is not a valid current earnings estimate. A superficially low multiple can therefore reflect a denominator the market no longer believes. A full valuation needs a conservative medical-cost and repricing bridge. There is no independently recovered June consensus EPS, historical multiple range, verified maintenance-capital split or Berkshire purchase cost. Avoid valuing a recovery as if it has already been earned. [1-3]

Evidence ledger, with dollar amounts in billions unless stated otherwise: the January 16 release reports 2024 revenue of $400.3, versus $371.6 in 2023, operating earnings of $32.3, operating cash flow of $24.2, GAAP EPS of $15.51 and adjusted EPS of $27.66. The April 17 release reports first-quarter revenue of $109.6, up $9.8, GAAP EPS of $6.85 and adjusted EPS of $7.20. UnitedHealthcare added 780,000 consumers year to date. April's reduced adjusted annual EPS range was $26.00-$26.50; the May leadership announcement suspended the outlook, so that range is no longer current guidance. The May statement disputes the press characterization of a supposed criminal investigation. These are issuer statements, not independent legal findings. [1-4]

Valuation discipline: the calculations in this document are analyst arithmetic, not management targets or a Berkshire underwriting model. Earnings multiples, book multiples and cash-flow yields answer different questions and are not interchangeable. A low multiple offers no protection if normalized earning power has been overstated. Conversely, growth has value only after the incremental capital required to obtain it. No verified contemporary consensus forecast or complete historical multiple distribution is supplied; an exact intrinsic value should therefore not be reverse-engineered from a subsequent transaction.
