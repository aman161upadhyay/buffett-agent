Title: Segment pre-tax profit is not distributable cash
Source: https://www.oxy.com/siteassets/documents/investors/2024-annual-report.pdf (2025-02 annual filing, FY2022–2024 tables); https://www.oxy.com/siteassets/documents/investors/oxy-annual-report-2021.pdf (2022-03 annual report, FY2020–2021 tables); https://www.oxy.com/news/news-releases/occidental-announces-2nd-quarter-2025-results (2025-08-06); other dated sources [1–4] below
Date: 2025-10-01

The verified earnings observations demonstrate profitability but also cyclical variability. A $436 million quarter in 2023 and a $213 million quarter in the current release are not interchangeable normalized estimates. The $260 million first quarter of 2024 supplies another point, not a complete annual cycle. There is no recovered stand-alone balance sheet or clean maintenance-capital schedule for the chemical business in these releases. [1,3,4]

### Financial record (FY2020 to FY2024)

Chemical-segment figures, USD millions, from the parent's annual segment and investing-cash-flow tables; not parent oil-and-gas results.

| December year-end | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Segment net sales | 3,733 | 5,246 | 6,757 | 5,321 | 4,923 |
| Segment pretax earnings | 664 | 1,544 | 2,508 | 1,531 | 1,124 |
| Earnings / sales, calculated % | 17.8 | 29.4 | 37.1 | 28.8 | 22.8 |
| Chemical capital expenditures | 255 | 308 | 322 | 535 | 685 |
| Pretax earnings minus capex, NOT FCF | 409 | 1,236 | 2,186 | 996 | 439 |

The five-year pretax earnings average is $1,474m and capex average $421m; these are descriptive averages, not normalized forecasts. From the 2022 peak, 2024 sales declined 27.1% and pretax earnings 55.2%. Capital spending increased 112.7%, so a constant earnings-to-cash conversion is especially unsafe. The last row omits depreciation add-back, working capital, taxes, allocated financing and environmental payments; it is intentionally not called distributable cash.

The 2024 balance sheet's chemical property/plant/equipment category is $8,725m versus $8,279m in 2023, BEFORE pooled accumulated depreciation. It is not segment total assets, net invested capital or a stand-alone equity book. The parent's external-customer revenue note has a different scope from segment net sales; the table consistently uses the MD&A segment series. Stand-alone EPS, shares, dividends, debt, cash and book/share do not exist as public separate-stock metrics. A segment D&A/maintenance-capital/OCF bridge and defensible ROIC remain unavailable after checking the annual report, quarterly release and earnings-call capital discussion.

The parent reports $3.0 billion operating cash flow and $2.0 billion capital spending in the current quarter, but oil and gas dominate much of that activity. Treating the parent's free cash flow as the segment's would radically overstate the underwriting evidence. Segment pre-tax income may also differ from cash available to an owner because of depreciation, investment timing, working capital and costs allocated at the parent. A conservative purchase analysis needs explicit provision for plant integrity and environmental obligations. No zero-capex assumption or precise unlevered return on invested capital is supplied. [1,2]

Evidence ledger: Occidental's August 6 release reports OxyChem second-quarter pre-tax income of $213 million. Excluding comparability items, income was relatively unchanged sequentially; negative inventory adjustments were offset by improved export demand for caustic soda and PVC. Earlier releases report OxyChem pre-tax income of $436 million in the second quarter of 2023 and $260 million in the first quarter of 2024. These are different quarters, not an annualized normalized earnings record. For the parent as a whole, second-quarter operating cash flow was $3.0 billion and capital spending $2.0 billion; those figures must not be assigned to the chemicals segment. The August earnings call discusses about $300 million lower OxyChem spending in the following year. No stand-alone acquisition agreement or price is publicly established in this dated source set. [1-4]

Accounting discipline: all dollar amounts in this document retain the period and basis given in the evidence ledger. Revenue is not owner earnings; neither adjusted profit nor operating cash flow alone measures distributable cash. Maintenance investment, working capital, compensation dilution and financing claims must be considered separately. A quarter is not a full economic cycle. Where a stand-alone balance sheet, maintenance-capital split or historical consensus series is absent, it is unavailable rather than assumed to be zero or inferred from a later filing.
