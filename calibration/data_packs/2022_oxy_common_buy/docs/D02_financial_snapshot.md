ROTC recovery attempts: the [issuer 2020 annual report](https://www.oxy.com/siteassets/documents/investors/2020-annual-report.pdf), [Macrotrends ROIC](https://www.macrotrends.net/stocks/charts/OXY/occidental-petroleum/roic), and [StockAnalysis ratios](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/oxy/financials/ratios/) did not supply a consistent FY2017–2021 return-on-tangible-capital series. ROIC, segment returns and an after-tax tangible-capital proxy are not interchangeable definitions.

FY2021 reported pretax income from continuing operations was $3.705bn, a 14.3% sales margin before tax; adding $1.614bn interest and debt expense gives a $5.319bn EBIT proxy (20.5%). This is not management's segment operating-income measure. The 2019 acquisition and 2020 impairments break comparability. Debt-adjusted, goodwill-excluded tangible capital is approximately $33bn at 2021 year-end; EBIT × 79% / this denominator is approximately 12.7%, a simplified return-on-tangible-capital proxy, not a maintenance-capex estimate. Company FCF excluding working capital ($8.809bn) differs from cash-flow-statement FCF ($7.56bn).

The February 24 release reports fourth-quarter 2021 net income attributable to common of $1.3 billion, or $1.37 diluted EPS; adjusted income was $1.4 billion, or $1.48. Full-year company-defined free cash flow was $8.809 billion. Fourth-quarter production from continuing operations was 1,189 thousand barrels of oil equivalent per day. The non-GAAP free-cash-flow measure must be read with its reconciliation. [Source above.]

Technical context: petroleum accounting profits depend on realized prices, volumes, royalties, taxes and the treatment of exploration and development costs. A reserve is not cash in the bank: it needs extraction, transport and often replacement investment. Distinguish upstream earnings from refining and chemical margins rather than assuming they move together. For owner earnings, separate maintenance needed to sustain economic production from expansion capital. Check whether reported free cash flow deducts preferred distributions and whether acquisitions are being called capital expenditure. Proved reserves, production and reserve life use different units and assumptions. A conservative analysis should explicitly test a lower realized-price case without claiming to forecast the commodity. Accounting discipline: the figures stated above are reported observations unless expressly labeled arithmetic. A period-end balance is not a flow and a market mark is not purchase cost. Do not mix fiscal years, currencies, share bases or enterprise and equity values. To construct owner earnings, begin with the relevant reported profit, reconcile noncash items and subtract the investment required to maintain economic position. Changes in working capital and recurring compensation remain economic costs even when a convenient adjusted metric excludes them. If maintenance investment, leverage or the proper share denominator is missing, state that the yield cannot be completed rather than present false precision.

Evidence boundary: the tables identify historical presentation differences and distinguish company-defined FCF from cash-statement arithmetic. A single favorable commodity year is not a permanent owner-earnings estimate.
