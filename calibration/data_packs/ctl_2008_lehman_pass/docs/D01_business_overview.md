The franchise combines fixed-income intermediation, equity execution, underwriting, mergers advice and asset management. Client relationships, distribution and specialist trading personnel are the productive assets. A new owner would acquire more than mortgage inventory: advisory fees and assets under management can continue earning while credit markets heal. The March results show actual profits in exceptionally difficult conditions rather than a purely projected turnaround. [S1]

Citi's March 28 upgrade argues that a profitable quarter, diversified fee streams and the Federal Reserve's dealer-liquidity facilities make a confidence crisis manageable. Its $65 target assumes a 15% through-cycle ROE, an 11% cost of equity and a 1.7 times terminal book multiple. The report describes substantial upside from March 27's $38.71 close and labels the recommendation Buy/High Risk. This is a genuine contemporary affirmative thesis, not Berkshire's analysis. [S2]

### Dated operating baseline

The March 18 release reports first-quarter net revenue of $3.5 billion, down 31%; net income of $489 million, down 57%; and diluted EPS of $0.81 versus $1.96. Negative mark-to-market adjustments were $1.8 billion, net of hedges and certain debt-liability gains. Capital Markets revenue was $1.7 billion; fixed income contributed only $262 million, down 88%, while equities contributed $1.4 billion, up 6%. Investment Banking revenue was $867 million, including $330 million of advisory fees. Investment Management revenue reached $968 million and client assets under management were $277 billion. These client assets are not Lehman's own liquidity. Reported common book value was $39.45 per share; total stockholders' equity was $24.8 billion. Holding-company liquidity was $34 billion, with $64 billion of other unencumbered assets; regulated entities held another $99 billion of unencumbered assets. Quarterly ROE was 8.6%, versus 24.4% a year earlier; pre-tax margin was 18.9%. Compensation absorbed 52.5% of net revenues. [S1]

### Underwriting distinction

The economic unit to value is the cash-producing franchise, not its stock-market category. Separate evidence of repeat customer behavior from a claim that those customers cannot leave. The affirmative interpretation here is an analyst's case, not a representation that Berkshire endorsed it. Costs needed to retain customers, renew products and maintain legal or regulatory permission belong in the economics even when reported in different accounting lines. A minority interest also lacks the ability to redesign the business simply because the owner has permanent capital. [Analytical interpretation of S1]

For diligence, identify which activity earns the customer relationship, which assets must be replaced, and which liabilities could consume the cash before it reaches common equity. The facts below define the scale of the existing enterprise. They do not convert management's expectations into a demonstrated ten-year record. [Analytical interpretation of S1]
