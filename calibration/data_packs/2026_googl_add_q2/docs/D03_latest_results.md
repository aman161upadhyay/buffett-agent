Title: Accelerating Cloud growth and an extraordinary spending plan
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000012/googexhibit991q42025.htm ; https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/department-justice-wins-significant-remedies-against-google ; https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/LSB/PDF/LSB11362/LSB11362.1.pdf
Date: 2026-03-31

The February 4 release reports fourth-quarter revenue growth of 18%. Search and other revenue rises 17% to $63.073 billion; Cloud rises 48% to $17.664 billion. Cloud operating income reaches $5.313 billion versus $2.093 billion a year earlier. Services revenue grows 14%, with its segment profit $40.132 billion. These are substantial realized improvements, not merely an AI product announcement. [1]

Management also projects $175-$185 billion capital expenditure for the current year. The outlook reflects customer demand and infrastructure plans, but the release does not give a guaranteed return, depreciation schedule or payback period for every new facility. The question for an existing owner is whether growth and durable customer demand justify that commitment after competitive pressure and reinvestment. No March-quarter result is public at the cutoff. Later placements, blocks of stock and any agreed purchase discount are not assumptions in this input set. The underwriting must remain anchored to released operating economics and prospective capital needs. [1]

Evidence ledger: February 4 results show 2025 revenue of $402.836 billion versus $350.018 billion in 2024; operating income $129.039 billion versus $112.390 billion; net income $132.170 billion; diluted EPS $10.81. Fourth-quarter revenue was $113.828 billion, operating income $35.934 billion and EPS $2.82. Services revenue was $95.862 billion and Cloud $17.664 billion, up 48%; segment operating income was $40.132 billion and $5.313 billion respectively. Other Bets lost $3.617 billion, including a Waymo compensation charge, and central activities cost $5.894 billion. Cash/marketable securities totaled $126.843 billion. Management expects 2026 capital expenditures of $175-$185 billion. These plans are forward-looking; no second-quarter capital commitment by an outside investor is included. [1-3]

Interpretation: the issuer's latest released quarter is the information boundary, not the quarter containing this assessment date. Forward guidance is management's expectation, not a realized result. Growth percentages must be matched to the stated comparison period and accounting basis. Where adjusted measures differ from GAAP, both are retained rather than mixing the favorable numerator from one with the denominator from the other. A favorable quarter does not settle the durability question; the operational causes and cash conversion still require scrutiny.
