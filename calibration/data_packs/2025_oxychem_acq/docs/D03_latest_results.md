Title: Inventory adjustments and export demand offset
Source: https://www.oxy.com/siteassets/documents/investors/quarterly-earnings/oxy2q25earningspressrelease.pdf (2025-08-06, segment tables); https://www.oxy.com/siteassets/documents/investors/quarterly-earnings/oxy2q25transcript.pdf (2025-08-07); other dated sources [1–4] below
Date: 2025-10-01

The August 6 quarterly release states that OxyChem pre-tax income was $213 million and that income excluding comparability items was relatively unchanged from the first quarter. The operational explanation is specific: negative inventory adjustments offset improved export demand for caustic soda and PVC. This means a flat profit number can conceal opposing underlying movements, rather than proving a stable steady-state margin. [1]

### Latest reported quarter/half (June 2025)

| Chemical segment, USD millions | Q2 2024 | Q2 2025 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Net sales | 1,274 | 1,227 | −3.7% |
| Reported pretax income | 296 | 213 | −28.0% |
| Pretax income / sales, calculated | 23.23% | 17.36% | −5.87 percentage points |

First-half 2025 chemical net sales were $2,415m and reported pretax income $398m, a 16.48% ratio. Q1 reported pretax income was therefore $185m; Q2 increased 15.1% on a reported basis. This does not contradict management's “relatively unchanged” sequential statement, which excludes comparability items. Preserve the adjusted/reported distinction rather than quietly substituting one basis.

Against first-half 2024 pretax income of $556m (the disclosed $260m Q1 plus $296m Q2), first-half 2025 fell 28.4%. Four-times-quarter arithmetic of $852m is below both the $1,124m actual 2024 year and $1,474m five-year historical mean, but is not evidence that either baseline will recur. The release does not supply separate chemical EPS, a public share count or a stand-alone operating-cash-flow statement. Parent capital and cash-flow figures remain excluded from this segment comparison.

The August 7 call discusses a prospective reduction in OxyChem capital spending of approximately $300 million in the following year. That is a statement about future spending, not cash already earned. The analyst must distinguish completing growth projects from permanently reducing maintenance needs. The parent is also reducing debt using cash generation and divestitures, creating a capital-allocation setting in which assets have to compete for funding. None of that establishes that any particular asset is for sale or that a buyer has agreed to a price. The September quarter has no published result in this pack. [1,2]

Evidence ledger: Occidental's August 6 release reports OxyChem second-quarter pre-tax income of $213 million. Excluding comparability items, income was relatively unchanged sequentially; negative inventory adjustments were offset by improved export demand for caustic soda and PVC. Earlier releases report OxyChem pre-tax income of $436 million in the second quarter of 2023 and $260 million in the first quarter of 2024. These are different quarters, not an annualized normalized earnings record. For the parent as a whole, second-quarter operating cash flow was $3.0 billion and capital spending $2.0 billion; those figures must not be assigned to the chemicals segment. The August earnings call discusses about $300 million lower OxyChem spending in the following year. No stand-alone acquisition agreement or price is publicly established in this dated source set. [1-4]

Interpretation: the issuer's latest released quarter is the information boundary, not the quarter containing this assessment date. Forward guidance is management's expectation, not a realized result. Growth percentages must be matched to the stated comparison period and accounting basis. Where adjusted measures differ from GAAP, both are retained rather than mixing the favorable numerator from one with the denominator from the other. A favorable quarter does not settle the durability question; the operational causes and cash conversion still require scrutiny.
