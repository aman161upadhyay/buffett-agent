## Reading and source rules

1. Each note extracts specific decisions, process implications and failure cases; it is not a complete letter transcription. Verbatim anchors were matched to retrieved primary-source text after whitespace normalization. Quoted excerpts can stop mid-sentence; they must not be mistaken for the full original statement.
2. Original Berkshire texts: [official index](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/letters.html). Exa /contents retrieved the HTML/PDF sources. webFetch encountered rate limits; queryWithLLM proposed candidate excerpts, then literal matching rejected alterations.
3. Partnership texts are third-party reproductions: [Ivey compilation](https://www.ivey.uwo.ca/media/2975913/buffett-partnership-letters.pdf), [1960](https://www.buffett-letters.com/en/02-letters/partnership/1960-Partnership-Letter), [1963](https://www.buffett-letters.com/en/02-letters/partnership/1963-Partnership-Letter-(1)), [1969 dissolution](https://buffettknowledge.com/letters/1969-midyear-letter). The grouped notes are selective, not a claim every 1957–1969 letter was fully transcribed. Original contemporaneous American Express detail and unextracted intermediate years remain gaps.
4. **2008:** signature date 2009-02-27; online release 2009-02-28. Do not admit the source to a February 27 public replay simply because the signature is dated then. [Release notice, 2009-02-25](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/news/feb2509.pdf). Other month-only dates deliberately avoid asserting an unverified exact release day.
5. **2024 annual letter:** published February 22, 2025, not February 2024. **2025 annual letter:** written by Greg Abel, published February 28, 2026, not Buffett. [2026 release notice, February 12](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/news/feb1226.pdf).
6. **November 10, 2025:** separate Thanksgiving/gifts/shareholder message, not a second annual-results letter. It says Buffett will stop writing the annual report while continuing Thanksgiving messages.
7. A source author is not automatically the individual trade originator. Combs/Weschler delegated decisions, subsidiary bolt-ons and transactions inherited by Abel need distinct attribution.

## Replay discipline

Later postmortems belong in training as labeled retrospective explanations, not original entry-day data. Dexter 1993 and IBM 2011 are especially important: include the actual favorable entry reasoning and later mistake acknowledgment without mixing dates. Opinions on the cash hoard or succession from forums/press are not management testimony. See ../01_investment_principles.md and ../02_reasoning_patterns.md for cross-letter evolution and templates.
