Title: A repaired forecast, but no final annual result
Source: https://www.unitedhealthgroup.com/content/dam/UHG/PDF/investors/2025/unh-reports-third-quarter-2025-results-and-raises-full-year-2025-earnings-outlook.pdf ; https://www.unitedhealthgroup.com/newsroom/2025/2025-07-24-uhg-responds-to-doj-investigation.html ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176625000022/a2024q4exhibit991.htm ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176625000123/a2025q1exhibit991.htm
Date: 2025-12-31

The first half brought a guidance reduction, leadership change and outlook suspension. July 24 brought acknowledgement of formal Department of Justice requests. October 28 brought a modestly higher annual earnings floor alongside the latest quarterly result. December 31 supplies a $330.11 market close. These are public information checkpoints, not exact trading instructions or evidence of private portfolio discussions. [1-4; historical price below]

The assessment is therefore not the same as evaluating the initial medical-cost cloud. Revenue continues growing, the company reports positive cash generation, and earnings remain far below the previous trajectory. There is no completed annual result in the information set merely because December has ended. No later government reimbursement announcement or subsequent change in institutional ownership is assumed. The timeline supports reassessing a temporary-cloud thesis against observed operating deterioration and remaining uncertainty, while leaving the eventual direction of the existing owner's decision entirely outside the documents.

Evidence ledger: the October 28 release reports third-quarter revenue of $113.2 billion, up 12%; operating earnings of $4.3 billion, down approximately 50% year on year; net margin of 2.1%; GAAP EPS of $2.59; adjusted EPS of $2.92; and operating cash flow of $5.9 billion. Management increased its annual floor to $14.90 GAAP EPS and $16.25 adjusted EPS. That floor is substantially below April's superseded $26.00-$26.50 adjusted range and 2024 adjusted EPS of $27.66. On July 24 the company said it was complying with formal criminal and civil Department of Justice requests and expressed confidence in its practices. No legal settlement or completed annual financial result is assumed. [1-4]

Timing discipline: the chronology separates publication dates from the economic periods discussed. A result for an earlier quarter only becomes an input when released. The market close is an observation available on the stated assessment date, not evidence of a particular investor's execution. This timeline includes no presumed private conversations or unannounced agreement. There is no catalyst completion forecast here: the analyst must decide whether the available business evidence is sufficient without relying on a future disclosure to reveal someone else's decision.
