## Buffett's thesis in his own words

At the May 4 meeting Buffett said Berkshire was paying a 21% federal rate on the Apple gains it was taking and discussed possible future higher rates. This explains the publicly discussed Q1 trim, not automatically every later share sold. He continued praising Apple. [1,2]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did

Major reductions in Q2 and Q3 2024 brought shares to 300M by September. Further reductions in 2025 are enumerated below. A falling mark can reflect price; the share series establishes actual net sales. [3; filings]
