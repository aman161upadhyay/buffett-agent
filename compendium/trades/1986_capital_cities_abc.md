## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**March 1985 commitment / early 1986 funding:** Capital Cities was acquiring ABC for about $3.5 billion and needed capital. Berkshire committed to buy 3 million shares at $172.50, the price prevailing when the commitment was made. The 1985 letter, issued after funding, called media prices full and anticipated unexciting near-term ABC economics. Those expectations distinguish a patient ownership commitment from a short-term acquisition-pop trade. Exact funding month is unverified. [1,3]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
“I think it is the best of any publicly-owned company in the country.” **Post-commitment**, March 4, 1986, about Cap Cities management. [1]

“Our Cap Cities purchase was made at a full price” — same letter, which cites exceptional properties and people and the ability to participate at meaningful scale rather than an asset bargain. [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Committed during March 1985 and purchased immediately after year-end, early 1986. Voluntarily arranged for Murphy, or Burke if CEO, to vote its shares for an extended period and accepted restrictions on sale of the block. Buffett says these arrangements were initiated by Berkshire, intended to protect managers from destabilizing owners. This is a minority block, not Berkshire's acquisition of ABC itself. [1,3]

## What happened after
By 1989 the original $517.5 million cost position had a $1.692 billion market value. The 1995 letter describes the Disney merger and Buffett's premature sale of one-third in 1994 for $635 million, versus about $1.27 billion those shares would have brought in the merger. The remaining stock's transformation into cash/Disney is not a pure cash exit or a new 1966 Disney continuation. [2]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Managers:** Murphy and Burke's skill and character were repeatedly explicit. [1]
- **Properties:** a good business combination justified a full price. [1]
- **Patience:** near-term ABC integration results were not expected to be exciting. [1]
- **Governance stability:** restrictions were intended as value-enhancing commitments, despite forfeiting a potential block premium. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: quality and management at a fair/full price, not the old tangible-asset bargain. The prior **1977–1980** Capital Cities position is a separate trade; Buffett explicitly regretted having sold it at $43. [1]

## Public discussion
The letter acknowledges media-market enthusiasm approaching mania in some property deals. Original transaction proxy and outside integration objections remain unrecovered. Commitment date, funding date and subsequent-event disclosure must not be conflated. [1,3]
