# 2011 Berkshire annual letter

**Information date:** 2012-02 (month-only where precise public release day was not independently established). **Era:** C. **Author:** Buffett. **Primary source:** [letter](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2011ltr.pdf). All rules below cite sections of this source; section names are used rather than fabricated PDF page numbers. Annual-period labels are not publication dates.

## Process rules and decision explanations

- Share Repurchases: 110%-of-book threshold was a conservative dated proxy for value; Berkshire bought only while price was available, not to meet a cash deployment quota.
- IBM discussion: an accumulating owner can benefit from lower prices when investee repurchases retire more shares. That arithmetic depends on durable intrinsic value; it is not proof IBM’s competitive thesis was correct.
- Opening developments: Combs and Weschler would each manage a few billion dollars in 2012. Label their decisions separately.
- Energy Future: bond loss arose from wrong gain/loss probabilities and gas-price-dependent economics; yield and a debt label did not establish safety.
- Opening developments / Investments: Lubrizol operating record, Bank of America preferred/warrants, and crisis investments called by issuers require separate security-level reasoning.

## Verbatim evidence anchors

- **Berkshire Hathaway Inc.; Purchase of Lubrizol** “On September 16th we acquired Lubrizol, a worldwide producer of additives and other specialty chemicals. The company has had an outstanding record since James Hambrick became CEO in 2004”
- **Berkshire Hathaway Inc.; Purchase of Energy Future Holdings bonds** “A few years back, I spent about $2 billion buying several bond issues of Energy Future Holdings, an electric utility operation serving portions of Texas. That was a mistake – a big mistake.”
- **Berkshire Hathaway Inc.; Delegation of portfolio management to Todd Combs and Ted Weschler** “Both of these men have outstanding investment skills and a deep commitment to Berkshire. Each will be handling a few billion dollars in 2012, but they have the brains, judgment and character to manage our entire portfolio”
- **Share Repurchases; Implementation of share buyback program** “Last September, we announced that Berkshire would repurchase its shares at a price of up to 110% of book value. We were in the market for only a few days – buying $67 million of stock”

## Attribution and point-in-time boundary

Buffett is the letter author; Todd Combs and Ted Weschler manage delegated portfolios. Do not assign their undisclosed individual stock choices to Buffett. Historical narratives are available only from this information date. A section discussing earlier purchases is retrospective; do not load its later performance, confession or corrected valuation into the original entry replay.
