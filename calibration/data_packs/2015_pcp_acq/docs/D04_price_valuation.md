A controlling buyer must value the operating enterprise and deduct net debt before deriving common-equity value. Final offer terms are not public by this cutoff in the recovered record. Exact August 7 close and a reliably dated trading multiple are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered here. A historical reputation for high margins cannot supply a missing maintenance-capex estimate or justify an arbitrary multiple. [Source above.]

Technical context: qualified industrial production can have a moat based on process expertise, certification and reliability rather than a consumer brand. Long program lives support repeat business but customer concentration can transfer bargaining power to the aircraft or engine maker. Backlog is not distributable cash and can move with production schedules. Test inventory, material costs, maintenance investment and acquisition accounting when translating reported profit into owner earnings. A whole-business price includes common equity and assumed net obligations; using enterprise value with after-interest earnings mismatches numerator and denominator. Normalize across relevant end markets instead of treating one strong period as permanent. [Source above; analytical framework.]

Valuation discipline: use a consistent claim and denominator. Enterprise value should be compared with cash generation before financing; common value should be compared with cash available after senior claims. Explicitly distinguish sustainable earnings from annualizing one quarter. A current quote from a different date, an eventual transaction price and an investor's later disclosed average cost are not substitutes for the cutoff price. State a range of operating assumptions, compare the result with liquid alternatives and require a margin of safety that survives a less favorable case. When a current input is unavailable, leave the numerical valuation incomplete instead of embedding an untraceable estimate.

Evidence boundary: missing figures are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered for this digest. Analytical questions are not additional reported facts or claims about the investor's private thinking. Read the financial, risk and fund-context documents together; no individual accounting ratio supplies a complete investment conclusion.
