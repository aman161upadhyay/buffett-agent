Portfolio-regime alternatives are represented by the Treasury and broad-market earnings yield rather than three corporate EV multiples. A 17.39x index multiple is neither a multiple on partnership income nor evidence that all individual companies are equally valued. HY OAS and modern VIX series do not cover 1969; a later observation is not backfilled. Shares outstanding, market capitalization and net-debt EV are not meaningful fund-level substitutes for the underlying holdings' values.

## Cutoff evidence — reconstructed

The table supplies an April index average and earnings multiple, not an exact May 29 close. The other valuation evidence is the scarcity of suitable individual situations at the required dollar scale. That is not equivalent to a price target on the Dow. Small statistically cheap companies cannot supply adequate expected dollars of profit if they cannot absorb a significant commitment. Moving to more speculative securities would require a different valuation capability. A portfolio-level decision should distinguish discounts, forced activity and after-tax alternatives.

## Economic reading of the supplied facts

Portfolio economics: aggregate stock ownership is a claim on business earnings, not a claim on the path of a market index. Dividends distribute part of those earnings; retained earnings add value only if employed productively. Interest rates affect the present value of both equities and bonds. An investor who sells one financial asset must still decide what to hold instead. Cash avoids duration and business risk but is exposed to inflation and the opportunity cost of missed investments. Market averages can obscure large differences among individual businesses. Comparing aggregate prices with book value or profits is a valuation exercise, not a mechanism for forecasting next month's quotation. The financial comparisons below are conditional on earnings durability and the stated measurement dates.

Valuation interpretation: the relevant asset is the stream of cash that can be withdrawn over the life of the business without weakening its competitive position. A low multiple is informative only when the earnings denominator is sustainable. A high return on tangible capital can justify a premium to book value, but does not justify an unlimited purchase price.

No closing quotation is inferred from a subsequent transaction average or from the low for a later month. If a dated market price is unavailable, a precise market capitalization, initial earnings yield and implied return are unavailable as well. A negotiated security can instead be evaluated from its proposed cash payments and priority, subject to credit risk. The adverse case should reduce sustainable earnings and allow for additional capital needs rather than simply apply a smaller multiple to optimistic earnings. Interest rates, taxes and reinvestment determine the opportunity cost; no unsupported discount rate or terminal growth estimate is presented as an observed fact.
