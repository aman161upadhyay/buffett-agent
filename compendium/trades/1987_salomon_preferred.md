## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**September 1987:** Salomon arranged Berkshire's preferred investment while repurchasing Minorco's block. Contemporary press reported a $700 million investment representing approximately 12% on conversion. Original preferred terms are corroborated by the following annual letter. No 1991 misconduct knowledge may enter this snapshot, and the stock-market crash had not yet happened. Exact initial purchase/settlement day is unrecovered. [1,4]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
“We, of course, have no special insights regarding the direction or future profitability of investment banking.” **Post-purchase**, February 29, 1988. [1]

“What we do have a strong feeling about is the ability and integrity of John Gutfreund, CEO of Salomon Inc.” Same letter. This original confidence must be preserved alongside the later crisis, not rewritten after the fact. [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Bought $700 million of 9% preferred in 1987, convertible after three years at $38/share. If not converted, redemption was scheduled ratably over five years beginning October 31, 1995. At year-end 1987 management valued it at 98% of par, a $14 million markdown. The 1991 scandal later required Buffett's direct involvement rather than remaining a passive fixed-income holder. [1–2]

## What happened after
The 1995 postmortem says the conversion-option judgment was “very poor” and management time consumed vastly exceeded the investment's economic significance. By 1997 Salomon's merger into Travelers had improved the outcome substantially, with Buffett praising Deryck Maughan and Bob Denham. He still said final results could not yet be tallied. `win` reflects the later recovery, not a claim that the original underwriting or trust judgment was unambiguously sound. [2–3]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Income/redemption:** financing terms, not a plain common-stock moat pitch. [1]
- **Option:** a valuable kicker depended on acceptable long-term investment-banking returns. [1]
- **People:** Gutfreund's perceived character was an explicit entry reason. [1]
- **Uncertainty:** preferred structure reflected lack of forecasting skill in this industry. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: a structured investment outside his strongest operating-forecast competence, subsequently demanding crisis management. It exposes the limits of relying on admired management and seniority. [1–3]

## Public discussion
The September 28, 1987 New York Times report is contemporary public deal evidence. Later scandal coverage cannot be represented as something the market knew in 1987. Original offering agreement and complete 1991 regulatory record remain gaps. [4]
