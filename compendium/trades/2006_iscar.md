## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**2005 approach / 2006 purchase:** Eitan Wertheimer sought an ownership solution for the family business. Buffett had purchased foreign stocks but needed tax/jurisdictional education before acquiring a foreign-based company. [1]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
“The quality of the company and the character of its management” stood out in Eitan's letter. **February 2007 explanation:** direct meetings reinforced confidence that the team would remain energetic after selling. [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Met management in November 2005 and acquired 80% July 5, 2006 for $4 billion; the family retained 20%. [1]

## What happened after
The 2009 letter reports profits despite a sharp downturn and integration work. This is operating evidence, not a realized IRR. [2]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Managers:** Wertheimer, Jacob Harpaz and Danny Goldman were central. [1–2]
- **Product:** Small consumable tools within costly production systems. [1]
- **Alignment:** Continuing family ownership and operating continuity. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: international expansion of negotiated quality-business ownership, not abandoning familiarity with business economics. [1]
