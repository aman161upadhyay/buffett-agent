## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**May 29, 1969:** Buffett reported increasingly scarce opportunities for quantitatively oriented analysis, a $100 million asset base requiring commitments around $3 million to matter, and a performance-chasing, more speculative market. Personal constraints were central: the earlier attempt to reduce objectives had not reduced effort. He also admitted poor workout results and faulted his own assessment of changing government action. [1]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
“The only way to slow down is to stop.” [1]

“I have no desire to grope around, hoping to ‘get lucky’ with other people's money.” [1]

“I am not attuned to this market environment and I don't want to spoil a decent record by trying to play a game I don't understand just so I can go out a hero.” All **contemporary May 29, 1969** statements, not a later market-crash prophecy. [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Buffett announced the intention to retire from partnership management and liquidate before year-end. He planned cash or marketable-security options and continued proportional ownership options for Berkshire and DRC. The October letter recommended Bill Ruane as an alternative manager. The December 26 letter scheduled an initial cash payment of approximately 64% of January 1, 1969 capital, adjusted for distributions, dated January 5, 1970. Residual holdings and workout cleanup prevented instantaneous total liquidation. [1]

## What happened after
This is a **decision not to force new investments**, not a short trade. `flat` denotes no attributable traded-security P&L; it does not grade the subsequent market or the partnership's full-year performance. Continuing ownership of attractive controlled businesses shows that closing the partnership was not a decision to sell every business on certainty of a crash. [1]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Opportunity scarcity:** named quantitative-investment approach no longer offered enough ideas. [1]
- **Capacity:** a small bargain could not materially affect the enlarged partnership. [1]
- **Competence:** Buffett rejected changing games merely to preserve a performance record. [1]
- **Personal utility:** competitive responsibility prevented the reduced-effort arrangement he wanted. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: discipline can require returning capital rather than maximizing assets under management. The personal factor prevents reducing this entirely to a valuation timing rule. [1]

## Public discussion
The source is a private partner communication subsequently published, not a contemporary public market call. Buffett's direct admission of workout errors supplies contrary evidence to any claim that he exited solely at the peak with perfect foresight. [1]
