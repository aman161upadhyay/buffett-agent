Date: 2025-06-30

January 16: the company released full-year results and reaffirmed its original annual outlook. April 17: it reported the first quarter and reduced guidance, citing Medicare Advantage utilization and Optum Health reimbursement. May 13: it announced a CEO transition and suspended the outlook; the SEC exhibit became public May 14. May 14: it disputed the characterization of a supposed criminal investigation in the press. June 30: the closing stock-price observation is $311.97. [1-4; historical price below]

The chronology does not include second-quarter results or any new annual EPS estimate. No investor's purchase is announced in these issuer documents. The analyst has evidence of a large existing franchise and evidence that the earnings model needs repair; neither can be erased to make the case easier. The outstanding questions concern cost stabilization, credible pricing and the scope of regulatory exposure, rather than an assumed date when market sentiment must reverse. The first trading day of an outside investor's accumulation cannot be inferred from this sequence.

Evidence ledger, with dollar amounts in billions unless stated otherwise: the January 16 release reports 2024 revenue of $400.3, versus $371.6 in 2023, operating earnings of $32.3, operating cash flow of $24.2, GAAP EPS of $15.51 and adjusted EPS of $27.66. The April 17 release reports first-quarter revenue of $109.6, up $9.8, GAAP EPS of $6.85 and adjusted EPS of $7.20. UnitedHealthcare added 780,000 consumers year to date. April's reduced adjusted annual EPS range was $26.00-$26.50; the May leadership announcement suspended the outlook, so that range is no longer current guidance. The May statement disputes the press characterization of a supposed criminal investigation. These are issuer statements, not independent legal findings. [1-4]

Timing discipline: the chronology separates publication dates from the economic periods discussed. A result for an earlier quarter only becomes an input when released. The market close is an observation available on the stated assessment date, not evidence of a particular investor's execution. This timeline includes no presumed private conversations or unannounced agreement. There is no catalyst completion forecast here: the analyst must decide whether the available business evidence is sufficient without relying on a future disclosure to reveal someone else's decision.
