# Numbers desk B5 enrichment

## Scope and information boundary

Updated D02, D03, D04 and D06 in the fourteen assigned packs below (56 documents). D01/D05/D07/D08, answer keys, prompts, manifests and agent instructions were not edited. Metadata and aliases were left unchanged: additions introduce numerical disclosures and independent peers, not new target-identifying subsidiary or product names.

This is an evidence enrichment, not a claim that every requested field was recoverable. Reported observations, desk arithmetic, stale quotations, management guidance and missing inputs are distinguished in the documents. Public-stock metrics are not fabricated for a private segment, partnership or market stance. Banks use capital, credit, book and tangible-return measures rather than industrial enterprise value or capex-based owner earnings.

Daily market data stop at each cutoff. Completed-month valuation observations do not use a month ending after the cutoff. Japan uses completed July prices, not August 31; the 2024 cash pack preserves its explicit pre-disclosure Q2 boundary. Holdout additions are financial statements, operating comparisons and market data only; no later investor action, transaction outcome, recommendation or rationale was added.

Some historical tables are retrieved from later-hosted reports or compilations. Only the identified earlier reported financial periods are used; the documents explicitly distinguish those reproductions from contemporaneous publication and exclude later results. This matters particularly for the FY2002 Fortune peer table and historical Japanese financial tables.

- **1969/1974:** Original partnership correspondence and dated interview, historical price/P-E/dividend tables and FRED yields. Existing three-source checks for missing current interim/market fields are retained. No hedge exposure is inferred from an action or later explanation.
- **GEICO:** Primary 1975 annual report, 1974 filing, Lu chapter 9, plus newly recovered June 11, 1976 New York Times operating/OTC observations. - **PetroChina/BNSF:** Original SEC/issuer annual and interim financial tables, historical local/share price archives, dated FT peers, historical Fortune financials and government/index series. Reporting-scope and FX limitations are stated where they affect comparability.
- **Japan:** Itochu financial section and August release; Marubeni historical financial-data table and May/August releases; Mitsubishi May 2017/2018/2020 and August 2020 releases; Mitsui 2019 report and May/July 2020 releases; Sumitomo historical fact book and June-quarter release. Digrin prices use the dividend-unadjusted, split-restated column with explicit restoration factors. No post-August-30 position announcement was used.
- **WFC/ATVI/BAC:** Existing issuer/SEC/Yahoo/FRED/Multpl tables retained and reconciled. The documents name Yahoo estimates, Macrotrends and StockAnalysis archival attempts for missing consensus. ATVI exact-close attempts additionally name Yahoo history, WSJ and Nasdaq; no present-day consensus is backdated.
- **Holdouts:** Pre-cutoff Oxy annual reports and August quarter release/call, three actual July/August peer releases, dated September prices and historical market series; Berkshire annual financial statements and the earlier nine-month statement for Q4 subtraction. Financial figures only were added. Oxy's $8.725bn property figure is gross chemical PP&E, NOT total assets or net invested capital. Berkshire 2021–2022 NI/EPS uses the 2023 filing's restated insurance-accounting comparatives.

Remaining missing fields are genuine limits of this enrichment, not permission to impute zeros, current database estimates, later prices, investor purchase costs or post-cutoff outcomes. Source attempts and the narrower recoverable substitute are identified rather than asserting a fully populated synchronized valuation grid.
