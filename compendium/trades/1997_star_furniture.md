## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**1997:** Star was one of the furniture retailers the Blumkins and later Bill Child recommended. An admired business was not automatically available for sale. [1]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
“I ... looked at Star's financials, and liked what I saw.” Buffett jointly described Star/Dairy Queen as understandable, possessing excellent economics and run by outstanding people. **February 1998 retrospective.** [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Bob Denham introduced the opportunity before the annual meeting. Melvyn Wolff attended, confirming his preference for Berkshire; a few days later he and Buffett met in New York and agreed in two hours. Exact closing date/price unknown. [1]

## What happened after
The letter says Wolff and sister Shirley Toomim shared sale proceeds with coworkers. Lifetime return not established. [1]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Network:** Existing furniture operators supplied informed recommendations. [1]
- **Economics:** Buffett checked the financials. [1]
- **Fit:** Mutual confidence made detailed employment/lease negotiations unnecessary in his account. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: domain expansion through trusted operating managers rather than an abstract strategic diversification plan. [1]
