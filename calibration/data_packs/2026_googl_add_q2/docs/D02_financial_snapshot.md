Title: A large earnings base with investment gains and central costs
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000012/googexhibit991q42025.htm ; https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/department-justice-wins-significant-remedies-against-google ; https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/LSB/PDF/LSB11362/LSB11362.1.pdf
Date: 2026-03-31

Annual operating income of $129.039 billion rises from $112.390 billion, while net income reaches $132.170 billion. Other income of $29.787 billion materially contributes to that net result and should not be capitalized as if it were recurring advertising profit. The operating record is strong, but operating earnings, net income and cash distributable after infrastructure spending are not identical. [1]

Fourth-quarter Services and Cloud profit sum to more than consolidated operating earnings because Other Bets and shared activities consume substantial resources. Waymo's $2.1 billion employee compensation charge contributes to Other Bets' loss. Shared AI research is included in central activities, so a segment margin does not capture all development cost. Cash plus marketable securities of $126.843 billion provides flexibility but is less than the high end of planned annual capital expenditure. A long-run owner needs a normalized return on that incremental investment, not simply confidence that the company can finance it. [1]

Evidence ledger: February 4 results show 2025 revenue of $402.836 billion versus $350.018 billion in 2024; operating income $129.039 billion versus $112.390 billion; net income $132.170 billion; diluted EPS $10.81. Fourth-quarter revenue was $113.828 billion, operating income $35.934 billion and EPS $2.82. Services revenue was $95.862 billion and Cloud $17.664 billion, up 48%; segment operating income was $40.132 billion and $5.313 billion respectively. Other Bets lost $3.617 billion, including a Waymo compensation charge, and central activities cost $5.894 billion. Cash/marketable securities totaled $126.843 billion. Management expects 2026 capital expenditures of $175-$185 billion. These plans are forward-looking; no second-quarter capital commitment by an outside investor is included. [1-3]

Accounting discipline: all dollar amounts in this document retain the period and basis given in the evidence ledger. Revenue is not owner earnings; neither adjusted profit nor operating cash flow alone measures distributable cash. Maintenance investment, working capital, compensation dilution and financing claims must be considered separately. A quarter is not a full economic cycle. Where a stand-alone balance sheet, maintenance-capital split or historical consensus series is absent, it is unavailable rather than assumed to be zero or inferred from a later filing.
