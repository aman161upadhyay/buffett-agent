# 1993 Berkshire annual letter

**Information date:** 1994-03 (month-only where precise public release day was not independently established). **Era:** B. **Author:** Buffett. **Primary source:** [letter](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1993.html). All rules below cite sections of this source; section names are used rather than fabricated PDF page numbers. Annual-period labels are not publication dates.

## Process rules and decision explanations

- Dexter Shoe: favorable contemporaneous account emphasizes management and an apparently durable low-cost domestic manufacturing position. Do not replace that actual entry thesis with the later disaster.
- Same section and financing: Berkshire shares were issued to acquire Dexter. Shares surrendered carry an economic opportunity cost, even if no cash leaves the company.
- Look-through earnings: distinguish investees’ retained earnings from cash dividends reported at Berkshire; evaluate capital allocation at each issuer.
- Capital Cities/ABC: partial sale is a real counterexample to a blanket never-sell-public-shares rule.
- Corporate Governance: director independence and practical ability to challenge a CEO matter; paper board structure is not sufficient evidence of oversight.

## Verbatim evidence anchors

- **Dexter Shoe; Purchase of Dexter Shoe** “So we promptly jumped at the chance last year to acquire Dexter Shoe of Dexter, Maine, which manufactures popular-priced men's and women's shoes.”
- **Dexter Shoe; Dexter shareholders' decision to exchange stock for Berkshire shares** “we believe that Harold and Peter (who were not interested in cash) made a sound decision in exchanging their Dexter stock for shares of Berkshire.”
- **Look-Through Earnings; Sale of Capital Cities/ABC shares** “For example, in 1986 we bought three million shares of Capital Cities/ABC for $172.50 per share and late last year sold one-third of that holding for $630 per share.”
- **Chairman's Letter - 1993; Stock issuance following redemption of convertible debentures** “In a transaction described in last year's Annual Report, we issued 3,944 shares in early January, 1993 upon the conversion of $46 million convertible debentures that we had called for redemption.”

## Attribution and point-in-time boundary

Buffett is the source author, but an acquired subsidiary’s bolt-on or operating action is not automatically his personally originated investment. Individual decider unknown where not identified. Historical narratives are available only from this information date. A section discussing earlier purchases is retrospective; do not load its later performance, confession or corrected valuation into the original entry replay.

## Coverage limitation

This is a decision-process extraction, not a full transcription or a complete portfolio ledger. Numbers outside the source passage are not estimated.
