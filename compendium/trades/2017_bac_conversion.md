# Bank of America — warrant exercise

Research cutoff: **2026-10-25**. Month-format dates are documented windows, not invented execution days. Unknown means an evidence gap. Where return is unknown, outcome class `flat` is an **unscored schema sentinel, not a claim of zero return**.

## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: 2017-06-29.** Berkshire held $5B preferred paying 6% and warrants on 700M common shares. BofA had announced a planned $0.12 quarterly common dividend on June 28. [1]

**Snapshot gaps:** Entry-day audited valuation, trading range and independently documented consensus: unknown; do not substitute the first 13F mark for purchase cost. Financial/consensus gaps are not filled with later outcomes.

## Buffett's thesis in his own words

The June 30 release states Berkshire would exercise when the dividend increased and use its preferred stock as consideration. Arithmetic: 700M × $0.48 = $336M annual common dividends, versus $300M preferred income. This calculation explains the timing without asserting a new $5B cash purchase. [1,2]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did

Intent disclosed June 30; exercise completed August 29 according to bank/CNBC reporting. The [2011 preferred case](2011_bac_preferred.md) contains original underwriting. Later adds and trims are separate common-stock allocation decisions. [1,2]
