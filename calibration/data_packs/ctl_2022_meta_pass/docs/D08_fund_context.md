Date: 2022-11-04

The latest available half-year report, published in August, places Berkshire cash and Treasury bills at approximately $105.4 billion; that is the metadata benchmark rather than a November 4 cash observation. The third-quarter report is not yet published at this cutoff. Apple, Bank of America, Coca-Cola, American Express and energy positions already concentrate the equity portfolio. Combs and Weschler have separate mandates, but a multi-billion-dollar founder-controlled advertising idea still needs explicit manager and sizing attribution. [S3,S4]

### Dated operating baseline

Third-quarter revenue was $27.714 billion, down 4%; operating income was $5.664 billion, down 46%; net income was $4.395 billion, down 52%; diluted EPS was $1.64, down 49%. Operating margin fell from 36% to 20%. Family monthly active people were 3.71 billion, up 4%, and daily active people 2.93 billion, up 4%. Facebook alone had 2.96 billion monthly active users. Ad impressions grew 17% while average price per ad fell 18%. Quarterly capital expenditure including finance-lease principal was $9.52 billion. Cash and marketable securities totaled $41.78 billion and long-term debt $9.92 billion. Buybacks consumed $6.55 billion. Headcount reached 87,314, up 28%. Management projected full-year capital expenditure of $32–33 billion and next-year capital expenditure of $34–39 billion; projected next-year expenses were $96–101 billion, including office-consolidation costs. These are forecasts, not completed investments or realized savings. [S1]

### Underwriting distinction

The denominator is capital available to Berkshire, not the issuer's enterprise value or an outside asset manager's client AUM. Insurance float carries obligations; reported cash is not all surplus. A new security should be compared with existing portfolio opportunities, retaining liquidity for operating subsidiaries and negotiated whole-business purchases. No leverage at the parent is assumed to make the proposal fit. The question is an incremental allocation within a large permanent-capital enterprise. [Analytical interpretation of S3]

For a public stock, a deputy-scale position and a Buffett-scale position need different sizing even when both are plausible investments. For a negotiated financing, the relevant cash commitment includes follow-on needs rather than only the opening check. No future trade, retrospective explanation or confidential public-equity holding is supplied as a recommendation. The issuer's current operating scale below helps compare the proposed exposure with the available fund capital. [Analytical interpretation of S1,S3]
