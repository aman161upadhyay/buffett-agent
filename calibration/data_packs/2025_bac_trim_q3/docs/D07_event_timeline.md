Title: The last released quarter is March, not June
Source: https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_70c8fb58fd3bea7a80c281a02cf2c22d/bankofamerica/db/806/10171/earnings_release/The+Press+Release_1Q25_ADA.pdf
Date: 2025-06-30

April 15: Bank of America reports March-quarter results, including $0.90 diluted EPS, 11.8% standardized CET1 and $36.39 book value per common share. May 15: the last publicly available Berkshire 13F reports March holdings; those are a dated exposure checkpoint, not a live trading ledger. June 30: the bank's stock closes at $47.32. No unreleased June-quarter result is treated as public information. [1; fund source in D08; historical price below]

The original franchise can be reassessed against stronger recent net interest income and continued shareholder returns, while the analyst separately assesses price, concentration, tax and balance-sheet uncertainty. Changes in ownership that are not yet publicly disclosed do not belong in this chronology. Neither a future annual meeting comment nor a later filing is used to assign a security-specific reason. The sequence permits a current economic judgment but does not identify the precise day, tax lot or ultimate quantity of any potential transaction. A mark is not a receipt.

Evidence ledger from the April 15 release: first-quarter net income was $7.4 billion, versus $6.7 billion a year earlier; diluted EPS was $0.90, versus $0.76. Revenue net of interest expense was $27.4 billion, or $27.5 billion fully tax equivalent. Net interest income was $14.4 billion, or $14.6 billion FTE, up 3%. Noninterest expense was $17.8 billion and credit provision $1.5 billion; net charge-offs of $1.5 billion were flat versus both comparison quarters. Ending deposits were approximately $2 trillion. Standardized CET1 was 11.8%, backed by $201 billion common equity tier 1 capital. Book value per common share was $36.39. Capital returned totaled $6.5 billion, comprising $2.0 billion common dividends and $4.5 billion repurchases. [1]

Timing discipline: the chronology separates publication dates from the economic periods discussed. A result for an earlier quarter only becomes an input when released. The market close is an observation available on the stated assessment date, not evidence of a particular investor's execution. This timeline includes no presumed private conversations or unannounced agreement. There is no catalyst completion forecast here: the analyst must decide whether the available business evidence is sufficient without relying on a future disclosure to reveal someone else's decision.
