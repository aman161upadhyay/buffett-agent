Dealer operating cash flow moves with trading inventory and financing; subtracting office capex does not identify cash safely available to a perpetual preferred holder. The record of high margins therefore requires a separate funding and asset-mark analysis.

The fiscal third-quarter release, for the period ended August 29, reports $6.04 billion net revenue and $845 million net earnings. Diluted EPS was $1.81, compared with $6.13 in the prior-year quarter. Investment-banking revenue of $1.29 billion was down 40% from the prior-year period. These figures show a still-profitable institution under severe earnings pressure, not an unchanged pre-crisis earnings stream. [Source above.]

Technical context: a financial institution's earning assets are supported by deposits, wholesale funding, creditors and equity with different seniority. Regulatory capital cannot all be distributed to investors. Analyze credit quality, liquidity and capital separately: a solvent institution can still face a funding run, and liquid assets do not prove all credit losses are recognized. For a preferred claim, identify whether dividends are cumulative, the issuer's redemption rights and the creditor cushion ahead of the security. For common, normalize provisioning and assess return on tangible equity rather than using industrial free-cash-flow conventions. Reported book value, tangible common capital and market capitalization are different denominators. [Source above; analytical framework rather than additional issuer measurements.]

Accounting discipline: the figures stated above are reported observations unless expressly labeled arithmetic. A period-end balance is not a flow and a market mark is not purchase cost. Do not mix fiscal years, currencies, share bases or enterprise and equity values. To construct owner earnings, begin with the relevant reported profit, reconcile noncash items and subtract the investment required to maintain economic position. Changes in working capital and recurring compensation remain economic costs even when a convenient adjusted metric excludes them. If maintenance investment, leverage or the proper share denominator is missing, state that the yield cannot be completed rather than present false precision.

Evidence boundary: missing figures are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered for this digest. Analytical questions are not additional reported facts or claims about the investor's private thinking. Read the financial, risk and fund-context documents together; no individual accounting ratio supplies a complete investment conclusion.
