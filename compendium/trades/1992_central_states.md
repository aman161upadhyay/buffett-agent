## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**December 1992:** Central States insured credit-card payments when borrowers became disabled/unemployed. Published later premium/profit figures are not a recovered entry-date filing. [1]

## Buffett's thesis in his own words
“A prototype of what we now look for.” “We could not be associated with better people.” **March 1993 explanation.** [1]

## What Berkshire Hathaway did
Acquired 82% in December, leaving 18% with the Kizer family, which continued operating the company. Buffett had known Bill Kizer for over 35 years. [1]

## What happened after
No standalone purchase return established. The letter compares the transaction with Berkshire's earlier National Indemnity purchase, not with speculative insurance-stock trading. [1]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Trust:** Long-established relationship. [1]
- **Alignment:** Family retained actual ownership. [1]
- **Business:** Identifiable profitable insurance niche. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: provide a permanent home while retaining owner-operators, rather than impose a turnaround on an unknown management team. [1]
