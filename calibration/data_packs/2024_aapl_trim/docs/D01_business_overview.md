Title: Business economics and ownership claim
Source: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2024/05/apple-reports-second-quarter-results/ (published 2024-05-02); https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2023ltr.pdf (2024-02-24 public prior holding)
Date: 2024-05-04

Apple's consumer-device and services ecosystem supports repeat purchases, recurring software and service spending, and a large installed base. The original Berkshire thesis concerned a consumer relationship rather than a short-term product forecast. The current holding review must separate intact customer economics from position size, tax consequences and the price attached to future cash flows. [Source above.]

Technical context: a consumer ecosystem may monetize both a device and recurring services, but they are linked rather than independent annuities. Customer retention, replacement frequency, pricing and the required cost of innovation determine owner economics. Separate hardware sales, installed-base activity and services revenue, avoiding double-counting value from the same customer relationship. Cash returned through buybacks must be compared with share issuance and purchase price; authorization is not execution. Foreign-held cash, taxes and operating liquidity needs affect deployable excess capital. Use one split basis for price, shares and EPS, since mismatched adjustments can make an otherwise correct earnings multiple meaningless. [Source above; analytical framework.]

Underwriting questions: identify the actual paying customer, repeat-purchase or replacement mechanism, and what prevents a capable competitor from taking the profit. Distinguish an enduring economic advantage from an asset that is merely expensive to build. The ownership claim being evaluated must match the economics described: operating control, common shares and a preferred claim confer different rights. Do not infer honest or capable management solely from a famous name or a long corporate history. Where direct customer-retention or pricing evidence is absent, leave that criterion uncertain rather than supply a generic franchise label. Record what observable deterioration would invalidate the business thesis before considering position size.

Evidence boundary: missing figures are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered for this digest. Analytical questions are not additional reported facts or claims about the investor's private thinking. Read the financial, risk and fund-context documents together; no individual accounting ratio supplies a complete investment conclusion.
