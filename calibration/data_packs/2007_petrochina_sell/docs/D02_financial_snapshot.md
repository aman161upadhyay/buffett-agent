The 2004 bridge comes from the original March 2005 IFRS release linked above. Its DDA is RMB46,411m, operating margin 37.7% and average-equity ROE approximately 26.3%. That release uses 2003 OCF RMB137,236m and capex RMB82,929m: its comparative scope differs from the earlier reported 2003 row, so these must not be interpreted as a uniform cash-growth series. FY2004 dividends attributable to the year total RMB.263430 per share. End-2006 shares are 179,020.978 million, not 2005's weighted-average 176,770 million. FY2006 total equity including minorities is RMB617,591 million; common-book valuation uses RMB586,677 million. FY2006 dividends total approximately RMB.357505 per share. Net debt rises from net cash RMB7,646 million in 2005 to RMB22,838 million in 2006 despite record net income.

The March results announcement reports 2006 net profit of RMB142.2 billion, an increase of 6.6% year on year. This is substantially above the RMB46.910 billion annual profit reported for 2002. The earnings record therefore reflects a very different commodity environment from the original investment period. A business appraisal must adjust both its historical earnings baseline and its required reserve-replacement investment. [Source above.]

Technical context: petroleum accounting profits depend on realized prices, volumes, royalties, taxes and the treatment of exploration and development costs. A reserve is not cash in the bank: it needs extraction, transport and often replacement investment. Distinguish upstream earnings from refining and chemical margins rather than assuming they move together. For owner earnings, separate maintenance needed to sustain economic production from expansion capital. Check whether reported free cash flow deducts preferred distributions and whether acquisitions are being called capital expenditure. Proved reserves, production and reserve life use different units and assumptions. A conservative analysis should explicitly test a lower realized-price case without claiming to forecast the commodity. Accounting discipline: the figures stated above are reported observations unless expressly labeled arithmetic. A period-end balance is not a flow and a market mark is not purchase cost. Do not mix fiscal years, currencies, share bases or enterprise and equity values. To construct owner earnings, begin with the relevant reported profit, reconcile noncash items and subtract the investment required to maintain economic position. Changes in working capital and recurring compensation remain economic costs even when a convenient adjusted metric excludes them. If maintenance investment, leverage or the proper share denominator is missing, state that the yield cannot be completed rather than present false precision.

Evidence boundary: missing figures are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered for this digest. Analytical questions are not additional reported facts or claims about the investor's private thinking. Read the financial, risk and fund-context documents together; no individual accounting ratio supplies a complete investment conclusion.
