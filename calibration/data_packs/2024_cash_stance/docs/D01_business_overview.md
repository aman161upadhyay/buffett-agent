Title: Business economics and ownership claim
Source: https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2023ltr.pdf (published 2024-02-24); https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/qtrly/2ndqtr24.pdf (2024-08-03)
Date: 2024-11-02

This is an allocation between productive businesses, marketable equities, own-share repurchases and liquid government instruments, not a forecast of the next stock-market decline. Berkshire owns insurance and many controlled operating companies, so liquidity supports both opportunistic investment and obligations. A market-stance answer must distinguish holding deployable capital from selling every productive asset or abandoning long-term equity ownership. [Source above.]

Technical context: cash, bills, long bonds and productive assets serve different purposes. Short government instruments preserve nominal liquidity but expose holders to reinvestment rates and purchasing-power erosion. A long bond fixes nominal payments while adding duration risk. A public equity is a residual ownership claim whose value depends on sustainable distributable cash, not tomorrow's quote. A controlled operating business adds capital-allocation rights and illiquidity. Compare after-tax expected economics on consistent horizons, retaining enough liquidity for obligations before allocating discretionary capital. The size of a cash balance alone does not identify a market forecast or explain every trade. [Source above; comparative investment framework, not a new macroeconomic estimate.]

Underwriting questions: identify the actual paying customer, repeat-purchase or replacement mechanism, and what prevents a capable competitor from taking the profit. Distinguish an enduring economic advantage from an asset that is merely expensive to build. The ownership claim being evaluated must match the economics described: operating control, common shares and a preferred claim confer different rights. Do not infer honest or capable management solely from a famous name or a long corporate history. Where direct customer-retention or pricing evidence is absent, leave that criterion uncertain rather than supply a generic franchise label. Record what observable deterioration would invalidate the business thesis before considering position size.
