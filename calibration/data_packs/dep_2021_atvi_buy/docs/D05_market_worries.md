### Underwriting distinction

This objection is intended to be capable of defeating the affirmative case. Distinguish a reversible mark-to-market loss from permanent impairment, and a temporary earnings interruption from a permanently more capital-hungry business. The investor must survive the adverse scenario without relying on the next buyer, a friendly regulator or unrestricted external financing. A long holding period cannot restore capital that has been diluted away or consumed by obligations senior to the purchased security. [Analytical interpretation of S1]

Observable thesis-breakers include customer economics deteriorating despite continued spending, new funding coming only on punitive terms, or reported cash failing to reconcile to recurring obligations. These are analytical monitoring conditions, not claims that such outcomes have occurred. The record below supplies the current scale of exposure against which to test them. No later failure, recovery or management admission is used to strengthen the bear case. [Analytical interpretation of S1]
