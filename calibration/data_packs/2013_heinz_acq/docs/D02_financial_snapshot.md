The implied share count is an approximation from rounded continuing EPS, not period-end shares. FY2010 total common net income was $864.9m, below continuing income because of a $49.6m discontinued-operation loss. FY2012 contains productivity charges; neither those charges nor all capital expenditure should automatically be excluded from owner earnings.

Fiscal 2012 sales were approximately $11.6 billion. The November fiscal second-quarter release reports $2.83 billion sales, up 0.5% as reported. Pricing contributed 1.9%, volume 1.4%, foreign currency reduced sales 2.4%, and divestitures reduced sales 0.4%. This bridge matters: the 3.3% organic growth rate is not the same as dollar revenue growth and does not by itself measure changes in consumer demand. [Source above.]

Technical context: branded food can generate recurring consumer demand, yet retailers control shelf space and private labels create alternatives. Evaluate price, volume and mix independently; a price increase with collapsing units is not unambiguous pricing power. Commodity inflation, foreign exchange, promotion and working-capital seasonality affect cash conversion. A control purchase can use several layers of debt, preferred and common, each with different return rights. The same enterprise can offer an attractive preferred coupon while leaving common owners exposed to leverage. Operating cost reduction should be evaluated alongside brand investment and customer retention, not counted as recurring growth automatically. [Source above; analytical framework.]

Accounting discipline: the figures stated above are reported observations unless expressly labeled arithmetic. A period-end balance is not a flow and a market mark is not purchase cost. Do not mix fiscal years, currencies, share bases or enterprise and equity values. To construct owner earnings, begin with the relevant reported profit, reconcile noncash items and subtract the investment required to maintain economic position. Changes in working capital and recurring compensation remain economic costs even when a convenient adjusted metric excludes them. If maintenance investment, leverage or the proper share denominator is missing, state that the yield cannot be completed rather than present false precision.

Evidence boundary: missing figures are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered for this digest. Analytical questions are not additional reported facts or claims about the investor's private thinking. Read the financial, risk and fund-context documents together; no individual accounting ratio supplies a complete investment conclusion.
